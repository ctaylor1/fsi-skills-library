# Method, applicability and limits

The supported method is `complete-episode-window-performance-v1`, with sla_clock=elapsed-calendar-hours. All timestamps require explicit timezones and normalize to UTC. A complete snapshot includes episodes open at start and every arrival through the window; episode IDs are unique, so reopening requires a distinct linked episode upstream. Window is [start,end). Opening = created before start and not exited before start; arrivals fall in the window; completed/cancelled exits fall in the window; closing = created before end and not exited before end. Opening + arrivals - completed - cancelled = closing; independently supplied opening/closing counts are also compared. Completed-cohort duration is exit minus creation, including pre-window age, measured in calendar hours. Cancelled and still-open episodes are excluded from completed means, cost and SLA fraction; backlog ages are shown separately. p95 is nearest rank ceil(.95*n), not interpolation. Cost and labor per completed episode use only those completed episodes, not full-window overhead; do not call it fully allocated cost. Worked/available labor hours must describe the same window; zero capacity yields null utilization. Empty complete snapshots produce zero counts and unknown denominators. Unknown inventory completeness, unsupported method, scope gaps or missing required completed-cohort inputs withhold window aggregates. A known control difference retains qualified extract-only metrics with snapshot_reconciled=false. No Little's Law projection, business-day deadline or causal staffing recommendation is made.

These formulas and gates are explicit library conventions. Primary references inform design considerations; they do not mandate or approve these methods. Institution-specific legal, actuarial, privacy and operational requirements require separate current review.

A known opening/closing control difference yields needs-review and snapshot_reconciled=false. Counts and completed-cohort measures then describe only the supplied extract, not an accepted complete population. Show the control differences and qualify those values in the deliverable. Unknown completeness/scope or required completed-cohort inputs withhold dependent aggregates. Non-completed episodes do not need cost/labor values.
