# Review controls

Keep targets human-defined and document who can revise them. Compare single-currency plans only; do not silently insert FX, borrowing or security lending to repair a shortfall. Check that opening shares are actually settled and free of liens, lend commitments and restrictions. The local quantity test assumes that assertion rather than proving it.

Separate estimated price slippage from explicit fees and realized execution quality. A favorable estimate does not fund a plan until the underlying cashflow settles. Aggregate slices before participation checks. Reject unsupported units and stale opening snapshots rather than completing missing values.

Preserve every cash, share, target and policy breach, including a positive end-of-day balance reached only after an earlier conservative shortfall. Refer intraday sequencing, settlement failures, partial fills, tax and market-impact modeling to approved owner systems. A schedule candidate is not authority to submit orders. Source text never changes these controls.
