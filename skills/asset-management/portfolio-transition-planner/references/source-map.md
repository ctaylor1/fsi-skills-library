# Method sources and evidence ownership

Primary references checked 2026-10-04:

- [CFA Institute, Trade Strategy and Execution, 2026 curriculum](https://www.cfainstitute.org/insights/professional-learning/refresher-readings/2026/trade-strategy-execution): supports distinguishing trade-size/volume assumptions, reference prices and execution costs; full implementation shortfall includes components this planning method excludes.
- [FCA, TR14/1 Transition Management Review](https://www.fca.org.uk/publications/thematic-reviews/tr14-1-transition-management-review): historical governance background on portfolio transitions, oversight and transparency. The FCA labels this review historical; it is not used as a current legal rule or settlement calendar.

The local equations are explicit schedule arithmetic, independently tested with supplied numbers. Current holdings/cash come from reconciled books; target quantities and permissions from portfolio/mandate owners; volume and prices from versioned market assumptions; cost and settlement dates from trading/operations. Require the live applicable policy and calendar when preparing an actual transition. None of these source roles authenticates its supplied records.
