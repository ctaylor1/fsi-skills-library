# Primary method references and supplied evidence

Checked 2026-10-04:

- [ICMA repo FAQ 21, What is a haircut?](https://www.icmagroup.org/market-practice-and-regulatory-policy/repo-and-collateral-markets/icma-ercc-publications/frequently-asked-questions-on-repo/21-what-is-a-haircut/) distinguishes a deduction from collateral market value from an initial-margin ratio against the cash amount. The package applies the explicitly supplied agreement convention.
- [ICMA professional repo course, Repo Types and Variants](https://www.icmagroup.org/assets/Uploads/Documents/Richard%20Comotto%20-%20Repo%20Types%20and%20Variants.pdf) illustrates simple repo interest on purchase price and explicit money-market day-count conventions. This is educational background, not an operative agreement.
- [Federal Reserve Bank of New York, SOMA securities-lending FAQs](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/sec_faq) describes that particular program's lending-fee market-value basis and actual/360 convention. Program-specific margins, penalties and eligibility are not imported as universal rules.

The actual agreement and institutional owner determine the allowed convention, rate schedule, accrued basis, collateral eligibility, recall date and applicable legal treatment. Current custodian/financing records supply outstanding quantities and collateral values; treasury supplies cash and separately committed funding. Source/version hashes demonstrate reproducibility only. Obtain current jurisdiction-specific advice rather than treating these references as legal netting or enforceability evidence.
