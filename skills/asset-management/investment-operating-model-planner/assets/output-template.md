# Draft AM operating-model decision packet

Review ID / manager / funds and books / as-of and knowledge cutoff / audience / accountable owner.

## Decision and observed baseline
Describe the operating decision, scope and supplied inventory completeness. Cite the shared operations review, original observation window and population. Distinguish measurements from proposed improvements and unverified claims.

## Service and ownership design
For each service, show current and proposed owner, retained accountable owner, backup, system of record, provider, calendar/time zone, dependency and source locator. Explain the proposed sourcing option and alternatives for owner review.

## Controls and transition
Map maker/checker, tested version, evidence date and exceptions. Show dependency order, training/access evidence, rehearsal, rollback owner/runbook and human acceptance gate. Preserve blocked services and unknown rights.

## Open decisions and handoff
List each evidence gap, risk, proposed treatment and accountable decision owner. State that no staff/provider/workflow change is authorized or performed. Preserve exact native results in a separate immutable attachment.
