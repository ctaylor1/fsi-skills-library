- fund-service-provider-oversight-reviewer: recurring fund/provider obligations and exceptions; operating-model proposals do not appoint or replace the provider.

Handoff common investment-evidence packets through asset-management-router with a detached source input and native receiver validation. Never pass this output as if it were another skill's input. Record explicit field mapping, source versions, context changes and open issues; both owner and receiver remain draft-only.
