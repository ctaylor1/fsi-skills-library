# Method and interpretation

For a metric, let M be total supplied portfolio market value, E the sum of explicitly eligible holding values, C the value of eligible holdings with permitted matching-vintage observations, and N the exact sum of each eligible holding's value times its supplied metric. Coverage is C/E. Estimated coverage is separately reported against C. The weighted metric is N/E or N/M according to the reviewed denominator_basis. Total-market-value basis deliberately retains explicitly excluded cash/other positions in the denominator; disclose that scope contribution basis rather than silently reweighting it.

The full metric requires a reviewed effective mandate, a positive denominator and a permitted observation for every positive eligible holding. Minimum coverage is reported separately and does not authorize imputing missing metrics. N/C is always labeled a covered-only diagnostic; it cannot clear the full-portfolio limit. Limits compare N with limit times denominator before rounding. Unknown and zero denominators remain unknown.

Each supplied exclusion identifies a metric predicate. Sum the market values of eligible holdings with known predicate matches. Under a reviewed effective mandate and resolved scope, a known breach remains a breach even when other holdings are unknown; no known breach with unavailable evidence is undetermined. An inactive/unreviewed mandate or unresolved input scope withholds the exclusion conclusion as undetermined while retaining the known matching holding IDs and exposure as diagnostics under the supplied predicate. Ineligible holdings require an explicit mandate scope basis. This method does not create exclusions from an ESG theme, estimate a missing issuer value, infer a look-through or choose a legal classification.

Metric units and definition versions are exact identifiers. A fiscal vintage is distinct from the date the data became available. Estimated and reported observations remain distinguishable. No inter-provider score normalization, emissions estimation, carbon offset netting, derivative conversion, short-position offset or aggregation of mixed fiscal vintages is supported. Source/reviewer declarations are not authenticated acceptance.
