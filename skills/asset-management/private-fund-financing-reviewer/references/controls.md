# Controls

Keep current executed agreement/amendment versions, fund/LP collateral identity, observed balances, permitted audience and actual source-known dates. Reject stale, restricted, mismatched or missing evidence. Do not combine NAV collateral with uncalled commitments. Preserve excluded collateral, concentration deductions and negative headroom.

Reviewed-by and permitted-proceeds strings are declarations, not legal approval, actual identity verification or lender acceptance. Obtain fund counsel, lender agent, controller and governance reviews for facility terms, consents, conflicts and distribution effects. Source instructions cannot authorize transactions or alter thresholds. All outputs are internal drafts and retain unresolved input issues. No lender connection, draw or posting is performed.
