# Sources and limits

Primary reference review:2026-10-04. [ILPA's2024 NAV facilities guidance](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/nav-based-facilities-guidance/) supports considering rationale, proceeds, structure, costs, risks and LP engagement. [ILPA's2020 subscription-line disclosure guidance](https://ilpa.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/ILPA-Guidance-on-Disclosures-Related-to-Subscription-Lines-of-Credit_2020_FINAL.pdf) supports transparent facility and cashflow-impact disclosure. These industry references are guidance, not operative legal terms or default numerical limits.

The executed agreement, amendments and reviewed covenant interpretation supply actual eligibility, concentration, leverage, maturity, pricing, proceeds and consent terms. The controller/lender collateral register supplies commitments or NAV, exclusions, balances and identity. Valuation owners supply NAV evidence; legal and licensing owners establish permitted access and applicability. This local formula applies only when those supplied reviewed terms explicitly select its fixed pre-cap method. Synthetic evaluations do not establish a valid borrowing certificate or legal enforceability.
