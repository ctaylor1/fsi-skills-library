# Local method

For eligible collateral, credit equals uncalled commitment (subscription) or supplied NAV (NAV facility), multiplied by `(1-haircut) × advance_rate`. Ineligible collateral contributes zero. Sum credits before concentration. For each group, included credit is the smaller of group credit and fixed total pre-cap credit times its reviewed maximum fraction. Sum included groups for borrowing base. This is a specific reviewed agreement illustration, not a universal lending formula.

Pro forma drawn = outstanding + proposed draw. Credit usage = pro forma drawn + LC + reserves. Draw headroom = minimum(commitment, borrowing base) minus usage. Keep negative headroom; do not report it as zero availability. Facility covenant headroom = reviewed debt cap minus drawn. Fund debt = drawn + other fund debt. Compare debt against `fund_nav × maximum_fund_debt_to_nav` exactly; the displayed ratio is rounded to12 decimal places and never drives covenant classification.

Period interest = constant pro forma drawn × supplied annual interest rate × days/day basis. Undrawn fee = maximum(0, commitment minus drawn minus LC) × supplied annual fee rate × days/day basis. Each cost is rounded to12 decimals; total is their displayed sum plus upfront fee. Reserves are a credit buffer, not a funded balance or reduction of fee-bearing commitment under this declared method. The agreement must support this interpretation. Variable balance schedules, benchmark resets, compounding, PIK, cash sweeps, default rates, multi-currency borrowing, tiered/circular concentration limits and hybrid/subordinated structures require separately accepted engines and contract interpretation.

Eligibility, valuation, total-register completeness, collateral enforceability, consent and proceeds permissions remain supplied assumptions. This method neither increases distributions nor establishes an investment performance improvement. Specialist approval and actual lender reconciliation are required before any transaction.
