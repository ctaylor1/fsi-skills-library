# Handoffs

Use `private-capital-commitment-planner` for institutional commitment/call pacing, `private-fund-performance-analyzer` for performance with correctly classified cashflows and `private-fund-capital-activity-reviewer` for capital-call/distribution operational evidence. Preserve facility and fund IDs, currency, agreement version, source/cutoff dates, collateral exclusions, balances, proceeds assumptions and unresolved issues. Prepare and validate each receiver's actual schema; no automatic conversion is supplied.

Complex facility math requires an institution-approved engine with request/input hash, object/currency/as-of binding, engine and model versions, applicable method profile, limitations and independent benchmark evidence. An engine result must not confer lending or transaction authority. Counsel, controller and lender acceptance are separate gates.
