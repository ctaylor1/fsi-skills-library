# Controls

The risk tier and accountable role remain in SKILL.md metadata. Native mode is offline draft-only. No source-record overwrite, trade, payment, price override, official NAV strike, accounting posting, legal conclusion, composite change or case closure is authorized.

Preserve ambiguity and unknown measurements; review dependent nulls and needs-data states before use. Independent source authentication, licensing, policy applicability and professional adjudication are deployment responsibilities. External delivery and any system-of-record action require the designated approval process; a valid artifact grants no execution permission.

Validate native output against the separately retained original input with `--input`. Do not embed source documents by default or pass restricted source evidence to a broader audience. Author a separately reviewed narrative and audience-specific handoff. No autonomous writes, connectors, retries or scheduled execution are bundled.
