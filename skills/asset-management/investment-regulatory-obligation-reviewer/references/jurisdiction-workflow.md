# Practitioner workflow and interpretation

Define the decision: for example, compare the evidence needed for a named manager's proposed distribution activity in two host jurisdictions. Identify entity legal form/status, activity, product, investor category, home/host jurisdictions and professional owner from actual records. A jurisdiction name or broad fund label is insufficient. Separate each activity and investor segment when they have different conditions.

Have the legal/compliance owner identify the operative rules, delegated measures, local implementation, regulator guidance, exceptions and transitional provisions. Record exact provisions, versions, lifecycle and both effective and knowledge dates. A consultation is retained as proposed; a future-effective final text is not applied to today's activity. Do not replace prior historical profiles with later versions when replaying an earlier knowledge cutoff.

Translate only the supplied reviewed conditions into explicit accepted entity/activity/product/investor values and a territorial basis. The local method is a consistency comparison, not a legal interpretation engine. Known false conditions can establish that a supplied profile does not match; absent material facts cannot establish it matches. Cross-border-only means the supplied home and host differ and every other reviewed condition still matches.

Supply reviewed absolute deadlines and their observed triggers. Business-day calendars, event-specific extensions, submission mechanics and transitional relief require the owner; the calculator only reports calendar distance from supplied due dates. It does not infer that a completed flag discharges an obligation.

Compare active profiles addressing the same obligation. Similar obligations can legitimately require parallel actions; differing dates or terms are potential conflicts requiring review, not proof that a regulator conflicts. Same-jurisdiction overlapping versions also require explicit supersession/effectivity review. Never remove a profile, choose a presumed stricter outcome or invent a cross-border exemption to make the matrix clear.

Prepare the [comparison template](../assets/jurisdiction-output-template.md) separately from immutable native JSON. Retain confirmed source facts, reviewed interpretations, assumptions and open questions distinctly. Use current authoritative source text for the institution's actual decision. Market access, legal advice, filings and external delivery remain with the authorized professional owner.
