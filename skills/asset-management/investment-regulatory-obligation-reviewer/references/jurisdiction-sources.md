# Primary source guide

Primary references checked 2026-10-04:

- [SEC statutes and regulations](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/statutes-regulations) is a primary navigation source for the actual U.S. statutes and regulatory text. Use the operative provision and current amendments when preparing a U.S. profile; this index is not itself an applicability finding.
- [EUR-Lex AIFMD original and related consolidated versions](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/en/ALL/?uri=celex%3A32011L0061) provides the directive and links to its legal history. A directive, national implementation and regulatory guidance have distinct roles; record which supplied text governs each condition.
- [UK AIFM Regulations 2013, revised text and changes](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/1773/contents) supports inspecting versions, commencement and outstanding amendments. Review the actual activity and temporal provisions; do not use an old point-in-time view as current law.

These are authoritative discovery anchors, not installed jurisdiction packs. No numerical threshold, exemption, investor classification, passporting right or deadline is hard-coded from them. In the synthetic fixtures, TEST-HOME/TEST-HOST and their conditions are invented test policies, explicitly not extracted legal rules. A primary URL there demonstrates source-link binding only.

The actual request needs entity/source records, reviewed operative provision, authority and version, effective interval, knowledge/retrieval date, and a named legal/compliance reviewer. Maintain the source's exact location and version; local consistency checks cannot authenticate the source or certify the reviewer's authority. Refresh current primary text before a real applicability decision.
