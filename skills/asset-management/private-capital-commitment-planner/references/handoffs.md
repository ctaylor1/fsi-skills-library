# Ownership and handoffs

LP capital-activity review supplies a reconciled opening snapshot. An allocator supplies future assumptions. Institutional policy/liquidity reviewers receive dated scenario evidence with unresolved inputs; a committee owns commitment decisions.

Carry request/object ids, source versions and hashes, source/knowledge dates, method/version, the specific finding and unresolved issues. A named adjacent skill is a routing destination, not an installed runtime dependency. Prepare and validate the receiving native schema; do not forward this result as if schemas were interchangeable. No automatic cross-skill action is implemented.
