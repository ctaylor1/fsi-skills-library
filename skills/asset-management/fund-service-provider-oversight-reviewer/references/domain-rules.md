# Bounded domain methods

## service_oversight

Compute failures/observations and compare exact failures to threshold times observations. Zero observations yield unavailable rate. Preserve assurance expiry/conclusion and age open issues against supplied due dates; supplied closed issues require closure evidence.

## Interpretation and exclusions

This is fund-service observation analysis, not enterprise vendor onboarding, SOC audit opinion, material outsourcing determination, procurement or incident remediation. A clean supplied assurance conclusion cannot close open issues.

Exact count/threshold cross-product for SLA decisions; displayed rate rounded to12 decimals. Calendar-day issue aging; business-day SLA clocks not inferred.

Use the supplied approved method and policy version; this package does not infer professional approval from source strings. Effective/expiry dates are enforced for referenced sources. Unknown evidence stays unavailable or rejects the input. Multiple modes are separate workpapers; do not add their outputs as if they were independent contributions.
