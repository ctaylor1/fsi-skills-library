**Access and information boundaries:** List principals, licensed purposes, permitted dataset and portfolio/client scopes, prohibited effects, retention/training terms and unresolved live permission evidence.

**Experiment results:** Show requested, observed and missing cases; known and unknown labels; errors and unsafe effects with their exact denominators; measured cost/latency coverage and finite-sample limitations. Explain every failed or unrun challenge case.

**Process economics:** Reference the reconstructed shared automation assessment and its assumptions/findings. Preserve its distinction between capacity and realizable cash.
