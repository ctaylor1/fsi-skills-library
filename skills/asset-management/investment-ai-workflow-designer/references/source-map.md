# Evidence ownership

| Evidence | Accountable owner | Supplied record and limit |
|---|---|---|
| Business decision, allowed effects, portfolio/client barrier | Investment and control owner | Workflow and case scope; declared identity strings do not prove access. |
| Research records and point-in-time snapshot | Neutral investment-research and data owner | Dataset source, snapshot, as-valid and known date; no research copying or inferred current data. |
| Licensed purpose and entitlement | Data procurement / identity owner | Separate license source, effective interval and permitted scopes; local checks do not authenticate terms. |
| Model, prompt, retrieval/tool configuration | Model and technology owner | Pinned workflow and episode versions, actual runtime logs; a friendly model alias is insufficient. |
| Held-out manifest and expected labels | Independent evaluation reviewer | Separate source and reviewer, frozen version and known date; independence must be verified outside this artifact. |
| Episode labels, effects, cost and latency | Runtime/observability owner | Separate observed source and case-linked records including failures; no executions occur here. |
| Process baseline and economics | Shared workflow-automation-assessor | Detached native input/output reconstructed by the canonical generated implementation. |

The executable method uses supplied institution evidence rather than embedding changeable regulatory requirements or paid data terms. Request the applicable source and accountable interpretation; do not infer that geography or industry label establishes a license or regulation. Retain original records under their actual data restrictions.
