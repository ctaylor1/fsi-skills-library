# Native owner handoffs

`workflow-automation-assessor` owns paired process measurements and capacity/cash economics. Supply its native input and output in `shared_reviews.automation`; the bundle reconstructs the native result from detached input and propagates findings. The generated shared module is version/hash pinned by the library resource lock. Manual utility copies and external relative import paths are unsupported.

`investment-data-architecture-planner` owns point-in-time research/holdings data, licensed connector design, source identity and access evidence. `investment-operating-model-planner` owns responsibility, service controls and incident escalation. `investment-platform-modernization-planner` owns migration, parallel-run, recovery and cutover planning. Send only the necessary scope, retained issues and source pointers; recipient-specific inputs must be rebuilt under that package's schema and validated.

Neutral issuer/security research stays with the investment-research owner. Research, trading, portfolio construction, compliance and disclosure decisions remain with their specialist packages and human owners. No consumer may interpret `review_candidate` as deployment, trade, client delivery or source authenticity approval.
