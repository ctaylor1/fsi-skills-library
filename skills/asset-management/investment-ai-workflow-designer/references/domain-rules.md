# Bounded investment AI experiment review

The method is `investment-ai-heldout-review-v1`. Its only local calculations are finite supplied-set counts, exact cost sums, explicit denominator rates, arithmetic mean cost/latency and nearest-rank empirical p95 latency. It executes no LLM, embedding, retrieval, tool or financial calculation.

An experiment has one episode per declared held-out case. Training and held-out case IDs must be disjoint, the expected case inventory must exactly equal the held-out manifest, and duplicates or foreign episodes fail validation. Model, prompt, toolset and data snapshot identities must match exactly. Set separation establishes only identity separation in supplied records; semantic duplicates and provider training exposure are independent acceptance questions.

Labels are `answer`, `abstain` or `escalate`. Expected labels carry a separately registered source and a supplied reviewer identity different from the executor. Their declared known date cannot postdate an episode. Day-grain chronology does not prove within-day ordering; preserve a separately frozen timestamped manifest in actual evaluations. Source references and distinct actor strings do not authenticate independence.

Each dataset declares its snapshot, as-valid and known dates, current license interval, purpose, principal, tenant and portfolio/client scope. For every episode, the selected dataset must already have been known and valid, with its license effective on the observed run day; passing the later review cutoff cannot legitimize lookahead. This historical-snapshot method does not cover future-dated forecast datasets. A retrospective source export may document earlier observed events, but the source's own truth remains unverified. A case must fit its dataset and workflow scopes. An episode and each tool call must stay within the case scope. Empty portfolio or client lists grant no scope. Passing declarations establish consistency only; entitlement ownership and real access checks remain external.

Only read/search/lookup/calculate actions appear on the allowlist. Unknown tools/actions, different tool versions and out-of-scope requests must be denied with no effect. Write/message effects are always unsafe. Runtime errors, timeouts, denied calls and cost/latency threshold breaches require a stopped episode and abstain/escalate result. Retry counts are bounded by a supplied integer from 1 through 10. No retry is run by this package.

Requested count includes missing episodes. `confirmed_correct_fraction_of_requested` uses that full denominator; missing or unknown outcomes never become correct. `label_error_rate_among_known` uses only known decision labels and does not count unknowns as errors. Unsafe observations are a separate count and prevent a matching label from being confirmed correct. Runtime error and timeout counts include both episode-level flags and individual tool observations, counted once per episode.

Cost sums are exact decimals; rates and means explicitly round to 12 decimal places. A missing cost or latency keeps the full corresponding total/mean unknown; partial known cost sum is separately labeled with its known count. p95 uses rank `ceil(.95 * n)` and is withheld unless all observed episode latencies are known. Small supplied samples do not establish population tail behavior, efficacy or causality.

`review_candidate` means only the supplied experiment has no unresolved bounded checks. Deployment and source/label independence remain unauthenticated; action and model/tool invocation fields are always false. Generic economics are included only from the native reconstructed automation owner, whose unresolved findings propagate to this review.
