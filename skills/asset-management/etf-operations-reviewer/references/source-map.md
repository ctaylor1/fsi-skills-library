# Source hierarchy and verification

Use controlled portfolio composition file, approved NAV, AP agreement and timestamped order/cutoff terms as the domain evidence. Reconcile conflicting snapshots with the data owner; preserve discrepancies and exact version/known-at dates. Registry and source-use assertions are supplied evidence, not live authentication. No retrieval connector is bundled.

## Primary method references

- [SEC ETF small entity compliance guide](https://www.sec.gov/investment/exchange-traded-funds-small-entity-compliance-guide). US staff guide dated2019-12-06, updated2022-11-21; explains adopted Rule6c-11 and its scope. Basket/cash, AP and custom-basket governance are distinct; guide is not a substitute for rule text or current applicability review.
- [ESMA ETF/UCITS guidelines2014/937](https://www.esma.europa.eu/sites/default/files/library/2015/11/esma-2014-0011-01-00_en_0.pdf). EU guidelines dated2014-08-01 with specified UCITS scope. Secondary market price and NAV differ; US ETF rule conditions are not silently imported into UCITS.

References checked2026-09-21. They explain method context and jurisdiction/status; they do not replace vehicle-specific documents or establish legal applicability. Recheck changed law/guidance and the deployed method before reliance. Record economic date separately from date known; never use evidence learned after the request knowledge cutoff.
