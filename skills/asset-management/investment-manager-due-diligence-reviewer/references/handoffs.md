# Handoffs

- `due-diligence-questionnaire-responder`: A manager’s own response drafting is a separate producer-side workflow.
- `investment-recommendation-evidence-reviewer`: Review a household recommendation after the responsible professional proposes it.
- `portfolio-exposure-analyzer`: Obtain independently sourced strategy/portfolio exposure analysis.
- `performance-attribution-builder`: Use supported performance and attribution calculations without reinterpreting them as a forecast.
- `private-market-client-reviewer`: Supply the manager artifact ID/hash, dated review status, criteria and unresolved findings for client-level commitments, notices, settlements and eligibility evidence. Manager diligence does not replace client account funding analysis.
- `client-review-preparer`: Include diligence concerns and monitoring questions in the household review.

Transmit artifact ID, household/request IDs, engagement capacity, policy version, source registry, substantive result and unresolved findings. These are draft evidence handoffs, not API calls or authority to act. Counsel, licensed professionals and custody operations own their respective decisions.
