# Bounded domain methods

## entitlements

Cash dividend gross=eligible quantity times cash/share; deduct supplied withholding rate. Split total units=eligible quantity times new/old ratio; additional units=post-split minus original. Compare supplied receipt to expected entitlement, using explicit tolerance and payable date.

## Interpretation and exclusions

The supplied eligible record-date position is essential. No inference from unsettled trades/ex-dates, stock-loan claims, beneficial ownership, currency conversion or treaty tax. Fractions remain entitlement evidence; cash-in-lieu unsupported.

Cash arithmetic exact Decimal; stock-split ratio rounded to24 decimal places. Tolerance is supplied in cash or additional-security-unit measure as appropriate.

Use the supplied approved method and policy version; this package does not infer professional approval from source strings. Effective/expiry dates are enforced for referenced sources. Unknown evidence stays unavailable or rejects the input. Multiple modes are separate workpapers; do not add their outputs as if they were independent contributions.
