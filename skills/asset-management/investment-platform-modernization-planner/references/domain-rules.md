# Method and interpretation

Generic architecture costs and nonfunctional fit belong to `solution-architecture-planner`; this adapter recomputes and consumes that native result. It does not independently total costs, rank vendors or invent scores. Alternative selection still needs procurement, information security, legal and business-owner review of actual evidence.

1. Require an explicit mapped or reviewed excluded disposition for all seven AM platform roles. Authority is defined by exact business domain, book scope and current/proposed state, not a product acronym. Shared components, AM systems and migration snapshots must resolve.
2. Each batch dependency must be in a strictly earlier integer wave. This rejects dangling edges, self-dependencies and cycles rather than repairing them. Preserve versioned mapping and distinct source/target snapshot identities.
3. For each complete financial grain, compute `difference = target value - source value` exactly; `within_tolerance = abs(difference) <= supplied nonnegative tolerance`. Do not round before comparison. Do not net offsetting breaks, sum mixed currencies, infer FX, resolve identifiers or choose a canonical book. A complete declared grain inventory is required; a missing comparison blocks readiness. An inventory review declaration is not independent proof of completeness.
4. Tie parallel-run records to the same mapping and snapshots. Retain all exceptions. Open and accepted exceptions block readiness; an accepted risk is not a reconciled record. A claimed closure needs source-bound resolution and chronology.
5. Compare supplied observed RTO and RPO seconds independently against supplied reviewed limits using inclusive comparison. All actual observations must be known by the review/cutoff/source-known date. Recovery evidence must postdate run completion. Every run needs recovery evidence; post-cutover writes require explicit observed delta-replay evidence bound to a latest-ending run for the same batch, mapping and snapshots. The replay cannot predate that rehearsal's completion.
6. Retain reconciliation, parallel-run, rollback, security and business-acceptance human gates for each batch. Native gates and business acceptance cannot predate exception closures or delta replay; business acceptance also follows all batch snapshot/run/recovery observations. Previously passed labels cannot certify evidence first observed later. A scoped external security review remains distinct from final business acceptance. Computed issues and inherited native issues take precedence over any supplied passed label. Evidence-ready means a supplied-evidence packet is ready for human review, never production-ready or authorized.

Specialist limits: no trade/order lifecycle simulation, accounting close, NAV approval, derivative valuation, tax-lot matching, corporate-action election, settlement guarantee, production failover test, identity authentication or regulatory certification. Route these questions with preserved issues and sources to the appropriate specialist and deployment owner.
