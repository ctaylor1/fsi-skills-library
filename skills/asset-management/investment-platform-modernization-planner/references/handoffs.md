# Handoffs

`solution-architecture-planner` is the canonical generic owner. Its native input/output pair is mandatory and reconstructed in this adapter. Keep object/date/currency and source versions exact; do not relabel an unrelated architecture review.

Use `investment-data-architecture-planner` for point-in-time data, licensed-purpose and data-contract evidence, and `investment-operating-model-planner` for target responsibilities, servicing arrangements and operational measurement. Provide a scoped packet containing request/object IDs, review date/cutoff, currency basis, mapping/snapshot versions, source references, method limitations and all unresolved issues. These narrative handoffs require the receiver's native input schema and validation; this package supplies no automatic cross-schema conversion.

Send accounting/NAV, investment risk, trading controls and legal applicability questions to their actual professional owners. Planning authority never becomes transaction, posting or cutover authority through a handoff. Production connector, security and deployment acceptance belong to the deploying institution and remain unverified here.
