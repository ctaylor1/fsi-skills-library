# Evidence to obtain

| Claim | Source and accountable owner |
| --- | --- |
| Platform and authoritative record responsibilities | Current architecture and operating model versions; investment technology and fund controller |
| Provider purpose, schema, versions, retention and license | Executed/provider-approved contracts and entitlement evidence; procurement, data licensing and legal |
| Actual permissions and connectivity | Trusted deployment identity/access verifier and read-only connector receipts; security and platform operations |
| Requirements, scope and mandatory dates | Supplied institutional policies, counsel/operations interpretation and change mandates; business and compliance owners |
| Native architecture alternatives, costs and fit | Detached `solution-architecture-planner` input and output with original measurement sources |
| Financial grain and snapshot identity | Approved source/target mappings, versioned snapshots and full inventory reconciliation; accounting and investment-data owners |
| Tolerances | Versioned, scoped supplied policy with named reviewer; controller or relevant control owner |
| Run results and exception resolution | Observed parallel-run logs and exception records; migration operations |
| RTO/RPO, rollback and delta replay | Approved continuity requirements and observed recovery/replay evidence; resilience owner |
| Human gate disposition | Dated identifiable review records; each accountable gate owner |

The native method is an internal engineering evidence check and makes no claim of compliance with a market standard, accounting standard or jurisdiction. Institutional policies and professional acceptance remain deployment inputs. Synthetic fixtures establish bounded software behavior only.
