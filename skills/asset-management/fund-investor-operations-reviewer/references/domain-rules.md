# Bounded domain methods

## dealing_review

Compare timezone-aware order receipt to supplied cutoff. Require current supplied-cleared AML evidence and NAV at the exact supplied valuation timestamp before indicative pricing. Subscription units=(cash-fee)/NAV; redemption net cash=units times NAV-fee. Late/unresolved orders remain unpriced.

## Interpretation and exclusions

Fund terms, domicile, applicable law, receipt chain, share class, AML/KYC and approved NAV must be established by accountable operators. Supplied-cleared is evidence text, not this skill issuing AML clearance. No gates, swing pricing, dilution or acceptance engine.

Subscription units rounded to8 decimals and corresponding cash residual disclosed. Redemption cash exact Decimal before any external settlement rounding. Timestamps compare actual instants; date boundary uses valuation timezone.

Use the supplied approved method and policy version; this package does not infer professional approval from source strings. Effective/expiry dates are enforced for referenced sources. Unknown evidence stays unavailable or rejects the input. Multiple modes are separate workpapers; do not add their outputs as if they were independent contributions.
