# Bounded domain methods

## tracking

Compound paired portfolio/index returns separately; subtract cumulative returns for tracking difference. Annualized tracking error is sample standard deviation of periodic active returns times square root of declared periods/year. One observation yields unavailable tracking error.

## weights

Require complete long-only portfolio/index weight union including cash, each summing exactly1. Report active weights and half-L1 distance, an idealized one-way weight realignment measure.

## Interpretation and exclusions

Returns must share intended currency and economically comparable bases; net fund versus gross index differences include fees/tax effects. No forecast tracking risk, constituent licensing, derivatives replication or reconstitution trading.

Linked factors and means24 decimal places; sample variance48; square root128-digit context; reported tracking error24. Weight sums/products exact Decimal.
