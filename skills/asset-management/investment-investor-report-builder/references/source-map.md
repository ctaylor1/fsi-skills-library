# Source contract and method references

Sources are supplied offline snapshots. Prefer official records and signed/versioned firm policies, then reconciled administrator/performance records, then attributed manager/client statements. Capture source identity, scope, observation date, knowledge date, version, permission and exact claim payload during intake. Do not upgrade an assertion to verified merely because it has a citation.

Method references (checked 2026-09-21; deployment teams must determine applicability):

- [SEC investment adviser marketing guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing): scope and presentation constraints for advisers subject to the rule; this package does not certify advertisements.
- [SEC marketing staff FAQs](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/staff-guidance/division-investment-management-frequently-asked-questions/marketing-compliance-frequently-asked-questions): staff guidance on performance presentation, not an automatic rule implementation.
- [FCA PROD 3.2](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/prod3/prod3s2): product-governance and target-market context where applicable; supplied policy gates do not establish UK legal compliance.
- [GIPS Handbook for Firms](https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/): performance-method context; supplied returns and these checks do not establish GIPS compliance.
- [SEC alternative-manager due diligence risk alert, 2014](https://www.sec.gov/about/offices/ocie/adviser-due-diligence-alternative-investments.pdf): historical staff observations on investment and operational diligence; not a complete current legal checklist.
- [ILPA DDQ 2.0](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/due-diligence-questionnaire/): private-markets question taxonomy; it is not represented as a universal public-market or investor policy.

Local arithmetic is expressly described in domain-rules. No external analytics-engine adapter, live connector or independently verified source is bundled.
