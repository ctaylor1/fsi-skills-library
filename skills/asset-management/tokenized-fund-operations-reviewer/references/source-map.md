# Source map

Primary reference checked 2026-10-04: [FCA COLL 6 Annex 4, DLT registers for authorised funds](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/coll6/coll6s19). The guidance addresses register responsibility, eligibility controls, aggregation across wallets and network risks within its stated scope. It also allows an on-chain record to be primary when applicable requirements are met. This package therefore requires the supplied legal register basis; it never assumes that either the chain or an off-chain register is universally authoritative.

For the actual fund, obtain constitutional/prospectus terms, legal register owner determination, administrator issued-unit statement, complete dated ledger/wallet exports, custodial subregister, transfer restrictions and institution-reviewed finality policy. Capture exact versions, block/snapshot IDs and locators. The synthetic example is not a UK legal profile or a prescribed confirmation threshold.

Jurisdiction applicability, chain-specific finality, custody title and investor eligibility remain specialist determinations. This package implements arithmetic and supplied evidence comparisons, not FCA compliance certification or any blockchain protocol.
