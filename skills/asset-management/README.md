# Asset management

Start with [asset-management-router](asset-management-router/SKILL.md) for a multi-step
request or an unclear professional job. The router plans from declared intents and
reports scope gaps; it does not execute another skill or establish permission.

This category owns manager/product operations, portfolio and mandate application,
investment oversight, fund operations, investor service and stewardship. Reusable
issuer/security/industry research belongs to [Investment Research](../investment-research/README.md).
Transaction advice belongs to [Investment Banking](../investment-banking/README.md).
Household advice remains Wealth Management. Shared investment source indexing and
record normalization belong to Data, AI & Model Governance.

The [support matrix](../../docs/asset-management/SUPPORT-MATRIX.md) lists all 64 target
AM capabilities and four moved shared capabilities. Phases 1 and 2 now contain **54 AM
packages**. Phase 2 adds 29 business, institutional investment, accounting, performance,
ETF/index and private-market foundations and expands proposal, manager diligence and
committee modes. The ten remaining capabilities are explicit Phase 3/4 gaps. See the
[Phase 2 implementation](../../docs/asset-management/PHASE2-IMPLEMENTATION.md).
Production accounting, optimization, complex pricing/risk and institutional acceptance
remain separately required even when a bounded review package exists.

See the [original-package disposition](../../docs/asset-management/DISPOSITION.md),
[architecture decisions](../../docs/asset-management/ARCHITECTURE-DECISIONS.md),
[implementation plan](../../docs/asset-management/IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md) and
[contract and packaging guide](../../docs/asset-management/CONTRACTS-AND-PACKAGING.md).

Results are drafts for professional review. Native reconstruction tests bind results
to separately retained supplied inputs; they do not authenticate sources or validate
business judgment. Execution, publication and changes to institutional records remain
outside these packages.
