# Sources and limits

Primary reference review:2026-10-04. [ILPA's continuation-fund disclosure template](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/continuation-fund-disclosure-template/) is a2026 organizing aid for LP cash/roll analysis and explicitly does not replace definitive transaction materials. [ILPA's2023 continuation-fund guidance](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/continuation-funds-considerations-for-limited-partners-and-general-partners/) frames transparency and process review. [ILPA's resource library](https://ilpa.org/resources-tools/resource-library/) also identifies2026 updated continuation-vehicle materials as draft; draft guidance is not treated here as adopted law or operative terms.

The actual transaction agreement, pricing statement, NAV records, interim call/distribution notices, fee/carry allocation and side letters supply native inputs. Obtain materials/election/settlement dates and conflict resolutions from their accountable parties. Future valuation, return assumptions and closing true-ups need separate evidence and specialist review. The package neither authenticates these sources nor establishes an appropriate election.
