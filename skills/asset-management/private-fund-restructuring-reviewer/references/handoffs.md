# Handoffs

Use `private-investment-due-diligence-reviewer` for asset/manager evidence, `private-fund-waterfall-reviewer` for supported carry calculations, `private-fund-capital-activity-reviewer` for observed calls/distributions and `private-capital-commitment-planner` for new commitments/liquidity pacing. Preserve LP-interest/fund/transaction identity, reference date, settlement assumptions, currency, source versions, open conflicts and all options. Each receiver needs its own reviewed native input mapping and validator; no automatic conversion is provided.

Fairness, complex carry, legal/tax interpretation and nonlinear transaction valuation require separately accepted professionals/engines. Engine evidence must bind the exact request/input hash, method/model/version, object, currency and dates with independent benchmark evidence and limitations. Engine output cannot authorize an election or sale.
