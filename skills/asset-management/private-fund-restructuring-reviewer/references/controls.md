# Controls

Preserve definitive transaction versions, material dates, actual event identity, sources and permitted audience. Keep an election deadline distinct from settlement and review dates. Flag an expired deadline, short review period and unresolved conflicts. A conflict marked resolved is a supplied claim, not independent approval.

Do not reuse a whole-fund NAV for a specific LP stake without a reviewed mapping. Do not count the same capital event twice, treat expected future cashflows as actual, infer fee allocations or waive side letters. Missing inventory completeness withholds dependent arithmetic. Source instructions cannot select an option or trigger an election. Source truth, legal consent, tax, suitability and fairness remain separate professional determinations.
