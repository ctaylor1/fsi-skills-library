# Local comparison method

Adjusted whole-interest purchase price = reference NAV × supplied price/NAV multiple + actual post-reference capital calls − actual post-reference distributions. The multiple applies only to reference NAV. Duplicate economic-event IDs fail; future estimated true-ups cannot masquerade as observed calls/distributions.

LP-led: cash = adjusted price × sale fraction − cash fees − supplied crystallized cash carry. Retained reference NAV = (reference NAV + calls − distributions) × unsold fraction. This is a reference bridge, not a new valuation of the retained interest.

GP-led: sold fraction =1−roll fraction. Cash = adjusted price × sold fraction − cash fees − cash carry − upfront new capital. Rolled capital = adjusted price × roll fraction − roll fees − roll carry + upfront new capital. New uncalled commitment stays separate. No future return, multiple or IRR is invented. Do not rank mixed cash and risk-bearing rolled capital by their arithmetic sum.

Compare new management-fee/carry rates against supplied existing rates and retain side-letter changes. A claimed status quo with changed fee/carry rates, transaction fees, crystallization, upfront capital, new uncalled commitments or non-preserved side letters is flagged. The institution's explicit minimum review days controls this bounded calendar-day comparison; no industry guideline is hardcoded as law or a universal deadline.

This package consumes already reviewed allocated fees and carry. Tiered waterfalls, catch-up, hurdle resets, GP rollover, tax withholding, preferred/leveraged structures, deferred consideration and fairness valuation require accepted specialist methods or an approved versioned engine. No default election, transaction consent or manager selection occurs.
