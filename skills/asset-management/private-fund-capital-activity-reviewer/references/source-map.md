No live MCP/API is bundled; deployment adapters must provide authorized immutable dated evidence and separately reviewed mappings.

- [ILPA 2025 template guidance](https://ilpa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ILPA-Suggested-Guidance-2025-Final.pdf): September 2025; records LP amounts and explicit unfunded impact. This native schema separately carries recallable classification for review and does not claim verbatim template compatibility.
