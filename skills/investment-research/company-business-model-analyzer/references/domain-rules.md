# Method and limitations

Contribution profit = revenue minus variable cost; contribution margin uses positive revenue. Revenue per unit uses positive units. Segment/customer shares use positive consolidated revenue. Reconcile known segment sum to supplied consolidated revenue; incomplete coverage prevents a full reconciliation claim. Do not turn contribution into EBIT, named customer exposures into full concentration, or management claims into verified pricing power.

All analysis is an internal draft from supplied evidence. The common contract withholds results if any declared evidence source fails timing, identity, permission, classification or validity checks. Complete-for-supplied-scope does not prove source authenticity, full population coverage, institutional review or deployment readiness.
