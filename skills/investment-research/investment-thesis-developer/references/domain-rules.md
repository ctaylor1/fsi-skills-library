# Method and limitations

Variant difference = analyst minus consensus; relative gap divides by absolute nonzero consensus. The arithmetic does not establish market pricing or misvaluation. Pending catalysts before as_of become overdue-unresolved. Evaluate the supplied falsifier operator against its threshold only with observed value and citation. A triggered falsifier yields revisit-thesis; no automatic trade. Counter-evidence remains unresolved and analyst judgments stay supplied.

All analysis is an internal draft from supplied evidence. The common contract withholds results if any declared evidence source fails timing, identity, permission, classification or validity checks. Complete-for-supplied-scope does not prove source authenticity, full population coverage, institutional review or deployment readiness.
