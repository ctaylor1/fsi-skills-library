# Method and limitations

Retain instrument/security/legal issuer/estate/document/version/guarantee/collateral identities. Sum principal only within exact currency and unit groups; unknown principal withholds full total and unknown dates withhold maturity completeness. Priority links are checked for unknown IDs, self links and cycles; they remain supplied assertions. No implicit guarantees, cross-currency totals or financing recommendation is generated. The legacy Phase 1 contract remains supported when schema_version is absent.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
