# Method and supported scope

Reconcile supplied segment enterprise and equity valuations into group equity with explicit debt, cash, corporate-cost and component non-overlap controls.

The domain helper validates structural, finite-number, basis and method conditions before computation; the separately retained original is required for complete output reconstruction. Supplied economic bounds and source metadata are evidence inputs, not institutional endorsements. Negative equity and failed funding checks remain visible. Professional acceptance remains not established.

Equity equals sum segment values minus debt attributable to enterprise-basis segments plus cash outside segment values minus preferred/minority claims and separately capitalized corporate costs. Segment component IDs cannot overlap; equity-basis segment debt is excluded from group subtraction. Source inventories determine attribution truth.
