# Public-finance financial mechanics

GO available recurring resources = general revenue - operating costs excluding debt service/pension - pension paid. Direct annual debt service is direct principal due + direct interest due. Illustrative general-resource coverage divides those available resources by debt service; recurrent balance subtracts service. Restricted cash never funds these measures. Fund balance/operating-cost ratio uses unrestricted available fund balance divided by operating costs plus paid pension. Direct debt/tax base requires complete direct inventory and positive stated tax base; neither ratio establishes taxing authority, priority, statutory capacity or legal pledge. Pension contribution shortfall = max(0, required-paid); unfunded actuarial liability is shown separately, never added as debt principal or treated as a cash payment.

A net pledge uses gross pledged revenue - operating costs - required prior payments before dividing by direct service. A gross pledge uses gross pledged revenue - required prior payments: operating costs are still reported in the separate system cash balance, not silently treated as paid. Debt service reserves are one-time supplements and do not raise recurring coverage; report recurring and after-reserve cash shortfalls separately. Numerical margin against a supplied threshold never establishes covenant compliance; definitions of revenues, permitted adjustments, priority, flow of funds and additional-bonds tests require actual documents and institutional/legal review.

Capital funding gap = max(0, capital needs - committed capital funding). Capital costs are not silently mixed into recurring coverage or assumed financed. Overlapping and contingent exposure allocable shares produce separate displayed amounts only; direct/overlapping debt tax-base definitions vary and must be adjudicated before any combined burden measure. An incomplete inventory prevents total coverage and residual-service conclusions.

Revenue/cost/interest/capital scenarios multiply the relevant supplied amount by 1+change; principal is not repriced. No market prices, credit spreads, agency rating, recovery value, legal enforcement, tax exemption or purchase/credit approval is calculated. Full sovereign, municipal law, pension actuarial and structured-finance methods are distinct workflows.
