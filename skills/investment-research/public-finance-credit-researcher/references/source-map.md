# Method provenance and actual evidence

Reviewed 2026-10-20; examples are synthetic. Definitions inform this deliberately bounded method, not a universal legal standard or covenant model.

- [MSRB, Sources of Repayment](https://www.msrb.org/Sources-Repayment): distinguishes issuer GO support from specifically pledged revenue sources; actual repayment rights depend on the security's documents.
- [MSRB, Official Statements](https://www.msrb.org/Official-Statements): primary disclosure context for bond terms, repayment sources, covenants and credit support. Actual final statements, amendments and continuing disclosures must be supplied and dated; retrieval/access is not bundled.
- [MSRB Glossary of Municipal Securities Terms, third edition (2013), pledged revenues and revenue pledge entries](https://www.msrb.org/sites/default/files/2022-08/MSRB-Glossary-of-Municipal-Securities-Terms-Third_Edition-August-2013.pdf): source context for gross/net pledge ordering. This package takes the pledge classification as supplied and does not parse or validate its legal effect.

Use jurisdiction-specific audited financials, adopted budgets, debt and maturity schedules, official statements/indentures, rate and customer records, assessed valuations and taxpayer concentration, pension disclosures and capital plans. Source currency/units/accounting period must match, and restricted amounts stay distinct from available balances. Professional credit, legal and tax review remain institution-owned.
