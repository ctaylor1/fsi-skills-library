# Public-finance intake and result

Schema `ir-phase4-public-finance/1.0` extends the bundled common evidence context. Subject is public issuer or its security with issuer_id. `financial_scope.entity_id` must equal the requested issuer. The scope carries jurisdiction, accounting_basis, uppercase three-letter currency, monetary units, fiscal_year, obligation_type, pledged_system_id, pledge_basis and legal_pledge_description as supplied. GO uses obligation_type `general-obligation`, pledge_basis `general-resources`, pledged_system_id null. Revenue uses obligation_type `revenue`, one actual pledged_system_id and `gross-revenues` or `net-revenues`. Other pledge types, double-barreled, appropriation, moral-obligation, overlapping revenue systems and bankruptcy/legal analysis need a separate reviewed method; no GO fallback.

`financials` has period_start/end (330..370-day reported fiscal year ending in fiscal_year), known_at/evidence_refs plus exactly the selected mode fields. All observations share this fiscal/accounting basis. All principal/interest due rows refer to that same annual period. One-year conditional scenarios shock these reported observations; they are not a forecast of a new fiscal period.

GO fields: general_revenue; operating_costs_excluding_debt_service_and_pension; pension_required_contribution; pension_paid_contribution; unfunded_pension_liability; unrestricted_available_fund_balance; restricted_cash; assessed_tax_base; top_ten_taxpayers_share; capital_needs; committed_capital_funding. Contributions/liability/tax base/concentration may be null; null contribution prevents resource coverage calculation. Separate pension cash payments prevent operating-expense double counting. Available fund balance must already exclude restricted/nonspendable balances under the stated accounting basis.

Revenue fields: gross_pledged_revenue; operating_costs; required_other_prior_payments; available_debt_service_reserve; capital_needs; committed_capital_funding; supplied_coverage_threshold (nullable). Amounts nonnegative; no automatic inclusion of tax revenues, enterprise cash or reserves outside the documented pledged system. The numeric supplied threshold is not a parsed or legally verified covenant.

`obligation_inventory_complete` is a required supplied boolean. `obligations[]` has unique obligation_id, unique security_id, period_start, period_end, obligor_id, pledged_system_id, scope `direct`/`overlapping`/`contingent`, outstanding, principal_due, interest_due, allocable_share 0..1, included_in_direct_debt boolean, known_at/evidence_refs. Each due period must exactly equal financials.period_start/end. A security-subject request must match a direct obligation security_id; the analysis remains the selected issuer/pledged-system scope, not a claim that all its debt belongs only to that security. Duplicate security identifiers are rejected to prevent double counting. Due principal cannot exceed outstanding. Direct scope matches issuer and pledged system, has full share and false duplicate-inclusion flag. Overlapping/contingent exposures identify a distinct obligor and stay separate; included_in_direct_debt indicates potentially duplicate exposure, never authorizes aggregation. Caller must supply only disjoint direct debt inventory; hashes cannot prove no source omissions.

`scenarios[]` may be empty; otherwise unique scenario_id, conditional label, known_at/evidence_refs and revenue_change, operating_cost_change, interest_cost_change, capital_cost_change, all decimal changes >= -1. Direct principal, pension payments, tax base, fund balance and reserve are held constant; interest change is a sensitivity to supplied annual interest cash cost, not a debt repricing or market-rate duration model.

`coverage[]` uses topic, status `reviewed-supplied`/`missing`/`not-reviewed`, note, evidence_refs and known_at for reviewed rows. Required topics: pledge-documents, economic-tax-or-rate-base, operating-finances, maturity-liquidity, pension-other-liabilities, capital-maintenance, governance-data-quality. Missing topic or unreviewed status remains unresolved. Review actual official statements, indentures, continuing disclosures, audits, economic/taxpayer or ratepayer fundamentals, management and governance evidence; reviewed-supplied does not mean legally verified or professionally accepted.

All quantitative blocks/obligations/scenarios and reviewed coverage need source-known <= row-known <= cutoff with equal temporal precision. Financial statement end cannot postdate its known time. Ineligible source rights/timing/subject withholds results. Output ir-analysis/2.0 contains fiscal scope, reported metrics, separately labeled scenarios and overlapping/contingent exposures, research coverage and non-approval flags. Incomplete direct inventory suppresses total coverage/residual-balance claims while retaining explicitly supplied subtotals. Full-output reconstruction requires the separately retained entire original.
