# Research source map

Primary sources checked on 2026-10-20; these describe research information needed,
not a universal transaction template or legal-compliance conclusion. Native formulas
are deliberately bounded and require supplied deal-specific reviewed terms.

- [SEC Regulation AB Item 1111, pool assets](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/subpart-229.1100/section-229.1111): pool scope, selection criteria and distributional/concentration information guide the collateral inventory and narrative review. The eCFR view stated current through 2026-10-16.
- [SEC Regulation AB Item 1113, structure of the transaction](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/subpart-229.1100/section-229.1113): cash priorities, support, triggers, fees, reserve operation and residual disposition motivate explicit terms and order. The eCFR view stated current through 2026-10-16. No profile in this package is prescribed by that source.
- [SEC Regulation AB Item 1121, distribution and pool performance information](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-229/subpart-229.1100/section-229.1121): distribution-period cash sources, note and reserve balances, shortfalls and trigger reporting guide research reconciliation. The retrieved view stated current through 2026-10-13. These actual reports must stay distinct from analyst scenarios.

The example is wholly synthetic. Actual work needs separately retained trustee and
servicer reports, the complete governing documents and amendments, collateral data
and eligibility rules, versioned review evidence and precise permitted citations.
Source truth, rights and institutional approval are not established by this package.
