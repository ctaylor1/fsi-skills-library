# Transmission, scenario math and falsification

The theme connects a supplied driver to an economic mechanism and at least two
requested asset classes. Preserve support and counter-evidence separately;
absence of counter-evidence is a gap. Analyst narratives are hypotheses for
review, not authenticated causal findings. Explain plausible offsetting channels
and observations that could change the sign or invalidate the argument.

For each scenario-exposure pair match the explicit driver_id and require exact
shock unit identity with the sensitivity denominator. No implicit basis-point,
percent or percentage-point conversion is performed. Missing shocks produce
null responses and partial status. A shock with no supplied exposure sensitivity
also remains an issue.

- Linear response = supplied sensitivity × supplied shock; the sensitivity is
  response units per one shock unit. A −0.02 percent-price-change per basis
  point sensitivity with a +100 bp shock produces −2 percent-price-change.
- Modified-duration response in percent ≈ −modified duration × yield shock in
  basis points / 100. A 5-year modified duration and +100 bp parallel yield
  shock gives approximately −5 percent. This is a local approximation that
  omits convexity, carry, curve shape, spread interaction and path dependence.
  Supported only for rates/credit exposure with basis-point yield shocks and
  percent-price-change responses.
- Qualitative responses preserve a supplied conditional statement; no numeric
  sensitivity or price response is invented.

An approximation yielding a price change of −100 percent or below is withheld
as outside positive-price range with an explicit issue. Other numerical results
remain conditional approximations, never a calibrated loss distribution. No
instrument response is summed into a portfolio result. FX responses retain the
quote convention; a positive response means the stated quoted price increases.

Driver observations preserve historical basis, vintage and source lineage. A
past macro growth figure does not supply a future shock. Do not treat a
correlation, a dated release or the supplied transmission narrative as causal
identification. Scenario assumptions and empirical sensitivity estimates need
their own evidence and institutional review.

A falsifier needs a monitor, due date and supplied test status. An observed
falsifier marks the hypothesis challenged and keeps reassessment open. An
untested overdue falsifier is a gap. A supplied not-observed result is bounded
to its declared evidence and cannot close all uncertainty. Highlight uncovered
requested asset classes, unavailable driver values and incomplete inventories.
Keep research views distinct from portfolio sizing, mandates, personal advice,
execution, custody and external-distribution authority.
