# Method and limitations

Require equal currency, option profile, supplied rating group and spread basis, duration within supplied tolerance, and price age within a supplied integer day window. Future/stale knowledge and same-issuer peers are excluded with reasons. Eligible peer median is unweighted; target minus median is descriptive, not a trade signal. No issuer credit rating, executable price, curve interpolation or option valuation is computed.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
