# Sovereign intake and result

Schema `ir-phase4-sovereign/1.0` extends the bundled evidence-standard context. Subject is the sovereign issuer, its security (issuer_id required), or economy. `sovereign_scope.country_id` must equal that issuer/economy identity. The scope explicitly carries jurisdiction, government_scope, accounting_basis, uppercase three-letter currency, monetary units (ones/thousands/millions/billions) and debt_measure `gross`. Central government, general government and public sector are separate scopes; do not combine them. GDP is annual nominal domestic GDP in that same reporting currency/scale, not a debt consolidation scope.

`baseline` has calendar-year-end balance_date, annual gdp, domestic_debt and foreign_currency_debt_local (already translated to reporting currency at that baseline exchange rate), known_at and evidence_refs. The two debt buckets are disjoint. Essential missing stocks/GDP are rejected rather than substituted by zero.

Each `scenarios[]` has unique scenario_id, explicit conditional label, known_at/evidence_refs and one to thirty contiguous annual `years`, beginning after baseline. A year has exactly: year, nominal_gdp_growth, domestic_effective_rate, foreign_effective_rate, fx_depreciation, primary_balance, other_cash_debt_creating_flows, other_noncash_debt_creating_flows, new_debt_foreign_share, domestic_principal_due, foreign_principal_due_local, committed_financing, usable_fiscal_cash, fiscal_cash_draw. Rates/shares are decimal fractions, not percent points. Primary balance is a current-year nominal **surplus-positive** amount; cash debt-creating flows are signed nominal financing flows excluding exchange revaluation, while noncash flows are signed debt recognition/valuation adjustments that increase debt without gross cash financing needs. Principal due is in opening-year currency equivalents before scenario FX; financing and cash are current-year reporting-currency amounts. Due principal cannot exceed its opening debt bucket. Fiscal cash draw is an explicit use, bounded by available fiscal cash and gross needs; unused cash capacity does not count as financing or reduce debt. Annual available cash must be independently supplied as the remaining available balance, never reused mechanically across years. Currency depreciation and growth exceed -1; rates, due principal and funding are nonnegative. New-debt foreign share is 0..1 and allocates total non-FX debt creation (including noncash recognition) or net retirement; an allocation making either debt stock negative is rejected. Inventory maturity/commitment completeness and refinancing feasibility must be reviewed in coverage, not inferred from submitted numbers.

`external_liquidity` is null or an object: assessment_date, scope `whole-economy`, currency/units identical to reporting conversion basis, reserves_available, short_term_external_debt_residual_maturity, imports_next_12_months, known_at/evidence_refs. Three numerical observations may be null. The caller must convert their underlying FX measures consistently and cite conversion evidence. This whole-economy block never supplies government usable_fiscal_cash. Imports_next_12_months is an explicitly supplied forward assumption known at cutoff; no forecast is invented.

`coverage[]` has unique topic, status `reviewed-supplied`/`missing`/`not-reviewed`, note and evidence_refs; reviewed rows also need known_at. Required topics: fiscal-debt-scope, maturity-financing-access, external-monetary, institutions-political-risk, contingent-liabilities, data-quality. Missing rows/statuses become unresolved findings. A reviewed-supplied label records supplied analyst work only. Read actual evidence for fiscal institutions, monetary sovereignty, market access, external imbalances, political/governance conditions, contingent liabilities, data revisions and uncertainty before institutional acceptance.

Every quantitative block/scenario and reviewed coverage row requires precise top-level-declared citations. Source-known time <= row-known time <= cutoff using matching date/timestamp precision; reported balance observations cannot be known before observation. Unknown source rights/barrier/timing/identity withholds the complete numerical projection.

Output uses ir-analysis/2.0 full-original hashing. Results contain scoped baseline, scenario_paths with exact debt-ratio bridges and funding gaps, external coverage ratios/nulls, research coverage and explicit non-acceptance flags. Outer complete-for-supplied-scope means only bounded supplied-method consistency. Output verification needs the detached full original and rejects deletion, extra output fields and any numeric/control change.
