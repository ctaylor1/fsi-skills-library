# Method provenance and data needs

Reviewed 2026-10-20. These publications inform the bounded definitions; they are not bundled data, methodology certification, jurisdiction advice or permission to reuse restricted material. Fixtures are synthetic.

- [IMF, A Guide and Tool for Projecting Public Debt and Fiscal Adjustment Paths with Local- and Foreign-Currency Debt (2021), section III](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/005/2021/005/article-A001-en.xml): source framework for nominal debt, surplus-positive primary balances, currency valuation and stock-flow adjustments. This package uses a declared simplified single-FX annual case; it does not implement that entire tool.
- [IMF, A Guide and Tool for Projecting Public Gross Financing Needs (2025)](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/tnm/issues/2025/01/24/a-guide-and-tool-for-projecting-public-gross-financing-needs-555913): financing need distinguishes maturing debt, fiscal flows and exchange-rate effects; actual financing access is separately supplied.
- [IMF, Staff Guidance Note on the Sovereign Risk and Debt Sustainability Framework for Market Access Countries (2022)](https://www.elibrary.imf.org/view/journals/007/2022/039/article-A001-en.xml): institutional framework context, not an implemented certification or mechanical rating.

Use dated treasury/debt-office maturity inventories, fiscal authority accounts/budgets, statistical agency GDP vintages and central-bank reserve/external-debt records. Record consolidation, gross/net treatment, valuation and residual maturity before using inputs. Qualitative political/institutional and contingent-liability research must remain explicit in coverage; package consistency does not authenticate it.
