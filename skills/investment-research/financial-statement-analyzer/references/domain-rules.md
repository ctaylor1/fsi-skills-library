# Method and limitations

Margins divide profit by positive revenue. CFO/net income is unavailable for zero or negative net income. Debt/equity and interest coverage need positive denominators; negative operating profit remains a signed signal. FCF = CFO minus positive cash capex. Balance residual = assets minus liabilities minus equity. Growth uses a positive prior value and only equal period kinds with duration difference at most seven days. Sequential quarters are not year-over-year. No values are annualized and no normalization or source extraction occurs.

All analysis is an internal draft from supplied evidence. The common contract withholds results if any declared evidence source fails timing, identity, permission, classification or validity checks. Complete-for-supplied-scope does not prove source authenticity, full population coverage, institutional review or deployment readiness.
