# Owned job and handoffs

Evidence registration and landscape define sourced boundaries; financial forecast/model skills own issuer revenue forecasts. Do not equate SOM with forecast revenue. Currency conversion, nonannual periods and different monetization formulas require a separately supported method, not relabeling inputs.

Retain producer identity/version, original-input hash, source versions, dates, units, permission/audience constraints and unresolved issues. A receiver requires its own prepared native input and reviewed field mapping, followed by actual input validation. Matching names or wrapper hashes do not establish native-schema compatibility or execution authority. Declared handoffs are not automatically launched.
