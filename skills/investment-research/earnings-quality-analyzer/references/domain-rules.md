# Method and limitations

Accruals = net income minus CFO. Accrual ratio = accruals divided by positive mean opening/closing assets. Cash conversion = CFO divided by positive net income. Supplied adjusted income adds all signed after-tax adjustments. Provisional normalized income adds only cited, supplied nonrecurring adjustments with known cash classification; recurring and unknown addbacks stay in review. The screen does not establish actual nonrecurrence, accounting acceptability, fraud, or non-GAAP compliance.

All analysis is an internal draft from supplied evidence. The common contract withholds results if any declared evidence source fails timing, identity, permission, classification or validity checks. Complete-for-supplied-scope does not prove source authenticity, full population coverage, institutional review or deployment readiness.
