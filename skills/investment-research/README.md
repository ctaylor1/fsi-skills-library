# Investment Research

Phase 4: 42 installed packages (42 target methods and 0 retained legacy methods). The broader architecture contains 42 target capabilities; planned methods are listed separately and are not installed.

[Architecture and controls](../../docs/investment-research/README.md) · [Support matrix](../../docs/investment-research/support-matrix.md) · [Migration](../../docs/investment-research/migration.md)

| Installed package | Current role |
|---|---|
| [investment-research-router](investment-research-router/SKILL.md) | Declared-intent routing against supplied installation inventory; no skill execution |
| [research-coverage-planner](research-coverage-planner/SKILL.md) | Plan issuer or universe research questions, required evidence and dependencies; surface critical gaps before initiation, updates or monitoring decisions. |
| [research-universe-screener](research-universe-screener/SKILL.md) | Apply reproducible filters to a supplied security universe with explicit missing, stale and incomparable exclusions; does not retrieve a complete investment universe. |
| [research-evidence-dossier-builder](research-evidence-dossier-builder/SKILL.md) | Single issuer/security supplied-claim register with timing, rights, identity and coverage gaps |
| [company-profile-builder](company-profile-builder/SKILL.md) | Assemble a factual issuer profile from supplied, source-linked facts; withhold mismatched, stale, future, unsourced or restricted facts and return explicit coverage gaps. Use for factual profiles and strip pages, not business-model judgment, recommendations or distribution. |
| [company-business-model-analyzer](company-business-model-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Analyze supplied corporate revenue drivers, segment contribution economics and named customer exposures for an internal business-model research draft. |
| [market-landscape-researcher](market-landscape-researcher/SKILL.md) | Organize supplied industry findings by requested dimensions, retain source provenance and compute concentration only when cited comparable shares are supplied. Use for qualitative or quantitative landscape research; missing or ineligible evidence remains partial, not a recommendation. |
| [market-sizing-builder](market-sizing-builder/SKILL.md) | Calculate supplied annual TAM/SAM/SOM assumptions by explicit top-down and/or bottom-up methods, check dimensions and show scenarios, provenance and reconciliation. Use for bounded market estimates; incomplete coverage or one method stays partial and never becomes security value. |
| [filing-disclosure-change-detector](filing-disclosure-change-detector/SKILL.md) | Compare supplied aligned filing sections and exhibit inventories for cited factual changes, including newly appearing restatement wording. Use for disclosure-change evidence; missing inventories stay not evaluable and no materiality or thesis conclusion is made. |
| [financial-statement-analyzer](financial-statement-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Analyze comparable nonfinancial corporate statements using common-size margins, growth, cash conversion and balance checks; excludes bank and insurer ratio methods. |
| [earnings-quality-analyzer](earnings-quality-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Review corporate accruals, cash realization and signed after-tax earnings adjustments with explicit recurrence and evidence gaps; does not issue an accounting or fraud opinion. |
| [kpi-segment-bridge-builder](kpi-segment-bridge-builder/SKILL.md) | Extract disclosed operational KPIs and build source-linked revenue, margin, volume-price, and segment bridges that decompose the change between two reported periods, with every component tied to a disclosure and reconciled to the reported totals. Use when a research associate asks to "build a revenue or segment bridge", "decompose the change in revenue or margin", "split growth into volume vs price vs FX vs M&A", or "reconcile disclosed KPIs to the reported total". This skill decomposes DISCLOSED, historical results only: it NEVER forecasts or projects any KPI, segment, margin, volume, or price, and it NEVER normalizes accounting judgment beyond approved, versioned mappings (no ad-hoc reclassification, non-GAAP adjustment, or restatement). It issues no investment recommendation, price target, or advice; forecasting routes to the model skills and normalization to financials-normalizer. |
| [three-statement-model-builder](three-statement-model-builder/SKILL.md) | Build a source-linked, integrated three-statement operating model: a driver-based income statement, balance sheet, and cash-flow statement that link so the balance sheet ties every year and cash-flow cash reconciles to the balance sheet, with debt, PP&E, and working-capital schedules and base / upside / downside scenarios — every line re-derived from sourced assumptions and every tie-out checked. Use when an investment-banking or research analyst asks to "build a three-statement model", "project the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow", "link the three statements", or "make the model balance". This skill models only; it NEVER issues an investment recommendation (buy/sell/hold), price target, fair-value verdict, or fairness opinion, NEVER discounts the model to a valuation (that is dcf-modeler's scope), and NEVER gives personalized investment, legal, accounting, or tax advice — those are licensed-human judgments. |
| [financial-model-update-preparer](financial-model-update-preparer/SKILL.md) | Prepare source-linked, version-checked draft updates to supplied three-statement or DCF inputs, with immutable originals, explicit date transitions and a reviewable change ledger; never edit a live model. |
| [research-financial-model-reviewer](research-financial-model-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Independently recompute supplied formula graphs, reconciliations, assumption evidence and economic funding bounds; retain exact legacy roll-forward behavior without approving the model. |
| [dcf-modeler](dcf-modeler/SKILL.md) | Build a source-linked discounted-cash-flow valuation: an explicit driver-based unlevered free-cash-flow forecast, a WACC derived from its stated components, a terminal value (Gordon growth or exit multiple), an enterprise-to-equity bridge to value per share, and base / upside / downside scenarios with full formula tie-outs and reproducibility. Use when an investment-banking or research analyst asks to "build a DCF", "value this company by DCF", "model WACC and terminal value", "walk enterprise value to equity", or wants a scenario DCF with drivers, sensitivities, and model checks. This skill models and explains only; it NEVER issues an investment recommendation (buy/sell/hold), a price target, a fair-value verdict presented as a decision, or a fairness opinion, NEVER guarantees a return or future price, and NEVER gives personalized investment, legal, or tax advice — those are licensed-human / investment-committee judgments. |
| [comps-analysis-builder](comps-analysis-builder/SKILL.md) | Build and refresh a comparable-company (trading comps) analysis: assemble sourced operating metrics, construct enterprise-value bridges, compute trading multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT, P/E on LTM and forward), flag non-meaningful and outlier multiples, compute peer summary statistics, derive an implied-value cross-check range, document peer rationale, and run QA tie-outs — as a source-linked draft for analyst and banker review. Use when an investment-banking or equity-research analyst asks to build, refresh, or QA a comps set, spread peer multiples, compute EV bridges, or produce a market-multiple valuation cross-check with citations. HARD BOUNDARY: draft-only — never issues a recommendation, rating, or price target; never states a valuation or fairness opinion; never fabricates a metric or picks peers outside the versioned criteria; never misuses MNPI; never sends or delivers. It assembles and cites; humans conclude value and deliver. |
| [dividend-residual-income-modeler](dividend-residual-income-modeler/SKILL.md) | Value supplied annual dividend or clean-surplus residual-income forecasts for explicit issuer profiles, including banks and insurers; no corporate DCF fallback or regulatory-capital assurance. |
| [asset-based-valuation-modeler](asset-based-valuation-modeler/SKILL.md) | Calculate a supplied gross asset NAV with sourced haircuts, liabilities, costs, tax leakage and dilution; does not appraise assets or assure completeness. |
| [sum-of-the-parts-valuation-builder](sum-of-the-parts-valuation-builder/SKILL.md) | Reconcile supplied segment enterprise and equity valuations into group equity with explicit debt, cash, corporate-cost and component non-overlap controls. |
| [valuation-reconciliation-analyzer](valuation-reconciliation-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Compare supplied equity valuations on matched dates, bases, units and shares; explain method differences without inventing a blended midpoint or selected value. |
| [scenario-sensitivity-generator](scenario-sensitivity-generator/SKILL.md) | Build scenario, sensitivity, breakeven, and decision-threshold analyses around the most material operating and valuation drivers of a supplied financial model. Use when a banking, research, or investment analyst asks to "flex the assumptions", "run bull/bear cases", "build a sensitivity table", "what margin breaks even", "how high does the multiple have to go", or needs a reproducible driver-by-output data table for a memo, model, or pitch. The skill recomputes outputs deterministically from explicit, cited driver assumptions and ties every number back to a formula. HARD BOUNDARY: it computes mechanics only — it NEVER issues a buy/sell/hold recommendation, a price target presented as advice, or any personalized investment/tax advice, and a human must review before external delivery. |
| [issuer-capital-structure-mapper](issuer-capital-structure-mapper/SKILL.md) | Map supplied issuer debt, maturities, securities, legal estates, guarantees and explicit priority links by currency and unit; retain unknowns and inventory gaps without legal-priority opinions or financing advice. |
| [credit-document-terms-reviewer](credit-document-terms-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Extract cited credit terms and consolidate executed document versions using explicitly reviewed supplied amendment changes; preserve unresolved interpretations without covenant compliance or legal opinions. |
| [issuer-credit-researcher](issuer-credit-researcher/SKILL.md) | Analyze supplied corporate issuer leverage, coverage, cash generation and structural credit risks, preserving reported versus adjusted EBITDA and legal-entity evidence; no rating or credit approval. |
| [issuer-liquidity-refinancing-analyzer](issuer-liquidity-refinancing-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Build dated liquidity and refinancing stress scenarios from unrestricted cash, eligible facilities, cash burn and debt maturities; distinguish committed resources from assumptions without financing recommendations. |
| [covenant-headroom-analyzer](covenant-headroom-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Compute indicative headroom from explicitly reviewed instrument and document-version covenant definitions and sourced components; withhold ambiguous or nonpositive-denominator tests without legal compliance opinions. |
| [credit-relative-value-analyzer](credit-relative-value-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Compare supplied bond spreads with currency, duration, option, rating-group and spread-basis matched peers, excluding stale observations; descriptive differences without investment recommendations. |
| [credit-recovery-scenario-analyzer](credit-recovery-scenario-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Calculate hypothetical recovery waterfalls within supplied legal estates and collateral pools using reviewed priority and deficiency assumptions; preserve collateral caps and avoid invented cross-estate recoveries. |
| [issuer-credit-surveillance-reviewer](issuer-credit-surveillance-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Compare sourced corporate credit ratios, liquidity, spreads and reported events across comparable periods, flagging defined changes without assigning ratings, changing a thesis or recommending a position. |
| [earnings-preview-packet-builder](earnings-preview-packet-builder/SKILL.md) | Prepare pre-event earnings evidence and frozen consensus, whisper, guidance and supplied-model comparisons with fiscal and basis alignment; no invented forecast, actual surprise or investment recommendation. |
| [earnings-results-analyzer](earnings-results-analyzer/SKILL.md) | Analyze reported earnings against separately frozen consensus, whisper, guidance and supplied-model baselines with fiscal, basis, scope and vintage checks; factual surprises without ratings or price targets. |
| [coverage-initiation-researcher](coverage-initiation-researcher/SKILL.md) | Assemble and check a supplied initiating-equity-coverage dossier: cited claims, forecast arrays and valuation-method outputs. Use for an internal draft-readiness packet; it does not execute supporting research/model workflows or approve a rating, target or publication. |
| [investment-thesis-developer](investment-thesis-developer/SKILL.md) | Develop an internal research thesis from supplied variant expectations, dated catalysts, testable falsifiers and counter-evidence; does not issue a trade or rating. |
| [corporate-event-impact-reviewer](corporate-event-impact-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Recompute supplied acquisition, equity raise, repurchase, refinancing or disposal mechanics and funding gaps while preserving announced-versus-completed status; no trade or completion determination. |
| [macroeconomic-researcher](macroeconomic-researcher/SKILL.md) | Analyze supplied macroeconomic releases by eligible vintage, comparable series basis and explicit growth/revision math; keep reported observations separate from forecasts and scenarios. |
| [cross-asset-theme-researcher](cross-asset-theme-researcher/SKILL.md) | Develop testable cross-asset transmission hypotheses and recompute supplied scenario sensitivities with counter-evidence and coverage gaps; no portfolio allocation or trade execution. |
| [sovereign-credit-researcher](sovereign-credit-researcher/SKILL.md) | Research sovereign fiscal debt dynamics, financing and external liquidity using supplied annual scenarios and institutional evidence; no rating or debt-sustainability approval. |
| [public-finance-credit-researcher](public-finance-credit-researcher/SKILL.md) | Research general-obligation or single-system revenue credit using supplied fiscal, pledge, obligation and scenario evidence; no legal pledge opinion, tax determination or credit approval. |
| [structured-credit-researcher](structured-credit-researcher/SKILL.md) | Research a supplied securitization pool and tranche using collateral diagnostics and an explicitly reviewed one-period sequential waterfall. Use for bounded collateral, subordination, default/recovery/prepay and trigger analysis; complex CLO/RMBS waterfalls and tranche pricing require a separate engine. |
| [research-publication-preparation-assistant](research-publication-preparation-assistant/SKILL.md) | Prepare research publication review by checking supplied applicability profiles, disclosure anchors, restricted-list coverage and source distribution permissions; never approve, publish or distribute research. |
| [research-quality-reviewer](research-quality-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Review a structured investment research packet for requested coverage, source timing, claim support, arithmetic, contradictions, risks and disclosures. Use before internal research handoff; this does not approve publication or authenticate evidence. |

Use the router to select explicit intents, methods, owner and installation status. Keep the original supplied input for every output validation. No source authentication, publication or execution authority is provided.
