# Method and limitations

Compare each reported metric independently to every eligible frozen consensus, whisper, guidance midpoint and supplied model. Absolute delta = actual - baseline; surprise fraction = delta / absolute baseline. Direction reverses classification for lower-is-better metrics. Beat/Miss requires effective surprise strictly outside supplied tolerance; zero baseline permits absolute difference but withholds percentage/classification. No blended consensus, vintage substitution, rating or target is invented. Legacy single-baseline packets remain supported without schema_version.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
