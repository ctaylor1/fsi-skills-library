# Method and limitations

Match institution,jurisdiction,intended audience,research type and effective date against the supplied policy profile. Require actual supplied note anchors/text for disclosures and complete per-source intended-distribution permission coverage on use_on; internal research permission is not distribution permission. Missing inventory, stale restricted list or unknown status remains a gap. Ready-for-owner-review is not clearance: actual note text, applicability and licenses remain unauthenticated and nothing is published or distributed. Legacy Phase 1 checklist preparation remains supported without schema_version.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
