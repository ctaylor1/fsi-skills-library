# Implemented method and limits

The native method normalizes supplied text candidates for maturity, coupon, seniority, collateral, call provisions, covenants and baskets. It is not a PDF parser or complete contract review. Simple date/coupon formats, keyword classification and limited USD literal extraction are supported. Guarantee enforceability, intercreditor priority, linked definitions, governing-term consolidation, covenant headroom, breach, waiver, default and recovery opinions remain outside the native method.

Execution-version candidates without amendment indicators may be normalized. Ambiguity markers, negative/qualified/released collateral language, formula/grower baskets, signed coupon expressions, invalid calendar dates and conflicting singleton fields are withheld for review. Keyword thresholds are extraction observations, never a covenant calculation. Simple normalized fields may still require surrounding definitions and legal review.

Load-bearing legal entity, instrument and document version may not be inferred from a display label. A supplied execution label does not prove the document is controlling or complete. Amendment flags withhold values; the method does not consolidate amendments. A normalized security or seniority token is not a legal-priority conclusion.

Economic dates/forecast periods do not establish when information was known. Where this native contract uses an intraday cutoff, source knowledge timestamps require timezones. A source reference and a reproducible hash do not prove content authenticity or licensed use.

Negated or qualified seniority remains needs_review. Floating coupons normalize a supplied positive additive spread in basis points or percentage points (2.50% = 250 bps); missing index or spread remains needs_review. This does not calculate reset coupons, floors, caps or contractual interest.
