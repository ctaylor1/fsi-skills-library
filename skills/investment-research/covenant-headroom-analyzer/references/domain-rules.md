# Method and limitations

Signed component sums form numerator and denominator. For a maximum test ratio headroom = threshold - numerator/denominator; minimum = numerator/denominator - threshold. Numerator-amount headroom = ratio headroom * denominator. Unknown inputs, nonpositive denominators, unreviewed definitions or unestablished applicability withhold all findings. Results are indicative against supplied definitions; cure rights, waivers, basket availability, springing conditions and legal compliance are never inferred.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
