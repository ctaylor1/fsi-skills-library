# Method and supported scope

Value supplied annual dividend or clean-surplus residual-income forecasts for explicit issuer profiles, including banks and insurers; no corporate DCF fallback or regulatory-capital assurance.

The domain helper validates structural, finite-number, basis and method conditions before computation; the separately retained original is required for complete output reconstruction. Supplied economic bounds and source metadata are evidence inputs, not institutional endorsements. Negative equity and failed funding checks remain visible. Professional acceptance remains not established.

Dividend discount equity equals PV dividends plus PV perpetuity. Residual income equity equals opening book plus PV(net income minus required return times opening book) plus PV terminal residual income. Required return must exceed terminal growth; residual income requires clean surplus, explicit zero other equity movements and exact annual book continuity. Neither method proves distributions permissible or bank/insurer capital adequate.
