# Method and limitations

Every declared filter must pass for inclusion. Any failed threshold excludes; an otherwise unresolved record is not evaluable. Compare units, currency, accounting basis, consolidation scope and period exactly. Age is as_of minus observed_on and may not be negative. Preserve every supplied security in one register. Screening is not a ranking, selection recommendation or population-completeness test.

All analysis is an internal draft from supplied evidence. The common contract withholds results if any declared evidence source fails timing, identity, permission, classification or validity checks. Complete-for-supplied-scope does not prove source authenticity, full population coverage, institutional review or deployment readiness.
