# Method and supported scope

Compare supplied equity valuations on matched dates, bases, units and shares; explain method differences without inventing a blended midpoint or selected value.

The domain helper validates structural, finite-number, basis and method conditions before computation; the separately retained original is required for complete output reconstruction. Supplied economic bounds and source metadata are evidence inputs, not institutional endorsements. Negative equity and failed funding checks remain visible. Professional acceptance remains not established.

Pairwise differences are calculated only for common valuation date, accounting basis, consolidation scope and share basis. Currency, units and diluted shares must match explicitly. Non-comparable methods retain their separate values but have no common range or pairwise gap; no forced midpoint or weighting is computed.
