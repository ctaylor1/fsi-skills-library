# Domain rules and current authority

No response is a gap; missing required evidence is needs-evidence; unmapped/temporally unsupported assertions are unsupported-assertion; missing bound approvals are needs-approval. Only structurally complete mapped items with supplied approval records are draft-ready-for-review. Approval in February does not establish policy applicability throughout an earlier January–June period. Section-name lists are JSON template coverage only, not rendered-document QA.

## Sources checked 2026-09-21

[FinCEN FIN-2025-G001, September 5, 2025](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2025-09/Crossborderguidance-508C.pdf) explains SAR confidentiality and treatment of underlying facts and requests for SAR material. It is not blanket permission to disclose a SAR or its decision process in any examination. The regulator, regulated entity, request authority, recipient and applicable disclosure rule must be established for each engagement.

Guidance is distinguished from binding underlying law; illustrative implementation choices are not legal safe harbors. Phase 1 does not certify professional adequacy.
