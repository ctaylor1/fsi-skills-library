# Ownership and handoffs

Specialist evidence producers own substantive conclusions; audit evidence and remediation closure retain their independent owners. SAR drafting is not evidence of SAR filing: obtain filing references from the authorized system. The exam owner must verify regulator/request authority, privilege, SAR disclosure eligibility and final request sufficiency before separately authorized delivery.

Pass the native result and detached original input by authorized references. Verify the producer with full original-input reconstruction before consuming the result. Bind recipient, purpose, evidence scope and authorized decision owner in a separate workflow envelope. Matching hashes do not authenticate evidence, approvals, recipients or legal authority. Missing access or supporting evidence is an explicit incomplete state.
