# Compliance source governance

Source records describe inspected authoritative publications; they are not executable
legal profiles. The current catalog is an initial US/EU/UK and standards reference set,
not complete jurisdiction coverage. Recheck operative text before consequential use.

For each operational rule profile, retain issuing authority, instrument/provision,
official URL, jurisdiction/nexus, covered entities/activities, legal status,
publication/adoption/effective/compliance/transition dates, stays/withdrawals,
knowledge cutoff, version/content digest, owner, review date and unresolved conflicts.
Absent dates remain null. A proposal has no invented commencement date. Guidance,
industry standards, firm policy and illustrative engineering weights remain distinct.

Compliance intelligence maintains sources; qualified legal/domain reviewers accept
interpretations and institutional applicability. Product and control owners accept
implementation mappings. Runtime operators may not silently override binding law with
a firm risk appetite or default threshold. Stricter firm policy is identified as policy.

Monitor volatile sources/list feeds according to approved feed SLAs; record outages and
coverage. Reassess operative rules at relevant case/change dates and on authority
updates. Review static sources on an owner-set cadence and on supersession. A future
review date does not establish current freshness, legal approval or source authenticity.

Use approved exports/adapters where data rights permit. Preserve source publication
dates rather than search-engine crawl dates. Vendor documentation can support a vendor
feature claim, not independent effectiveness or an institution's legal obligation.
