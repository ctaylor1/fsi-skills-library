# Scenario design method

A scenario design is a risk hypothesis and controlled proposal, not a determination of suspicious activity. Exact source-fact mappings support consistency of supplied inputs only. The helper's prerequisite checks require supported risk, scope, mapping, logic, evaluation and change fields; positive window; distinct prior/proposed configuration; nonempty data, parameter and technology requirements; scoped engine observations; no training/holdout identity or backtest/holdout date overlap; and accepted supplied holdout/leakage evidence.

Every data requirement compares supplied expected/loaded distinct counts and exact Decimal amount strings on the declared currency/accounting basis. Unknown quantity is an unknown reconciliation measure and a material unresolved data gate. A mismatch, incompatible basis, unmet freshness, future data evidence or unsupported source binding withholds the configuration proposal. It does not prove a source's completeness when counts match. The native helper does not interpret natural-language logic or execute an engine.

Mapping dataset/namespace/version, graph reference/version and identity limitations are explicit. Ambiguous or conflicting links block readiness. A string link is not a resolved entity or inferred beneficial owner. Population units remain separate; a transaction, economic event, customer and account are not interchangeable. Amount/event aggregation is proposed in the source-bound specification; repeated source documents cannot be assumed additive.

Parameters, reconciliation totals and window values require nonnegative plain decimal strings, without float, exponent or coercion. Parameters retain explicit units/currency. Logic must state inclusivity, event/processing time, timezone, window anchoring, direction, deduplication, missingness and aggregation; these are owner-reviewed semantics rather than executable defaults.

Evaluation definitions and criteria are institution-supplied. The helper checks identity/date/leakage controls, not representativeness, label validity, benchmark transportability or model performance. Backtest and holdout are not performed. Report label limits and bias dimensions, confidence/uncertainty plan, positive/nonalerted reference sampling, candidate sensitivity and operational capacity tradeoffs in the separate narrative when supported. No universal risk weights, regulatory amount defaults or statistical inference is provided.

Engine observations and benchmark references remain separate collections with source/fact locators. At least one scoped engine observation is a proposal prerequisite. Every observation field must have supported exact source-fact evidence; null/conflicted/unmapped scope or observation fields and an observation date after assessment/cutoff/source receipt block the proposal. A benchmark-only packet cannot establish scoped engine support. Technology requirements record supplied versions, review references, support state and limits for real-time, vendor, statistical model, copilot and graph controls. Unreviewed/adverse prerequisites are material blockers; a reference or institutional owner label cannot prove implementation. Real-time/hybrid designs require an explicit real-time control requirement. Other technology types are included when actually used; do not invent vendors or model usage.
