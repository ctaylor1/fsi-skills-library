# Domain rules

Require actual entity/activity applicability and sourced event/awareness chronology. Materiality follows explicit accepted all/any criteria, never universal breach thresholds. An immediate timing obligation becomes urgent human review; do not invent a calendar deadline. Fixed-date duties require sourced deadlines and applicable channel/forms. Draft facts and exhibits only; no submission or legal materiality decision. Source status and effective-date changes remain separate from institutional acceptance.

Supported deterministic method: `entity-activity-event-notification/1.0`. Jurisdiction applicability is supplied and reviewed for the actual institution/entity/activity. Published guidance, supplied facts, analyst inference and human legal decision are separate. Unsupported methods retain unknown; references alone never make a supplied profile applicable.
