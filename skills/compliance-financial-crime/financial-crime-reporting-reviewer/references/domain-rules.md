# Domain rules

Every obligation needs actual applicable entity/activity context, event IDs, type, period, currency, threshold comparison, due-date/channel facts and accepted supplied method. No implicit rules, FX, calendar or legal defaults. Native event membership is supplied; do not treat partial population as complete reportability. Exceptions require an exact obligation-scoped validity and basis claim. Accepted/rejected/pending acknowledgments require receipt, status, obligation identity and date facts. Supplied receipt is not independently verified filing. NonSAR does not replace SAR. No file.

Supported deterministic method: `typed-nonsar-obligation-review/1.0`. Jurisdiction applicability is supplied and reviewed for the actual institution/entity/activity. Published guidance, supplied facts, analyst inference and human legal decision are separate. Unsupported methods retain unknown; references alone never make a supplied profile applicable.
