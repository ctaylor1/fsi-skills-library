# Effectiveness review method

The helper reconciles exact supplied population IDs against expected and received distinct counts, completeness and freshness assertions, data-through/window dates, mapping dataset/namespace/version and ambiguity/conflict status. Missing or inconsistent scope blocks dependent population coverage and sample measures. It retains known engine observations separately even when full population completeness is unknown. Observations outside a known population/window/cohort/scenario/engine version and duplicate item identities stop intake; unsupported fact values remain unknown.

Observed engine counts describe known source-bound detections/non-detections only. `observed_detection_fraction` divides detected by known engine observations, not by the supplied total. `population_engine_observation_coverage` divides eligible observed population identities by reconciled total. Known-observation coverage below one can be reported; absent records are unknown. An event/transaction/account/customer is one declared unit and never automatically another. Graph edges and identifiers remain source assertions, not identity resolution.

The selected sample is a subset of the exact population. Selected labels require accepted source-bound definition/domain/version/review/owner, sample frame/method, independence evidence, reviewed leakage-free status and no overlap with training IDs. Each selected item additionally needs supported same-scope engine observation, positive/negative reference label, label-record reference and review. Unknown/unassessed or missing selected items enter the unknown count; label disagreement or missing record is not a negative. Unselected labels do not enter sample metrics.

TP/FP/FN/TN classify engine observation against the accepted target-pattern reference only. Sample precision is TP/(TP+FP); sample recall is TP/(TP+FN). Both are withheld if any selected item remains unknown, population/label method is incomplete or denominator is zero. All counts retain exact integer denominators. No confidence interval, representative population inference, criminal guilt, SAR conversion or assurance opinion is computed. Sampling and label acceptance statements remain supplied evidence, not authenticated professional acceptance.

Criteria are nonnegative plain decimal strings in fraction units from zero through one, with source-bound owner/review/basis. The comparison multiplies the exact denominator by the exact Decimal threshold with sufficient precision; 12-place half-even display rounding does not drive the result. Unknown criterion evidence withholds comparison but can leave a supported sample metric. A material unresolved technology control requirement also withholds criterion comparison. Unknown operational costs are separate optional unknown metrics.

Operational minutes/cost per completed review use supplied exact strings and same scenario/version/population/cohort/window only. Each quantity has its own source-bound dependencies: unknown cost/currency/accounting withholds cost alone, while supported minutes can survive; unknown minutes withholds time alone while supported cost can survive. Zero completed reviews, missing shared outcomes evidence or incomparable scopes withhold both dependent unit measures. Currency and gross/net accounting remain attached; amounts are not currency-converted. Alert reduction, closure time and unit cost are not detection effectiveness. The caller may describe supported quality/capacity tradeoffs, with separate observations and inferential limits.

Benchmark references retain their own IDs, domain/version/context/claim/limits and source facts. Their data are never pooled with observed engine counts or used as a threshold. Audit/known-positive findings identify bounded examples and cannot prove all false negatives or negative labels. Versioned real-time, vendor, model, copilot and graph requirements are supplied control evidence only; algorithms, extraction, deployment and professional validation remain unbundled.
