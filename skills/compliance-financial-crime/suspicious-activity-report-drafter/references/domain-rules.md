# Domain rules and current authority

Distinguish blocked (no valid supplied drafting authorization), needs-evidence (authorized draft with identified gaps), and ready-for-quality-review (bounded structural checks passed). All are draft states. Partial/blocked packages can validate if faithfully reconstructed and correctly labelled. Ready does not mean approved to file, legally sufficient, or verified true. Use the output template as an optional rendering target; JSON section checks do not verify a rendered document.

## Sources checked 2026-09-21

[FinCEN/interagency FAQs, October 9, 2025](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2025-10/SAR-FAQs-October-2025.pdf) clarify existing institution-specific obligations. Near-threshold deposits alone do not require a structuring SAR. Separate post-SAR reviews are not universally required, and the continuing-activity filing schedule is elective guidance. No-SAR documentation can be institution policy without being a universal BSA requirement.

[FinCEN FIN-2025-G001, September 5, 2025](https://www.fincen.gov/system/files/2025-09/Crossborderguidance-508C.pdf) distinguishes protected SAR existence/nonexistence and decision-process material from potentially shareable underlying facts, subject to other applicable law. It does not alter the section 314(b) safe harbor. Foreign SAR/STR/DAML rules and filing clocks are not implemented here.

Guidance is distinguished from binding underlying law; illustrative implementation choices are not legal safe harbors. Phase 1 does not certify professional adequacy.


## Independent forward-test correction

Activity windows and listed transaction dates must be real, ordered and on/before as_of; future activity produces a chronology break and cannot be ready.
