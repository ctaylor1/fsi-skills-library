# Domain rules

Legacy assembly remains unchanged and residual risk remains Unknown. Use mode=substantive for Phase2. Reconcile exact same-currency claims and corroboration with explicit source-bound tolerance, time and unique evidence identities; no FX, duplicated proceeds or fabricated wealth. Reconciled numbers do not establish lawful origin or plausibility. Evaluate hypotheses/counterevidence and assessed design/operating control evidence. Residual score requires separate accepted edd-residual method, explicit sourced scores/bands, required controls and assessed operating sample evidence; any unresolved issue keeps Unknown.

Supported deterministic method: `edd-reconciliation/1.0`. Jurisdiction applicability is supplied and reviewed for the actual institution/entity/activity. Published guidance, supplied facts, analyst inference and human legal decision are separate. Unsupported methods retain unknown; references alone never make a supplied profile applicable.

Economic-capacity review compares each exact claim amount with a same-currency sourced capacity and its economic activity/basis. Exceeded/unknown capacity is a plausibility gap and blocks residual risk calculation. Within capacity is a bounded observation; human assessment still evaluates lawful origin, causal continuity and counterevidence. Control identities and risk-band labels/required control IDs are source-bound.

## Economic-event identity and additivity

A document is evidence of an economic event, not necessarily an additional event. Each
corroboration record may include `economic_event` as a source-value-mapped claim whose
known value contains `event_id`, `claim_id`, `amount`, `currency`, and `date`. These must
match the resolved claim scope and monetary/date/currency facts. Missing or ambiguous
identity is an evidence gap: do not infer separate receipts from differing document IDs,
fact IDs, descriptions, equal/different amounts, or timestamps.

Each funds/wealth claim also needs source-bound `additivity`: exact `claim_id`, an explicit
supported basis (`distinct_funds_receipts` for SoF or `disjoint_wealth_components` for SoW),
and the exact distinct `event_ids` set. The applicable accepted methodology and supplied
review workpaper must support economic distinctness/nonoverlap. Identity values and a
review workpaper remain supplied-unverified; human review verifies the actual underlying
receipts/assets, lineage and absence of overlap. No generic document-sum method is supported.

Consistent same-ID events with equal monetary amount, currency and date count once; retain
all document IDs and explicitly mark duplicate-document evidence as nonadditional funds.
Conflicting versions of an event are unresolved and withhold the dependent total/difference.
A monetary fact cannot support two claimed distinct economic events. Missing identity,
ambiguous scope, unsupported additivity or provenance collisions withhold total and
reconciliation and prevent residual-risk calculation. Distinct equal-valued events may
be additive only when exact identity and disjointness are separately sourced.
