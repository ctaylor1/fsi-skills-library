# Bounded practitioner method

Bind every criterion and retest to the original finding/version and affected scope. Compare evidence with acceptance criteria and the required operation window. Distinguish designed, implemented, retested and sustained states; a policy rewrite is design evidence only. Count known failures, unknown/untested records and coverage gaps separately. Supplied approver identity/acceptance is retained as unverified evidence, never authenticated authority. Withhold a closure-evidence recommendation when original acceptance, criterion coverage, operating evidence, retest or authority is missing or contradicted.

## Supported scope

The native method reconciles supplied facts using the schema; it does not contain a complete
legal-rule engine. Institutional review criteria must carry exact source-fact references,
version, scope and dates. No legal profile, legal threshold, risk weight, materiality decision
or authority is installed by default. Record unsupported jurisdiction/product/sector profiles
and route contested applicability to Legal. Human narrative must separately distinguish
verified fact, supplied assertion, inference, recommendation and unknown.

## Original finding and operating-evidence detail

Retain the original accepted finding, version, affected scope, root cause, owner and original
closure criteria. Every criterion/evidence record must bind that finding/version and exact scope.
A revised policy, newly approved design or implementation ticket is a separate stage from a
successful retest and sustained operation. The native method requires both supported successful
retest and sustained-operation evidence covering the complete supplied criterion window, with
positive minimum observations and no unresolved failures or coverage gaps.

The human draft tests whether the change addresses the original root cause, whether operating
workpapers represent the affected population and whether failures or residual issues contradict
closure criteria. Do not substitute a different finding, product, control version, sample or
period. Review customer/regulatory remediation separately where applicable. State the remaining
criterion, exact missing evidence, accountable owner and proposed retest scope. A criterion-level
review candidate and a supplied closure-authority assertion do not close the finding; the
authorized closure reviewer adjudicates and records closure in the governed process.
