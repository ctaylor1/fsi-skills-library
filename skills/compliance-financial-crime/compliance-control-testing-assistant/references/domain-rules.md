# Bounded practitioner method

Bind the mandate, period and control objective to the exact population and selected sample IDs. Reconcile selection and observation coverage, detect duplicates/out-of-population records, and apply explicit acceptance criteria to observed results. Report failures and unknown observations separately; never treat untested items as passes. Evaluate supplied independence evidence including operational involvement, reporting/conflicts and mandate owner assessment. A tester role label is insufficient. Probability inference requires a separately accepted sampling method; convenience samples yield observed exception rates only.

## Supported scope

The native method reconciles supplied facts using the schema; it does not contain a complete
legal-rule engine. Institutional review criteria must carry exact source-fact references,
version, scope and dates. No legal profile, legal threshold, risk weight, materiality decision
or authority is installed by default. Record unsupported jurisdiction/product/sector profiles
and route contested applicability to Legal. Human narrative must separately distinguish
verified fact, supplied assertion, inference, recommendation and unknown.

## Mandate and workpaper detail

Identify the accountable mandate owner and exact control/objective, test period, procedure,
population definition, population IDs, selected sample, sampling rationale and acceptance
criterion. Distinguish operational quality checks, second-line compliance monitoring, statutory
independent testing, model validation and internal audit. The native package supports the first
three supplied mandates; model-validation and internal-audit requests retain open routing issues
for their respective owners. No role label proves independence or permits an assurance opinion.

Independence review needs supplied owner assessment, operational involvement, conflicts,
reporting line and competence evidence. The native result only compares those assertions;
professional acceptance/authentication remains outside the package. Reconcile each observed item
to the selected population, exact control/procedure and mandated period. Preserve the observed
record and result; duplicates, out-of-scope items, unknown outcomes and incomplete workpapers
never become passes. Identify observed failed procedures individually with source evidence and
required control-owner investigation, even when a supplied tolerance accepts the sample.

Observed exception rate uses supported completed observations only; unobserved selected items
are separately exposed and withhold acceptance. A judgmental or convenience sample does not
justify population inference. Statistical sample design/confidence and extrapolation require a
separate accepted method; this package does not silently infer them.
