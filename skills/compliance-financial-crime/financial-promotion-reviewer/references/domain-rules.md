# Bounded practitioner method

Freeze the exact content/version and audience/channel/product/perimeter context. Require an explicit reviewed applicability profile; record unsupported profiles rather than infer sector law. Map each consequential claim to its exact source fact; compare risk/benefit balance, required disclosure content, visibility and conditions against supplied rules. Compare approval actor, authority, content version, audience, product and use period separately. Return claim-level remediations and required owner review; historical or draft approval never approves changed content.

## Supported scope

The native method reconciles supplied facts using the schema; it does not contain a complete
legal-rule engine. Institutional review criteria must carry exact source-fact references,
version, scope and dates. No legal profile, legal threshold, risk weight, materiality decision
or authority is installed by default. Record unsupported jurisdiction/product/sector profiles
and route contested applicability to Legal. Human narrative must separately distinguish
verified fact, supplied assertion, inference, recommendation and unknown.

## Cross-sector content review detail

This workflow owns external financial promotion content; sector specialists own their product,
advice, suitability, valuation and surveillance methods. Capture final rendered content for each
channel, exact version, intended/foreseeable audience, product, sector, jurisdiction, proposed
use interval and distribution chain. Legal determines perimeter, exemptions and applicable
sourcebooks. Unsupported profiles remain explicit, including cross-border, insurance, credit,
mortgage, fund, crypto and other overlays without reviewed supplied criteria.

The native profiles `institution-content-review/1.0`, `uk-fca-content-review/1.0` and
`us-finra-member-content-review/1.0` are comparison methods. They require exact source-backed
profile authority/basis and asserted support. They contain no complete sector legal-rule pack;
recognizing the method does not prove legal applicability. The UK method needs jurisdiction UK;
the FINRA method needs jurisdiction US and sector FINRA-member. Context jurisdiction must match.

Inventory each consequential statement, numerical claim, testimonial, ranking, performance
period and implied guarantee. Obtain exact source evidence and review qualifications/conditions,
risk-benefit prominence, required disclosures and audience comprehension. Native textual source
matching is deliberately bounded; semantic and layout sufficiency remain professional review.
Draft edits that identify the specific unsupported wording, supporting source, missing condition
and disclosure location. Compare approval actor/authority, content ID/version, audience, product,
sector, jurisdiction, channel, method profile and exact proposed use interval. Approval scope
consistency remains an unverified comparison; no approval or publication is issued.
