# Bounded practitioner method

Establish the exact allegation and supplied investigation mandate before investigative analysis. Separate alleged conduct, observed records, inference and counterevidence. Build chronology from observed event dates, compare support and contradiction for each hypothesis, and expose incomplete evidence. Draft interview questions and preservation/collection requests within the mandate; Legal/HR determine privilege, employment rights, whistleblower safeguards and permitted collection. No external contact or access is performed.

## Supported scope

The native method reconciles supplied facts using the schema; it does not contain a complete
legal-rule engine. Institutional review criteria must carry exact source-fact references,
version, scope and dates. No legal profile, legal threshold, risk weight, materiality decision
or authority is installed by default. Record unsupported jurisdiction/product/sector profiles
and route contested applicability to Legal. Human narrative must separately distinguish
verified fact, supplied assertion, inference, recommendation and unknown.

## Investigation planning detail

An allegation remains an allegation. Triage its identity, urgency, evidentiary basis and required
owner; proceed to investigation support only with the supplied mandate. Legal/HR decide scope,
conflicts, privilege, employment requirements, whistleblower safeguards, collection rights and
retention holds. Do not promise privilege, anonymity or investigation independence from a label.

Organize the human draft into a chronology with exact dates/source locations, competing
hypotheses, supporting records, contrary records and missing links. Test reasonable alternative
explanations before recommending findings. State which records would distinguish hypotheses and
which interview questions should resolve observed contradictions. Questions should be neutral,
refer to the supplied event/record, and ask about chronology, involvement, authority and possible
alternative explanations. Specify proposed collection/interview owner and permitted evidence
scope; do not contact witnesses or collect/search devices. Native timeline eligibility is a
source/mandate/date consistency check, not proof that testimony or records are true.
