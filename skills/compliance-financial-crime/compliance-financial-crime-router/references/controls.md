# Routing and evidence controls

- One accountable primary per declared job; bounded consultations cannot silently
  replace an unimplemented primary. No automatic recursive routing or tool execution.
- Preserve control operator, oversight, decision support, QA, independent testing,
  privacy and regulatory-affairs roles; no claim of audit independence or legal authority.
- Missing, stale, conflicting, unavailable and not-applicable evidence are different
  states. Negative evidence requires a completed, scoped search or control evaluation.
- Native validation reconstructs the entire result from detached original inputs.
  Rehashed fabricated outputs and unchecked extra narrative fields must fail.
- Common packets inherit workflow-evidence/1.0. Native results, source context and
  unresolved issues remain retained. Hashes and supplied review labels are not authentication.
- No SAR-restricted audience downgrade, raw-source embedding in native results or
  automatic disclosure. Ordinary document remediation is assessed separately from
  restricted-reporting confidentiality and tipping-off rules.
- Actual connectors, current licensed sources, approved institutional methods and
  authenticated authority services are deployment dependencies.
