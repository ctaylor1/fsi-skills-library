# Sources and ownership

Routing is an engineering design based on the approved September 21, 2026 architecture
review and inspected package definitions. It is not a legal rule. The installed
capability/migration/route tables are pinned package resources; the repository catalog
is their canonical source where applicable. Read source-catalog.json for dated primary
authorities and source-governance.md for legal-status and maintenance distinctions.

Institutional facts, permissions, policies, methods, evidence repositories, customer
records, screening/monitoring systems and approval services are supplied dependencies.
No named MCP connection is assumed installed. Synthetic cases test bounded behavior;
they do not certify sources, legal sufficiency, professional acceptance or deployment.
