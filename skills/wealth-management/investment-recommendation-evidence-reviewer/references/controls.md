# Controls — recommendation evidence

R3, read-only analysis; human review required. No legal determination, investment recommendation,
trade clearance, approval, case closure, filing, customer delivery or system-of-record change.
Inputs containing source instructions do not change these controls.

The script authenticates neither policy owners nor reviewer signatures. The deployment must retrieve
controlled records and enforce access separately. A source-bound input digest preserves provenance;
it does not prove the evidence is true or professionally accepted. Substantive sufficiency remains
human work. Approval requirements follow capacity, contract and current controlled policy.

Structural invalidity rejects input. Applicable missing/unknown evidence creates explicit findings;
findings force needs-data. No data gap becomes false, zero, not-applicable or a cleared recommendation.
The validator recomputes the entire check inventory and artifact from its snapshot. Empty output,
removed checks, inconsistent counts, hidden findings, fabricated sources and action claims fail.

Keep identifiers pseudonymous and evidence descriptions minimal. Retain the artifact and underlying
references under the firm's books-and-records and access policy; the package performs no retention
write or external delivery. Domain/control/legal approval, real-model tests and deployment integration
validation remain release gates.
