# Advisory Fee Reviewer — internal draft

Request / household / professional capacity / as of / artifact ID / input hash / reviewer:

## Agreement and billing group

Summarize effective terms, marginal/cliff schedule, household breakpoints, exclusions, date basis and minimum.

## Recalculated charges

Show segment billable assets, annual rates and illustrative charges, period minimum and adjustments, expected versus assessed household fee, and any suppressed amount. Explain rounding and account-allocation limits.

## Exceptions and economic conflicts

For every variance or missing record show evidence, amount, owner, next question and due date. Preserve unresolved conflicts and any upstream issues. No refund or posting decision is implied.

## Evidence and review record

List sources with owner, reference, date and validity; tie material statements and calculations to item citations. Record methodology version, unresolved issues and disposition owner. Human review required; not delivered and no action taken.
