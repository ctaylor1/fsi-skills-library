# Handoffs

- `wealth-client-discovery-assistant`: Collect household spending, income, dependents and client objectives.
- `household-wealth-data-reconciler`: Resolve available-resource and ownership evidence.
- `coverage-gap-analyzer`: Compare the modeled needs with actual policy provisions and exclusions.
- `policy-document-explainer`: Explain existing insurance terms from cited contracts.
- `client-review-preparer`: Package professional-reviewed scenario findings for the household review.

Transmit artifact ID, household/request IDs, engagement capacity, policy version, source registry, substantive result and unresolved findings. These are draft evidence handoffs, not API calls or authority to act. Counsel, licensed professionals and custody operations own their respective decisions.
