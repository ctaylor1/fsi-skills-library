# Client-review controls

R3, draft-only, human review required. No investment recommendation, suitability determination,
trade, case closure, filing, delivery, legal/tax advice or system-of-record/CRM write.

Only draft-review records are packageable. Incomplete/unsupported/stale/unresolved records remain
blocked with reasons. The approved engagement/review policy supplies the human review roles;
requirements are not proof of completed approval. Review and execution remain separate activities.

Validate substantive output against its supplied input snapshot; reject identity/value/disclosure,
provenance and content tampering. A SHA256/artifact ID is evidence lineage, not permission or
source-truth verification. No review-ready assertion based only on a self-reported missing list.

Minimize NPI/PII and restrict snapshots and derived packs to approved storage/recipients. Human
source support review, firm policy ownership, source/integration validation, accessibility and
real-model/domain/compliance acceptance remain pending production gates.
