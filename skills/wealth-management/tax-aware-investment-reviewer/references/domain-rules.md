# Tax-aware investment review method and input contract

Create a tax-lot inventory, a proposed transition illustration, a conservative household replacement-purchase screen, and an asset-location review against supplied policy. Tax terms and equivalence are human supplied. This model cannot determine a legally deductible loss, update tax basis, select substantially identical substitutes, or prepare a tax return.

## Specific fields

- `currency: USD`; `accounts` has `account_id`, `tax_treatment: taxable|tax-deferred|tax-exempt`, `owner_relationship`, `history_complete` boolean, `forward_orders_reviewed` boolean, `history_from`, `history_through` no later than `as_of`, and `source_ids`. Include applicable spouse, IRA, other custodian and reinvestment activity in the sourced scope. `scope_review: {household_scope_complete, source_ids}` is explicit.
- `lots`: nonempty `lot_id`, `account_id`, `security_id`, `asset_class`, positive `quantity`, nonnegative `unit_cost_basis`, `unit_price`, `acquired_on`, `price_as_of`, supplied `tax_term: short|long|unknown`, `source_ids`. Prices and basis are normalized per unit with at most eight decimal places. Terms are not derived from acquisition date; verify holding-period adjustments professionally.
- `proposed_sales`: possibly empty `sale_id`, `lot_id`, positive `quantity`, nonnegative cents `estimated_fees`, `trade_date`, `source_ids`. Aggregate sales cannot exceed a lot and cannot precede acquisition. A historical sale cannot use a quote dated after the sale; input rejects until a contemporaneous pre-sale snapshot is supplied. Historical dates support replay of a sourced pre-sale snapshot; this does not assert the sale occurred. The agent must establish the correct snapshot and chronology.
- `equivalence_map`: `{security_id, group_id, source_ids}` identifies human-reviewed potential substantially identical groups. Missing or uncited mappings remain unknown; never infer equivalence from a ticker or similar name.
- `purchases`: possibly empty `purchase_id`, `account_id`, `security_id`, `acquisition_lot_id`, positive `quantity`, `trade_date`, `lifecycle: executed|proposed|cancelled`, `source_ids`. Executed purchases cannot be future-dated. Record automatic dividend reinvestments and known forward orders as applicable. Acquisition-lot identity prevents treating the disposed lot's own original purchase as replacement shares.
- `tax_profile`: `version`, `owner`, `short_term_rate_pct`, `long_term_rate_pct` in 0..100, `max_positive_gain_amount`, `max_tax_reserve` as nonnegative cents, integer `max_price_age_days` in 0..366, `source_ids`. Rates and budgets are scenario inputs, not current tax-law defaults.
- `location_policy`: `version`, `source_ids`, `preferences: [{asset_class, preferred_tax_treatments, rationale, source_ids}]`; preferred treatments use the account enum. Missing policies are unassessed rather than automatically acceptable.

## Arithmetic and interpretation

Lot value = quantity × price; basis = quantity × unit basis. Net sale proceeds subtract fees; gain/loss subtracts basis on the sold quantity. Only taxable-account positive gains consume the gain budget. Reserve positive gains at the supplied tax-term rate; unknown terms suppress the combined reserve. Losses receive no assumed tax benefit and are reported before wash-sale review. Asset location compares supplied preferences and account treatment; it never instructs a taxable transfer.

For taxable loss sales, screen purchases in the inclusive 30 days before and after the sale using the supplied equivalence groups. Track remaining replacement quantity across loss sales to avoid allocating a purchase twice. The oldest eligible purchase is allocated first, but this is a potential-overlap inventory, not final legal share matching. Preserve account treatment, including IRA exposure, and proposed versus executed lifecycle. Exclude cancelled purchases and the disposed acquisition lot itself. Unmapped securities, incomplete household/history/forward-order review, and an unelapsed future window remain findings even if no overlap is found. A fresh loss candidate is only a review candidate; harvesting requires a separate human decision with current evidence.

The example sells 20 shares at $100 with $10 fees and $80 basis: $390 taxable gain and a $78 illustrative reserve at the supplied 20% rate. Tax-deferred lot losses do not become tax-loss harvesting opportunities. Complex instruments, options, straddles, corporate actions, carryovers, NIIT/state tax aggregation, basis adjustments and final deductibility require specialist analysis beyond this bounded model.

## Common input and evidence

Use the complete synthetic [request](../evals/files/request_example.json) as the serialization example. Required top-level fields are `schema_version: wealth-p0-v1` (the retained envelope version, not the delivery wave), `request_id`, `household_id`, ISO `as_of`, `engagement` with explicit `capacity` (advisory, brokerage, private-bank, or trust), `engagement.jurisdiction: US`, and nonempty `sources`. Each source has unique `source_id`, `reference`, `owner`, `as_of`, `valid_until`; its window must cover the request date. Source identifiers on evidence are `source_ids: [id, ...]`. Source metadata establishes traceability, not truth or authority. Registry date defects reject the request; missing item citations remain findings.

`unresolved_issues` is an explicit list of objects with `code`, `message`, `source_ids`; copy known upstream concerns unchanged, using `[]` only after substantive review. Every input row identifier is a unique nonempty string within its list; referenced accounts/lots must exist. Dates are exact ISO calendar dates. Monetary inputs accept finite decimal strings or JSON numbers, excluding booleans; money has at most two decimal places where specified, quantities/rates at most eight. No FX conversion, missing-to-zero conversion, short positions, or automatic source retrieval is implemented. Input structural validation is necessary but the calculation also performs financial consistency checks.

The output is the shared draft envelope: input hash and artifact ID, scope, sources, findings, status, full `result.input_snapshot`, and calculated evidence. Any finding means `needs-data`; `review-ready` means the structured artifact has no detected gaps, not professional acceptance. Output validation recomputes the entire artifact, including financial values and findings, from that snapshot. An attacker who changes both input and output consistently can produce a new valid draft; hashes are not signatures or an approval record.
