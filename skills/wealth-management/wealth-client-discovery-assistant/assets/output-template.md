# Household discovery fact-find — DRAFT

- Household / request / artifact IDs; as-of date; engagement capacity and scope.
- Status: review-ready intake evidence or needs-data. Human review is required in both cases.

## Household and members
Masked household/member details and documented roles, each with source IDs. Do not imply
verified identity, legal ownership or authority from role labels.

## Documented and client-reported facts
For every fact: subject, area, label, actual value, currency/period where applicable,
evidence status, and source IDs. Show uncertainty and missing values explicitly.

## Client goals
Preserve each stated goal and any supplied priority, amount and date. Leave unknowns unspecified.

## Coverage and information gaps
All eight intake areas with provided / none-reported / not-collected status and cited explanation.
List each unresolved finding, affected item, and question for the advisor/client.

## Evidence and handoff
Source registry, input hash, artifact lineage and next professional review. No identity/KYC
conclusion, recommendation, approval, delivery, or system change has occurred.
