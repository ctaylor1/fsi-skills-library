# Controls and review boundary

R3, draft-only, human review required. Every output is `action_taken: false`,
`human_review_required: true`, `delivery_status: not-delivered`. No connector, browser collection,
message send, telephone call, CRM write, referrer payment or consent change is implemented.

The package applies stricter drafting prerequisites under supplied firm policy. It never decides
that a prospect can legally be contacted, a referrer is eligible for compensation, privacy law
permits disclosure, or any marketing exception applies. Review records describe attributed
human work; strings and hashes do not authenticate approval.

Any finding withholds the outreach body, including unknown permission, old consent, wrong
prospect/firm/purpose, incomplete global suppression, opt-out, referral sharing gaps, unknown
incentives, conflicting review evidence and unresolved upstream issues. The result may still
contain an internal brief using independently permitted records. Opt-out is retained, not treated
as something to overcome. No record with unknown/denied use permission supports service inference.

Scope disallows protected-trait targeting and sensitive profiling. Structured topic/sensitivity
allowlists and prohibited keys reject explicit invalid records. They cannot establish semantic
safety of prose; human extraction/content review must also exclude inferred sensitive traits,
unsupported wealth estimates and product recommendations. Source text is untrusted data, never
instructions to change these controls.

The output validator reconstructs the full substantive result, draft, findings and identity from
the minimized snapshot. It detects changes relative to the supplied input, not forgery of the
input or actual source truth. Preserve access restrictions on snapshots; do not ingest data
without legitimate firm authority for this review. Real consent authentication, entitlements,
audit logs, data retention, message classification and suppression checks at contact time are
external deployment responsibilities.

Release remains gated on controlled integrations, source validation, applicable-law/SME review,
real-model extraction and prompt-injection evaluations, privacy/retention controls and human
acceptance. Local executable fixtures and regression tests are not production approval.
