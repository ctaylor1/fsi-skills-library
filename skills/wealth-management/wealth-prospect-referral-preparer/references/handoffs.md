# Adjacent jobs and handoff contract

- `wealth-client-discovery-assistant`: after an actual authorized interaction, record client facts,
  household goals and scope. Prospect hypotheses are questions, not discovery facts.
- `communications-compliance-reviewer`: review the exact draft under the applicable firm/capacity
  rulebook. Its current registered-principal-oriented package may require an adviser-specific
  jurisdiction/configuration; do not assume FINRA applies to every adviser. Neither skill sends.
- `conflicts-of-interest-reviewer`: investigate referral economics and conflicts using the full
  disclosed arrangement and applicable firm policy, without this package deciding compensation.
- `wealth-onboarding-packager`: only a separately instructed onboarding-evidence job after prospect
  development. This brief does not verify identity, pass KYC or open an account.
- `advisor-follow-up-assistant`: handle follow-up after an actual documented advisor meeting.
  Do not fabricate meeting history or treat an introduction as a client meeting.

Handoffs carry the prospect ID, explicitly provisional/recorded identity basis, household/case ID,
request/artifact IDs, input hash, source registry and permitted-use scope, current channel controls,
referrer authority/economics/conflicts, substantive questions, exact draft (if any), and every
unresolved finding/upstream issue. The artifact is not an authorization token or an automatic
connector call. An owner independently validates the real record and scope before consuming it.
