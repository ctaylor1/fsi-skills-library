# Handoffs

- `wealth-client-discovery-assistant`: Collect missing family facts and intentions.
- `household-wealth-data-reconciler`: Resolve ownership and asset-inventory conflicts before relying on estate comparisons.
- `wealth-account-service-packager`: Package separately authorized, professionally reviewed beneficiary or authority service requests.
- `contract-obligation-extractor`: Extract supported document clauses without legal interpretation.
- `family-wealth-governance-preparer`: Prepare a separately scoped family discussion about sourced intentions, participation, succession and education; family agreement never changes governing-document authority.
- `trust-administration-reviewer`: Review a separately identified trust’s supplied governing obligations, distribution evidence and accounting; estate intentions alone do not establish trustee powers or beneficiary entitlement.
- `client-review-preparer`: Use the cited estate coordination findings in a household review.

Transmit artifact ID, household/request IDs, engagement capacity, policy version, source registry, substantive result and unresolved findings. These are draft evidence handoffs, not API calls or authority to act. Counsel, licensed professionals and custody operations own their respective decisions.

For P3 targets, create the explicit family-group or single-trust request and preserve reviewed identity links and originating issues. A household ID is not a family/trust collection marker. Resolve the authorized audience before including confidential records; a family relationship does not authorize disclosure.
