# Domain rules

## Discussion evidence and authority
Treat a family charter, meeting agreement or discussion role as evidence of a family
process. It does not replace governing trust, estate, account or corporate instruments.
An attributed legal-role assertion remains an assertion for qualified review. Do not
appoint or remove a fiduciary, activate contingent powers, change ownership or decide
whether a document is enforceable. A legal-authority topic always creates a specialist
review finding regardless of voting results.

## Positions, quorum and denominators
Preserve each eligible member's literal statement. Supported dated written or in-person
participation contributes to quorum; an abstention is participation but not support.
An absent, undated or uncited response is not-recorded. Silence never supports a proposal.
Policy defines the method and required quorum. Unanimity requires every eligible member
to support. Majority requires support strictly greater than half of all eligible members.
The program additionally requires complete supported positions before qualifying a
threshold observation. Dissent remains a finding even when a majority threshold is met.
Discussion-only topics have no threshold outcome. None of these observations is a
legal ruling, adopted decision or fabricated consensus.

## Succession and education
Readiness means only that the supplied required preparation criteria have current
attributed evidence, a candidate has expressed dated interest, the role is documented
and a transition target is known. It does not predict performance or evaluate personal
competence. An absent criterion and a not-applicable label cannot bypass the policy.
Legal-role succession always remains legal-review-required. No seniority, age,
gender, wealth, personality, family branch or inferred psychological trait changes a
criterion result. Learning activities use stated needs and participation preferences,
not generation stereotypes. Outcomes are not assessed by a proposed lesson.

## Meeting and work preparation
Use supplied sequence, durations and meeting time. Agenda total is the sum of every
topic and its linked education activity, in minutes. Missing duration is unknown,
not zero; observed known minutes remain a subtotal. Unsupported component or policy
evidence withholds the qualified total. The meeting budget requires a cited meeting,
supported facilitator and attributed willingness of attendees. Overbudget work remains
visible rather than silently dropping topics. A follow-up requires its stated owner,
acceptance and due date; no automatic assignment or completion occurs.

## Audience projection
The internal result and input snapshot retain restricted data for controlled review.
Only the audience_draft projection is a candidate for the specified recipients. For
each included item, every requested recipient must appear in every contributing
record's cited disclosure list. Policy/rule/criterion and related member/role/response
permissions matter as well as topic permissions. Withhold a whole topic if any
contributing record fails this test; do not expose the reason, hidden title, IDs or
counts in the audience view. Dependent succession, education and follow-ups inherit
topic visibility and also require their own supporting-record permissions.

An allowed list is supplied evidence for a draft, not authenticated consent or a legal
privacy exception. The agent must review the actual permission scope and intended use,
then obtain the appropriate separate release decision. Never distribute the full
internal artifact as though filtering one nested view had sanitized it.
