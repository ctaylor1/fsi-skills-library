# Sources and professional orientation — reviewed 2026-09-17

Use current engagement and firm policy to scope the review. Family agreements and
dated meeting records support discussion procedures; attributed individual statements
support participation and positions. Governing legal instruments and qualified review
records support role assertions. Criterion-specific observations support succession
preparation. Source actual learning requests, owner acceptance and completion evidence.
Privacy/consent records must specify who may receive each item and for what purpose.
Preserve conflicting documents and upstream issues rather than choosing one silently.

Every material item cites the registry. Registry dates describe the current evidence
snapshot; historical observations keep their actual dates. Never relabel stale evidence
as fresh. Scope, source metadata and signatures represented as strings cannot prove
identity, authenticity or professional approval. The package has no live source connector.

This skill does not assess capacity. |
| [SEC Regulation S-P amendments](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2024/06/s7-05-23) | Customer-information safeguarding obligations apply to covered institutions. | Deployment/privacy professionals determine applicability and permitted disclosure. This permission-list filter does not implement or certify Regulation S-P compliance. |

These sources orient the boundaries only. No legal eligibility, tax, family-office
exclusion, trust distribution or nationwide governance rule is encoded. Review the
current applicable policy and instruments for the specific engagement. Synthetic
fixture policy and future recertification metadata are not documented firm approval.
