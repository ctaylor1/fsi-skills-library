# R3 draft controls and records

The executable workflow reads local supplied JSON and prints a draft. It has no network call,
system write, scheduler, payment, filing, signature, subscription, beneficiary update or external
delivery. Human review is required in every artifact. No approval or identity authentication is
inferred from strings, source references, hashes, professional titles, relationships or reviewer names.

Validate structure, one-trust scope and dated source metadata first. Economic identity errors,
stale registry evidence and unsupported cash-transfer structures reject with errors. Valid missing
or contradictory data produce a needs-data draft retaining raw observations and sourced questions.
Local component qualifications also require their supporting records; an overall needs-data flag
cannot justify labeling an uncited component complete. Output reconstruction binds all results,
findings, identities, scoped handoff context and no-action flags to the complete supplied snapshot.
It cannot authenticate the underlying source or an entirely self-consistent false replacement input.

The legal reviewer owns applicable law, document precedence, authority, joint action, classification
and entitlement questions. Fiduciary accountants review the actual ledgers; the administrator owns
record completion and operational routing. No person receives another role's authority automatically.
Human findings/resolutions must preserve attribution, dates and citations; no silent case closure.

Trustee, beneficiary and family records can contain confidential third-party information. Limit
access to the approved trust-review team. Before preparing a beneficiary-specific or family-facing
handoff, confirm recipient authorization, purpose and minimum necessary scope through the firm's
controls; this package never distributes it. A family-governance role, beneficiary status, contact
record or household association alone grants no access. Do not leak another beneficiary's request
or personal circumstances into the user's draft for a different audience.

Retain masked IDs and minimum necessary source extracts under deployed privacy/records controls.
The CLI does not configure production retention, deletion, encryption, access or audit logging.
Source access failures require the missing approved source; no invented replacement or unapproved
third-party contact. Professional/legal/SME acceptance, real-model extraction and routing, privacy
review and certified read-only integrations remain release gates; local tests establish none of them.
