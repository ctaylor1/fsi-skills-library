# Evidence sources and applicability

Use approved read-only sources, preserving precise locators, dates, owners and versions. The
source registry describes a currently reviewed evidence packet; original document effective dates,
settlement dates and valuation dates remain separate. All material records need citations; a
valid registry alone cannot support an uncited component. Reviewer and policy fields are attributed
assertions, not authentication. Refresh sources and legal profiles for deployment.

| Source | What the reviewer obtains |
| --- | --- |
| Trust instrument, amendments, orders | Document inventory, exact clauses and effective dates; qualified legal reviewer supplies applicable law and precedence, including contradictions. |
| Appointment/delegation records | Named fiduciaries, actual powers, scope, joint action and current attributed review; a job title is insufficient. |
| Beneficiary records and requests | Actual separately reviewed household links, masked identity, interest description, request intent, purpose support and conflict evidence. |
| Trust/custodian accounting | Independent dated opening/closing cash, gross settled movements, pending status and explicit reviewed principal/income classifications. |
| Asset statements | Cash account references and mutually exclusive noncash holdings, amount/date and source coverage; no inferred current valuations. |
| Review policy | Required authority scopes, packet document types, valuation-age limit, actual professionally extracted obligations and named review owners. |

## Primary orientation checked 2026-09-17

[OCC, Personal Fiduciary Activities, Comptroller's Handbook, February 2015, version 1.0](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/personal-fiduciary-activities/pub-ch-personal-fiduciary.pdf), particularly printed pp. 10–11 and 34–35, discusses separating principal/income accounting,
governing-instrument and applicable-law review, and evidence around discretionary distributions.
The [official OCC landing page](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/files/personal-fiduciary-activities/index-personal-fiduciary-activities.html)
identifies the publication as February 2015. This is bank-supervision orientation in the OCC's
stated supervisory scope, not a rulebook applicable to every trustee. It does not establish this
trust's governing state law, document precedence, entitlement or power to adjust.

The package embeds no universal state trust statute, annual cadence, principal/income rule, tax
filing threshold, distribution percentage or approval requirement. A current qualified human review
must provide the trust-specific applicable-law profile and exact documentary requirements. The
synthetic fixture is not a legal opinion or an institutional policy. The cash scenario and numerical
tolerance are explicit software review conventions, not purported requirements from the OCC.
