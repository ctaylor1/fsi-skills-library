# Sources, freshness and interpretation

Use controlled read-only access, preserving source IDs, locators, owners and dates. Registry validity
means the supplied evidence packet is current for this review; historical settlements retain their
actual dates. It does not mean every NAV inside the packet is current. NAV and professional reviews
have separate domain-date checks. Never replace a missing source with a public webpage or a generated
fact. The numeric fixture policy is synthetic and has no institutional authority.

| Evidence | Required record and review |
| --- | --- |
| Operative commitment / recallability / call allocation | Executed governing documents and amendments, precise notice components, administrator reconciliation to the current operative total; legal interpretation owned by specialists. |
| Notice and settlement lifecycle | Original notice ID/date/due date plus posted custodian/administrator payment or receipt evidence; reconcile partial settlements and cancelled notices. |
| Cash and obligations | Current settled available account cash, protected reserves, separate household obligations and explicit completeness assertions; no assumed transfer or facility availability. |
| NAV | Administrator statement amount and valuation date, professional source review and supplied freshness limit; no pricing inference. |
| Eligibility | Current offering/account-specific legal applicability memo, named required documentary evidence and attributed review with expiry; metadata is not authentication. |
| Forecasts | Attributed dated call forecast with incremental scope, sources and supersession link; no probability or expected-return assumptions. |
| Manager diligence | Shared artifact ID/hash, evidence register and exact unresolved issues; it is consumed rather than reproduced. |

## Primary orientation sources checked 2026-09-17

- [ILPA Capital Call & Distribution Template Guidance, September 2025](https://ilpa.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/ILPA-Suggested-Guidance-2025-Final.pdf), pp. 9, 12, 20–23:
  notices describe cash activity and its effect on LP unfunded commitment; recallable amounts
  increase that balance. Reporting should conform to the governing documents and fund accounting.
  The 2025 template infers inside/outside commitment and recallability from the commitment impact;
  this package keeps explicit classifications as a local extraction aid, not a claim of ILPA schema compliance.
- [SEC, Assessing Accredited Investors under Regulation D](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/assessing-accredited-investors-under-regulation-d), updated April 24, 2026:
  assessment standards differ with the offering route. Investor self-certification alone does not
  satisfy Rule 506(c)'s verification requirement. Use the actual offering’s controlled legal profile;
  the skill neither selects an exemption nor determines eligibility.
- [SEC Investor.gov, Private Placements under Regulation D—Updated Investor Bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins-31):
  private placements can involve restricted resale, illiquidity and limited disclosure. This supports
  preserving liquidity/evidence uncertainty, not treating every private vehicle as legally identical.

Public sources provide orientation only. Refresh applicable sources and controlled legal/policy
profiles for deployment; the package embeds no investor thresholds, legal deadline or call-notice
period. Review horizons and NAV ages are supplied firm choices, not purported regulatory requirements.
The “no credit for unsettled distributions” rule is this package’s conservative cash convention.
