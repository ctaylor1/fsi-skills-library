# Follow-up controls

R3 draft-only; human review required. No sending, CRM/system-of-record writes, trading/staging,
scheduling, case closure, regulatory determination, suitability approval or performance guarantee.
Status fields stay draft, not-delivered and not-written; configured approvals stay pending.

A reviewed communication policy determines required human roles and classification. Neither a
citation string nor the artifact hash is professional approval. Do not replace an absent review
flag with false. Do not invent owner, date, client acceptance, disclosure delivery or source evidence.

The validator checks every material item through the retained source snapshot, substantive section
content, action completeness, reviewed disclosure/recommendation routes and fixed no-action flags.
A needs-data draft may be shown as a gap report but cannot pass the ready-for-review output gate.
Prohibited-language patterns are an extra screen, not proof of compliant real-model behavior.

Minimize NPI/PII; retain the sensitive snapshot, output, versions and corrections only in approved
records/access settings. Source grounding, policy approval, read-only integration tests,
privacy/retention, domain/compliance and independent real-model acceptance remain production gates.
