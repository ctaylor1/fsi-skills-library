# Controls — investment risk profile

R3 read-only decision support, required human review. Never generate an approved assessment,
approval signature, composite score, chosen risk rating, investment recommendation or customer delivery.
Protect the distinct dimensions: willingness does not override inability to bear loss; the software
flags the discrepancy instead of choosing a rating. A source-linked method-approval record is
supplied evidence and is not authenticated by these local scripts.

Require finite typed values and complete item source coverage; preserve missingness and findings.
The output validator recalculates arithmetic and all findings, checks the complete dimension set,
and enforces null assigned_risk_rating and no action. Structural failures reject; valid incomplete
or inconsistent records remain needs-data. An input digest binds content, not authority.

Minimize personal data, use pseudonymous IDs and retain only the structured evidence needed for review.
The firm owns access, retention, records and approval policies. No connector or storage mutation is
bundled. Professional/control-owner acceptance and real-model quality tests remain release gates.
