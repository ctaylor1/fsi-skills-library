# Changelog — investment-risk-profile-reviewer

## [0.1.0] — 2026-09-16
- Added a distinct risk-profile evidence job with six separately sourced dimensions: willingness,
  financial loss capacity, liquidity, horizon, experience and constraints.
- Added documented assessment/method-approval references, optional sourced scenario/lockup review,
  explicit unknowns and conflicts; no composite risk score or assigned risk rating.
- Added wealth-p0-v1 input/output envelope, complete-input digest, deterministic arithmetic and
  full output recomputation. All artifacts are internal, human-review-required and not-delivered.
- Added de-identified complete/incomplete/conflicting fixtures and executable regressions for
  missing sources/method approval, finite types, currency mismatch, conflicts and result tampering.
