# Source map

Use authorized household cash/debt statements, dated lender quotes and executed facility terms, collateral eligibility/encumbrance records and separately reviewed sale/tax illustrations. Preserve conflicts across sources for the responsible adviser/lender; no automatic source precedence is imposed. A source registry date does not replace the actual quote/valuation date or prove ownership.

[FINRA's SBLOC explanation](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/securities-backed-lines-credit) describes non-purpose use restrictions and collateral, floating-rate and demand-loan risks. The lending model therefore separates proceeds, existing debt, reserves and supplied collateral terms and shows explicit stress outcomes. It does not import typical industry advance rates or cure periods as firm rules.

[CFPB's explanation of interest rate and APR](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-loan-interest-rate-and-the-apr-en-733/) distinguishes interest from a broader fee-inclusive credit cost measure. The bundled nominal-rate illustration is expressly not statutory APR. Obtain applicable lender disclosures and specialist analysis when comparing products with different terms or legal treatment.

Primary references checked 2026-09-17. They orient the workflow; they are not substitutes for the actual contract, current jurisdiction/capacity-specific policy, lender underwriting or legal interpretation. All fixture references and assumptions are synthetic, not production policies.
