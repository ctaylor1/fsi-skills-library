# Supported method

Each permitted typed identifier is linked to distinct claim IDs; at least the supplied minimum_shared_claims triggers a review signal. Excluded common identifiers require sourced rationale. One claim in multiple signals is counted once in total flagged exposure. Window rates use complete claimed populations and supplied exposure; counts per day disclose unequal window lengths. Labels require a sourced outcome date no later than review and no earlier than report. Confirmed share uses only confirmed/cleared flagged cases. Links are discovered across both windows at review time, so this is retrospective exploration, not historical alert precision or a predictive model test.

NAIC fraud orientation identifies investigation and referral responsibilities. The configured exact-match screen is an engineering convention, not a fraud detection model or proof of intent. See the primary [orientation source](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/insurance-fraud), reviewed 2026-09-18. These references do not establish adoption, source access or an institution-approved model. Monetary arithmetic uses Decimal. Unknowns remain null; booleans and nonfinite numbers are invalid.
