# Source hierarchy and access

1. Actual issued contract/endorsement, interpretation reviewed by authorized owner,
   applicable market/program/product profile, and covered-event/aggregate scope.
2. Designated verifier's source publication with exact event/location/unit/window,
   version, publication/finality and explicit supersession relationships. Preserve
   outage/fallback evidence; an asserted URL or hash is not authentication.
3. Source-reconciled cumulative net-paid event ledger and explicit completeness.
4. Exposure/operations evidence explaining basis risk and outstanding owner decisions.

Retain source_ref/version/as_of/locator and adverse statements. Do not fill review,
permission or completeness flags from an inventory list. No live verifier connector
or claims/payment access is bundled. Deployment must establish read-only source
identity, rights, freshness, monitored failures and authorized reviewer access.

Primary orientation checked 2026-10-04: [NAIC Parametric Disaster Insurance](https://content.naic.org/insurance-topics/parametric-disaster-insurance),
page last updated 2023-12-21. It describes contract-defined event payments,
contract-specified verification and basis risk. It is explanatory context, not an
adopted contract, current law for every market, or permission to use another verifier.
The native arithmetic and boundary controls are explicit library methods, not NAIC
requirements. Real jurisdiction adoption remains a qualified local review.
