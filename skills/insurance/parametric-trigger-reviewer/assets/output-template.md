# Parametric contract/event memorandum — draft

Case / represented party / owner / jurisdiction and market-program-product / as-of.

## Contract and event evidence

Identify actual contract/version, event/window, metric/unit/location, designated
verifier, finality/revision chain and source locators. Show contrary readings and
outage/fallback basis. Label authenticated facts versus supplied assertions.

## Conditional arithmetic

Show exact threshold operator and tier, event cap, complete aggregate ledger,
current event paid, aggregate remaining and unpaid scenario. Preserve nulls with
reasons. A zero or positive scenario is not a claims decision or payment authority.

## Basis risk and owner review

Explain economic loss versus index amount, remaining contract interpretation,
source/finality gaps, needed evidence and named reviewer decisions. Separate proposed
next steps from completed actions. Retain native JSON alongside this reviewed prose.
