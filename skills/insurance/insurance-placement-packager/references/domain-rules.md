# Method and limits

Version history is immutable input evidence. Selection means choosing which supplied response to review, not selecting a market for the customer. Terms groups require exact agreement on currency, period, layer, coverage basis, covered interests, exclusions, limit and retention. Differences partition groups; matching summaries do not prove contract equivalence. Array order differences conservatively split groups.

The monetary calculation is premium plus separately supplied fees plus separately supplied taxes. It excludes installments, financing, changing taxes, carrier fees not supplied, commissions and FX unless a separately reviewed profile provides reconciled inputs. Open subjectivities are review findings. Absent subjectivity data is a gap; an explicit empty schedule is distinguishable.
