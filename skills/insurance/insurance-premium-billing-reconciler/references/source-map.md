# Native source map

Common top-level fields: case_id, as_of, context{capacity,represented_party,object_id,jurisdiction,line_of_business}, sources[{source_ref,version,as_of?}], explicit unresolved_issues[], optional upstream_artifacts[], and data. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Source IDs must resolve uniquely.

`data`: account_id, currency, basis gross-premium-receivable, unique policy_ids, period{start,end}, ledger_complete, opening_balance and closing_balance {account_id,amount,currency,basis,as_of,source_refs}. Opening is prior-day close; closing is period-end close. `entries`: unique entry_id, kind (invoice/debit-adjustment/credit-adjustment/receipt/refund/writeoff/reversal), nonnegative amount, date, account_id, policy_id, currency, source_refs; invoice adds invoice_id/due_date; reversal adds reverses_id. Signs are fixed by kind. `invoice_inventory_complete`, `opening_invoices`[{invoice_id,policy_id,currency,remaining_amount,due_date,source_refs}], `allocations`[{allocation_id,entry_id,invoice_id,amount,currency,source_refs}]. Opening detail must equal the opening balance. Full in-period reversals only; partial/prior-period reversal needs another reviewed method. Aging supports allocation of receipts, credits and writeoffs; refunds/debits require more detailed invoice treatment. All amounts are in supplied currency units, serialized as exact decimal strings. No rounding or FX convention is invented.

Use controlled policy/PAS, billing/cash, authority and jurisdiction-rule evidence. The scripts fetch no source and do not authenticate it. Locate each asserted fact in the supplied source before mapping JSON.
