# Domain rules — Liability Evidence Matrix

- A relation is usable only when the fact exists uniquely, evidence identity is unique, role is recognized, and source reference/version/quote/relation basis are present.
- Legacy fact-ID arrays without passages produce unverified_legacy_relation gaps. A citation string or document type alone is not proof of a relationship.
- Supporting and contrary evidence -> contested; support alone -> supported; contrary alone -> contradicted; neither -> unsupported. These labels mean supplied evidentiary mapping, not factual truth.
- Contrary, contested, unsupported, dangling and duplicate cases require gaps-for-review. matrix-complete describes a fully supplied supporting map, not legal sufficiency.
- Keep verbatim source statements, including witness assertions about fault. Prohibited-conclusion checks apply to assistant-authored narrative/decisions, not quoted source statements. Output validation independently reconciles evidence/status/counts.
- Source passages and relation rationales are supplied, not independently authenticated or semantically proven by deterministic code.

## Scope limit
No credibility ranking, causation finding, legal elements analysis, fault allocation or legal sufficiency certification.
