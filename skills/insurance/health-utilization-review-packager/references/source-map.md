# Source map and freshness

Primary contextual source reviewed 2026-10-04:
[CMS Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule CMS-0057-F](https://www.cms.gov/newsroom/fact-sheets/cms-interoperability-prior-authorization-final-rule-cms-0057-f)
(fact sheet January 17, 2024). Read its payer definitions, operational/API dates,
drug exclusion and timeframe exception for QHP issuers on FFEs together. These
limits prevent universal timing claims; the article is not a case applicability
opinion or a bundled rule profile.

Case hierarchy: institution-adopted applicable rules and operative benefit/clinical
criteria; source receipt and urgency records; attributed professional observations;
source inventory attestation and contrary evidence; public context last. Require
version, explicit locator, observation date, criterion effective interval and actual
payer/program/product service scope. A source summary cannot substitute for the
underlying applicable criterion. No licensed clinical criteria are bundled.

Distinct institutional profiles are required for commercial fully insured,
self-funded employer, Medicare Advantage, Medicaid managed care and other programs.
Renew the applicability review when scope/rule versions change. Future, unknown or
expired source dates withhold dependent conclusions. Ordinary missing clinical data
is retained as a review question, never invented from public general guidance.
