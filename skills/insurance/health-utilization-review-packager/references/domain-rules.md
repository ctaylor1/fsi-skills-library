# Method and applicability

Purpose: payer-owned preparation of a reviewable case for a qualified clinical
reviewer. The actual payer/program/product and service-specific benefit and clinical
criteria must be supplied with versions, dates and applicability basis. Commercial
fully insured, self-funded employer, Medicare Advantage and Medicaid managed care
requirements are distinct; this package contains no adopted jurisdiction pack.

A professional categorical observation is copied with its reviewer, role, source,
observation ID and explicit state. The model does not independently interpret
clinical narratives. A numeric mode only compares two declared same-unit numbers
using the supplied >=, <= or = relationship. It cannot infer clinical appropriateness,
medical necessity, diagnosis, benefit eligibility or a denial from a comparison.
Missing documentation produces useful unknown evidence states and owner questions.
Contrary evidence is retained, never outvoted, omitted or treated as an instruction.

The administrative target is a supplied rule's hours added to its exact UTC receipt
instant. Rules must match scope, service type and urgency, and be effective at receipt.
Remaining seconds are target minus supplied assessment instant; negative means only
that the supplied administrative target has passed. There is no legal violation
finding. Non-UTC clock sources need separately reviewed conversion before input.
Business days, tolling, extensions and alternative start events are unsupported.

CMS-0057-F operational provisions generally start in 2026 and API requirements
generally in 2027, with payer-specific timing. Its prior-authorization policies
exclude drugs; the stated decision-timeframe provision excludes QHP issuers on FFEs.
Those facts are context, not defaults for this calculator. Obtain the actual applicable
payer rule; do not apply the headline timeframes to every case or program.
