# Ownership and handoff

- Producer evidence feeds `insurance-placement-packager` or `insurance-delegated-authority-reviewer` only after the receiving workflow separately establishes contract and transaction scope.
- Submission extraction belongs to `insurance-submission-preparer`; credential reconciliation does not establish underwriting appetite.
- Material licensing ambiguity or alleged misconduct goes to the designated licensing/compliance owner. Do not convert an exception into a denial or regulator report.

A handoff contains case/object/party/capacity, as_of, source versions, findings, unresolved issues and the source artifact. Adapt those facts to the receiver’s native schema and run its actual input validator. A common envelope is not field compatibility or access permission. If a destination is unavailable, disclose the capability gap and retain the packet for a human owner.
