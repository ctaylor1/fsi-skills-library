# Method and limits

The method evaluates explicit requirements, never infers all required states from a mailing address, and never treats the existence of an NPN or record match as authority. Requirements must cover both individual and entity roles where relevant. Licensing exceptions, residence changes, limited lines, surplus-lines requirements and training obligations need a reviewed specific profile. A name mismatch or multiple matching records remains unknown until reconciled. A known adverse status is retained even though no legal determination is made.

`observed-match` means the supplied record matches the supplied scope at the review date. `observed-exception` means that record exhibits a non-active or out-of-period condition. `unknown` means applicability, identity, provenance or freshness is unresolved. `not-required-by-supplied-profile` must retain its rule source. These are engineering review states, not licensing determinations.
