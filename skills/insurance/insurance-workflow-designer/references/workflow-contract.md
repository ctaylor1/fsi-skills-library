# Workflow evidence contract 1.0

Each draft retains case_id, as_of, context, versioned sources, source_document, unresolved_issues and a content-bound artifact. Context includes capacity, represented_party, object_id, jurisdiction and line_of_business (the actual business segment, not necessarily insurance). Hashes detect content changes; they do not authenticate evidence or grant authority. Native output validation recomputes the method from retained input.

New artifacts use workflow-evidence/1.0. The bundled legacy insurance-contract validator accepts insurance-evidence/1.0 upstream artifacts explicitly, preserving their issues and status. No other contract is implicitly translated. Unclassified input or upstream issues conservatively withhold dependent measures and recommendations; a reviewed context mapping is required before cross-scope calculation. Legacy insurance receivers do not accept new workflow envelopes without a separately reviewed adapter. Cross-object/role/party/line/jurisdiction transitions require exactly one source-bound context_mappings record identifying source_artifact_hash, source_context, target_context, reviewed_by, basis and source_refs.

Use workflow_contract.make_handoff only after mapping real receiver-native fields. It retains the source artifact and unresolved issues. The insurance router's native handoff checker dispatches by contract version, resolves an installed package and runs its actual input validator. Envelope validity is not field compatibility, source access, approval or execution. Outputs always have action_taken=false, approval=not_requested, execution=not_performed and human_review_required=true.
