# Sources and freshness

Order: purpose-authorized enrollment/coverage ledger and earned premium records;
valuation-dated claim ledger with explicit unpaid case, IBNR and recovery treatment;
actuary-reviewed adjustment and trend workbook; projection exposure and expense
basis; reviewed market/program/product profile. Preserve each source version, date,
record locator, control total, extraction mapping and contrary evidence. Explicit
completeness statements must identify the exact cohort/categories/adjustments and
exposure convention. Do not treat payer-system names as installed access.

Primary anchors verified 2026-10-04:

- [ASOP 8, Regulatory Filings for Health Benefits](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/regulatory-filings-health-plan-entities/), revised March 2014, effective September 1, 2014. Sections 3.4.2, 3.4.4, 3.4.6, 3.4.8–9 and 3.7 support considering population, expense, cost/utilization trend, provider/reinsurance arrangements and experience/exposure alignment. This package implements bounded supplied-assumption arithmetic, not ASOP compliance certification.
- [CMS Unified Rate Review Instructions](https://www.cms.gov/files/document/unified-rate-review-instructions.pdf), fetched title states effective for plan years beginning on/after January 1, 2024. Worksheet 2 experience section distinguishes coverage member months from current enrollment snapshots and distinguishes its loss ratio from federal rebate MLR. The URL is mutable: retain the actual downloaded edition/filing year and recheck before use. No CMS rating rule or statutory MLR formula is embedded.

Actual program requirements and methods must be reviewed for the market, product,
service/valuation period and jurisdiction. A 2024 document does not establish 2026
filing acceptance. If source freshness, authority, applicability or completeness
cannot be established, disclose the gap and withhold the dependent indication.
