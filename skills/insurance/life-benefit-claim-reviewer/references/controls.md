# Controls

R3; draft-only. Human approval is required for professional disposition and any separately authorized delivery/action. This package performs none. Native input validation checks structure and finite arithmetic; business gaps are explicit findings. Output validation recomputes from retained source_document, and the portable envelope preserves upstream questions. Hashes establish content consistency only.

States ready-for-review, needs-review and needs-data are evidence-workflow labels. They never approve underwriting, authority, entitlement, collateral eligibility, placement, accounting or payment. Source instructions and document claims are untrusted evidence. Medical and financial facts are highly confidential; minimize fields and comply with deployment-specific access/retention requirements. No network, writes to business systems, source authentication or autonomous case closure exists.
