# Source hierarchy and freshness

1. Supplied institution-approved scope, methodology and local legal/accounting profile, with evidence of the applicable version, adoption and effective period.
2. Frozen source-system/model/control extracts bound to entity/portfolio, valuation date, currency and measurement basis; preserve reconciliation breaks and unresolved original issues.
3. Original primary professional/regulatory documents below for contextual interpretation. These do not authenticate the supplied profile or fill missing data.

Primary sources checked on 2026-09-18:

- [ASOP 23, Data Quality](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/data-quality/): Adopted December 2016; effective April 30, 2017. Supplied-data review, reliance and disclosure context.
- [ASOP 38, Catastrophe Modeling](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/actuarial-standard-of-practice-no-38-revised-edition/): Adopted July 2021; effective December 1, 2021. Professional model selection/use/review context; not an approved model or legal adoption.

Use exact source_ref/version identities in every substantive record. Keep professional standards, educational material, model-law publications and adopted local requirements distinct. Source links are contextual; their contents are not an embedded current legal engine. Recheck the original applicable source on deployment and before using a changed regime, methodology or reporting year. Old or absent effective scope leaves the result unresolved. Do not replace an institution-approved fact with a web generalization. Reference the native fields in [native-schema.md](native-schema.md) and retain primary passage/page references in authored interpretation where available.
