# Native domain method and limits

The supplied reviewed profile chooses accounting basis, period and debit-positive convention. Positive balances are debit amounts; liabilities commonly appear negative. Account normal side is retained for interpretation and does not flip signs again. Both subledger and GL rows bind the same entity, currency, basis and valuation date. A component maps to one reviewed account; every selected account needs an explicit control and subledger amount, including a supplied zero. Duplicate components/accounts, incompatible bases, missing balances or incomplete scope withhold the account reconciliation.

For each account: difference_before = subledger - GL. A proposed journal must have unique identity, at least two unique lines, all accounts in the selected scope, a period-valid date, a supplied reviewer, proposed status and an exact zero sum of signed line amounts. No balancing plug or posting is created. Proposed GL delta is the sum of journal amounts by account. Pro forma GL = GL + proposed delta; difference_after_proposal = subledger - pro forma GL. Any original or residual break is reported for review even if the proposal ties. All draft journals remain unposted.

This reconciles supplied balances and reviews proposed control corrections; it does not calculate earned premium, loss reserves, deferred acquisition costs, reinsurance risk transfer, tax or recognition/measurement under SAP, GAAP or IFRS. A name such as statutory does not establish jurisdictional authority. NAIC SAP describes a national framework that remains subject to state prescribed and permitted practices. The relevant local adopted basis, version and any exception must be supplied and reviewed.
