# Domain rules and bounded method

The bounded model supports ceded paid-loss charges and cash recoverables, not premium settlement statements, incurred/case/IBNR stocks, funds withheld, FX translation or multi-treaty netting. Opening/closing balances and all transaction rows must match the exact scope. Explicit empty arrays mean the supplier asserts no rows for that dataset; missing arrays fail input. Duplicate IDs in one dataset and incompatible scopes invalidate arithmetic rather than selecting a first row. Negative statements/cash are not treated as reversals: use explicitly sourced signed adjustments. A roll-forward can tie while claim-level statement/bordereau breaks persist, so both outputs matter. Required and eligible held collateral are supplied reviewed amounts; no fixed statutory percentage or eligibility opinion is generated. A sent-date alone without a source-backed dispatch record does not prove notice was sent. The notice inventory requires explicit completeness confirmation, including when no notices apply. No notices are emitted.

These implementation rules are engineering constraints. Applicable contracts and reviewed jurisdiction/product profiles control professional interpretation; public primers do not establish universal legal requirements.
