# Source hierarchy and reviewed authorities

Primary sources reviewed 2026-09-18:

- [ASOP 7, Life or Health Cash Flow Analysis](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/life-or-health-cash-flow-analysis/). Adopted December 2025; effective June 1, 2026; sections 3.2-3.6 and 4.1. Asset/liability scope and internally consistent scenarios matter; the static method deliberately omits dynamic options and management actions. This revision excludes P&C practice from its scope.

- [ASOP 23, Data Quality](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/data-quality/), adopted December 2016, effective April 30, 2017, sections 3.2-3.5: evaluate data limitations and disclose reliance; source inventory is not a data audit.
- [ASOP 56, Modeling](https://www.actuarialstandardsboard.org/asops/modeling-3/), adopted December 2019, effective October 1, 2020, sections 3-4: model scope, intended use, validation and communication require professional judgment beyond a passing deterministic regression.

These are professional guidance or educational sources. They do not establish jurisdiction-specific enacted law or approve the package formulas. Obtain the institution's source-scoped method and policy pack, contract/accounting basis and authoritative effective-date version before operational use. Synthetic fixture profiles establish no legal applicability.

Evidence hierarchy: authorized original policy/treaty and adopted institutional basis; source-system population and cashflow extracts with extraction date; reviewed assumptions/curves and model documentation; reconciled derived data; then this draft analysis. Retain source_ref, version, as_of, document title, section/page and extraction notes. Every numeric or categorical row names a registered source_ref; PROFILE must support method, object, product, dates, currency and any supplied tolerance. Source as_of must not postdate review as_of. Sources must be refresh-reviewed on input basis changes or recertification; no universal number of freshness days is invented. The full field contract is in [schema](schema.md).
