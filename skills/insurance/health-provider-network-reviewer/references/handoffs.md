# Triage, investigation and handoffs

Triage network inventory/access review here; investigate only supplied evidence
within the authorized plan and member population. Provider NPI reconciliation may
support identity but must return unresolved credential/license questions to the
credentialing owner. Do not turn a registry match into network enrollment.

Health cohort pricing and trend assumptions go to `health-plan-pricing-reviewer`;
that package routes provider-site contract/access evidence here. Clinical necessity,
benefit coverage, claims edits and payment reviews require their own specialists;
this package neither decides them nor borrows their decision authority. Regulatory
network interpretation and real credentialing decisions remain with named human
network/compliance and credentialing owners. The automation designer may consume
proposed exception handling, but no proposal changes the provider master.

Handoff: case ID, capacity/represented party, plan/market/program/product,
observation date, full provider/site/specialty identities, source versions/locators,
credential and contract issues, population denominator, exact threshold and travel
basis, missing measurements, native artifact and separate authored draft. Preserve
all nulls and open issues. Context changes require reviewed mapping and the receiver
must validate its own mapped input. Passing a supplied threshold does not authorize
certification, external notices, contracting or directory publication.
