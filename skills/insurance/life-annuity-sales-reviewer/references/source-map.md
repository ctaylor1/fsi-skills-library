# Sources and applicability

Reviewed 2026-09-18. Primary sources are orientation anchors; refresh before operational reliance.

- [NAIC Annuity Transactions Model 275](https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-275.pdf), 2020 model, sections 5–7: supports consumer-profile, care, disclosure, conflict, documentation, supervision and training review themes. Actual state adoption, amendments and transaction scope must be established separately.
- [NAIC Replacement Model 613](https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/model-law-613.pdf), sections 2–6 and appendices: supports identifying replacement/financed-purchase questions and comparing existing and proposed benefits and costs; it is not a nationwide enacted requirement.
- [FINRA Rule 2330](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2330): addresses member responsibilities for deferred variable annuities, including specified recommendation and principal-review obligations. Do not generalize its scope or timing to other products.
- [FINRA Rule 2320](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/rulebooks/finra-rules/2320): variable-contract framework; use the applicable current rule text and capacity.
- [SEC Regulation Best Interest materials](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/regulation-best-interest-form-crs-related-interpretations): anchor for broker-dealer retail recommendation standards. A listed reference does not establish that a specific transaction falls within it.

Evidence priority: enacted applicable authority and reviewed legal applicability; executed contracts/current endorsements; authoritative source-system extracts; reviewer-authored summaries; unverified submissions. Retain version, source_ref, observation date and precise page/record/section in source entries. Conflicting sources require reconciliation rather than silently selecting the more favorable one. The tools check supplied references and dates, not authenticity or truth.
