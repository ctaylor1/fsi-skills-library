# Method and limits

The matrices are a reusable evidence scaffold, not a universal statutory minimum or a suitability algorithm. Profiles may add consumer/product topics. The evaluator never aggregates a score or chooses a recommendation. Missing, stale, duplicate or unsupported topic evidence is unresolved; summaries with an explicit zero or false value are preserved as supplied evidence.

The implementation distinguishes permanent/term life, variable life, fixed/fixed-indexed annuities and deferred variable annuities. Annuities add annuitization/rider evidence; life adds premium-obligation/duration evidence. Complex linked benefits, group contracts, qualified-plan exceptions, registered index-linked annuities and other unlisted products require specialist profiles and are not silently treated as supported.

Replacement yes/no needs a source. Replacement unknown remains unknown. Model applicability does not determine enacted law. FINRA 2330 applies only where the deferred-variable-annuity/member scope is established; broker-dealer rule references require corresponding capacity. The code checks consistency of a supplied applicability determination, not its legal correctness.
