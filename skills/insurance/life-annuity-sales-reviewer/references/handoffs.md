# Ownership and handoff

- `insurance-producer-onboarding-reviewer` owns credentials; sales evidence does not establish a license or appointment.
- `policy-document-explainer` explains supplied contract wording; `coverage-gap-analyzer` compares stated prospective needs; neither makes a life/annuity sales-supervision decision.
- Underwriting acceptance and policy servicing use their distinct life workflows. Cross-domain investment, tax, estate and securities questions require the relevant authorized specialist; an insurance review packet does not authorize their execution.

A handoff contains case/object/party/capacity, as_of, source versions, findings, unresolved issues and the source artifact. Adapt those facts to the receiver’s native schema and run its actual input validator. A common envelope is not field compatibility or access permission. If a destination is unavailable, disclose the capability gap and retain the packet for a human owner.
