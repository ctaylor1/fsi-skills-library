# Draft controls and operation

R3; owner: Insurance disability and long-term-care claims. Work only on authorized local evidence and an authorized draft destination. Preserve durable case ID, as_of, represented party and professional capacity. Minimize NPI/PII and health/investigation details; use internal evidence locators rather than copying unnecessary sensitive material. No data is uploaded by these scripts.

The native method is `continuous-calendar-daily-indemnity-offset`. Unknown sources, stale scope, contradictory bases, duplicate identity, role mismatch and missing required inputs remain unresolved. Review existing output status and findings together with results; a local calculation cannot remove inherited issues. Source hashes indicate content consistency, not authentication.

Human reviewers own adjudication, legal/clinical interpretation, communications, filing, posting and payment. These offline scripts cannot execute them, and an output must always retain approval not_requested, execution not_performed and action_taken false. A user asking to send or approve this draft requires a separate authorized workflow with its own review; this package never silently broadens capacity.

States: ready-for-review, needs-review, needs-data, not-applicable. Do not close a case from a passing test or draft status. Retain source snapshot/version, profile, native artifact, authored report and review disposition under the institution's configured access and retention policy. Do not invent a retention duration.

Before deployment record qualified domain/control/legal acceptance, evidence access and retention owners, review SLO and escalation owner. Monitor wrong routing, missingness, unresolved breaks and human corrections. Roll back to the prior reviewed package/profile on regressions; suspend unsupported/stale methods until recertified. No professional or production acceptance has been recorded by this build.
