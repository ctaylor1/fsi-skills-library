# Native method and limits

The current gross-direct position separates paid indemnity, paid expense, outstanding indemnity and outstanding expense. Reported incurred indemnity = paid indemnity + outstanding indemnity; the same identity applies separately to expenses. A scenario supplies additional unpaid indemnity and expenses. Projected gross total = paid + scenario future amounts, never paid + case + future, because case reserves are an existing estimate of the same unpaid obligation. Proposed case change = scenario future − current outstanding case amount. Recoveries remain explicit, then net scenario total = projected gross indemnity + projected gross expense − expected recovery.

Every scenario preserves its assumptions and currency. Unknown amounts remain null; malformed, boolean and nonfinite values reject. Unknown recovery is not zero. The helper does not choose a scenario, establish coverage or liability, decide damages, apply policy limits/deductibles automatically, allocate expenses inside/outside limits, determine collectibility or post accounting entries. The reviewer must supply the intended scenario after examining those issues and document current valuation, gross/net basis and timing. This is claim-level scenario arithmetic, not actuarial IBNR or an adequacy opinion.
