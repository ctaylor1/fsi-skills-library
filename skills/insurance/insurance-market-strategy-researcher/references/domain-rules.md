# Supported method

Market concentration is 10000 times the sum of squared fractional shares, only for a complete inventory reconciling to a positive market total. Unknown observations stay null. Keep fact, inference and hypothesis labels, implications, contrary sources and further evidence explicit. Do not infer comparable pricing or legal market power from HHI.

NAIC competition database report explains scoped market shares and HHI. The code implements only supplied-data concentration; strategy remains an authored evidence synthesis. See the primary [orientation source](https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/publication-cdr-im-competition-database-report.pdf), reviewed 2026-09-18. These references do not establish adoption, source access or an institution-approved model. Monetary arithmetic uses Decimal. Unknowns remain null; booleans and nonfinite numbers are invalid.
