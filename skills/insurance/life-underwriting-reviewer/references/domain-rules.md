# Domain rules and bounded method

The engine compares explicit structured disclosures literally; an agent must explain ambiguities and cannot turn literal equality into medical agreement. Evidence requirements must expressly cover medical and financial kinds; no universal list or income multiple is encoded. A missing, stale, disputed, future, duplicated or wrong-applicant document does not satisfy a requirement. Disclosure conflicts require investigation, not automatic declination. Disclosed income is not authenticated by this tool, and a ratio is not a needs recommendation. No accelerated-underwriting model, medical diagnosis, risk class, price or eligibility decision is implemented.

These implementation rules are engineering constraints. Applicable contracts and reviewed jurisdiction/product profiles control professional interpretation; public primers do not establish universal legal requirements.
