# Handoffs

- `stablecoin-payment-controls-reviewer`: separately supplied active participant/jurisdiction policy_profile and role-specific control evidence. Assessment JSON is not its native input; retain gaps and validate conversion.
- `payment-liquidity-and-funding-analyzer`: dated fiat account, obligation, redemption and access windows by legal entity/currency.
- `payment-initiation-control-reviewer`: a later separately requested payment intent and native control review; no assessment authorizes submission.
- `regulatory-change-impact-analyzer`: current source text and jurisdiction/actor questions; legal owners decide applicability.

Carry original sources and unresolved issues. Never call issuer, custody or legal review complete because a holder-side cost comparison is complete.
