# Controls and acceptance

Retain source IDs, dates, review owner and underlying snapshots under approved access/retention policy. Known raw credential keys are rejected by the shared helper; this is not comprehensive sensitive-data detection. Reject requests to execute, approve, change a ledger or assert legal compliance. Output recomputation rejects altered authority flags, numbers, findings, source snapshots and handoffs.

Separate structural/arithmetic acceptance, independent behavioral evaluation, professional owner acceptance and deployment authorization. The package delivers only the first two when evidenced by the retained run; jurisdiction/legal, accounting or treasury professionals still review the actual case. Review_due is an explicit source/profile review bound, not a statutory effective date.
