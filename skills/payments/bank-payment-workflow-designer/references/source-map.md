# Source basis and freshness

Reviewed 2026-09-18. Production decisions require current institution contracts, procedures, policy and participant-specific evidence; public descriptions establish scheme orientation only. Source registry `as_of` and optional `review_due` dates are preserved; overdue evidence blocks reliance. Profiles with overdue review dates require refresh. Offline provenance is supplied, not cryptographically authenticated.

Primary orientation sources:

- [Federal Reserve FedNow participants and services](https://www.frbservices.org/financial-services/fednow/organizations): participant/service access and 24x7 operation; this is not proof that an individual account is reachable or funded.
- [EPC SEPA direct debit](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/what-we-do/sepa-direct-debit) and [mandate description](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/what-we-do/epc-payment-schemes/sepa-direct-debit/sdd-mandate): distinct Core/B2B roles and mandate requirement. Do not infer a B2B mandate from Core evidence or encode one universal refund clock.
- [Pay.UK Faster Payments operation](https://www.wearepay.uk/what-we-do/payment-systems/faster-payment-system/how-faster-payments-work/): sent payments cannot simply be cancelled; customer access and recovery procedures remain separate from rail operation.

These public descriptions support boundary design, not full current operating rules. The bundled native rail registry supplies additional per-rail source references. Licensed rulebooks, account/product contracts, consumer law and national overlays remain deployment inputs. Fixture permissions, costs, balances, limits and deadlines are illustrative, never universal scheme facts.
