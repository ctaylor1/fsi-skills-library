# Draft corridor design

- Actual use case, origin/destination, exact parties/accounts and accountable reviewer.
- Each delivery option: principal, oriented dated FX quote, itemized fee/tax inventory, payer debit and expected receipt in their own currencies, delivery review and comparability gaps.
- Separate customer collection, correspondent, payout and nostro funding obligations with unique economic identities.
- Current jurisdiction/activity review and full intermediary screening scope, unresolved evidence and next owner.
- Separate native funding/initiation handoffs and explicit unsupported adapters.
- Evidence snapshot/hash and false authority/action flags. Arithmetic, model behavior, professional acceptance and deployment gates stated separately.
