# Corridor rules

Keep customer transfer intent, each intermediary obligation, the nostro funding chain and local payout separate. A correspondent message or account debit does not prove beneficiary receipt. No equivalent USD group total or legal netting is inferred from these records.

Compare sender debit and recipient receipt as a pair for the same principal and parties, retaining the supplied delivery review. Do not rank an option with unknown intermediary charges, expired FX, mismatched payout, unresolved local permissions or missing operational evidence. Scenario arithmetic does not create a disclosure compliant with any law.

Use only a current firm FX quote to call an option comparison-ready. Evaluate aware timestamps as instants in UTC, not lexicographic local strings. Public profiles do not establish a participant's reach, price, liquidity or compliance. Same nominal method names across jurisdictions do not create one interoperable rail.

A source-bound handoff is an evidence carrier. Transform it into the destination's native contract and validate the exact data separately. Preserve every gap; unsupported downstream jurisdictions remain unsupported.
