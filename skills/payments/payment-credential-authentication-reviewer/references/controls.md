# Controls

R3, offline read-only decision support; no credential operation or payment is executed.
Human owners review credential authority, exact intent and applicable rules. Never
infer regulated clearance from a complete result. Output tampering or original-input
mismatch fails recomputation. Upstream issues stay in downstream handoffs.

Only opaque references belong in inputs and output. The shared key rejection is a
bounded guard against common credential-field names, not general DLP. Inspect source
extraction for token values, cryptographic assertions, secrets and biometric material.
