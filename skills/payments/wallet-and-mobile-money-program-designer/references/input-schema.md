# Wallet native input

Envelope: schema_version 1.0, request_id, as_of (ISO date), jurisdiction US|EA|UK|KE, participant, sources with source_id/reference/as_of/review_due, upstream_issues and data. Only analyze/draft actions. Every row has registered source_ids and a unique first identifier. Amounts are nonnegative finite decimal strings or integers; null is unknown. One request is one complete opening-to-as-of interval supplied by the caller; no event clock or historical reconstruction is inferred.

- model: credential-pass-through|stored-value|e-money|mobile-money. ledger_complete boolean.
- entities: entity_id, role, legal_name. roles: role_id, role, entity_id, responsibility_ref. Required roles are operator, holder, merchant, funding-provider, support-owner, plus issuer for stored value; mobile-money also cash-agent and agent-supervision.
- positions: position_id, issuer_entity_id, currency, opening_liability and reported_liability (required, nullable). Exactly one group per issuer/currency.
- events: event_id, economic_id, position_id, type issue|redeem|internal-payment, state settled|pending|failed|unknown, holder_ref, currency, amount. One canonical outcome per economic identity. Settled issue requires funding_state settled, exact funding_amount/currency and unique funding_receipt_ref. Settled redeem requires unique external_payout_ref. Internal-payment requires payee_claim_ref and preserves total liability. Pending redemptions remain owed; pending issuance is not issued liability.
- backing: backing_id, account_ref, position_id, owner_entity_id, beneficiary_issuer_entity_id, ownership_review_ref, currency, eligibility_review_ref, amount (required, nullable). No reuse of a backing account across positions. beneficiary_issuer_entity_id is the issuer liability book backed by the evidence, not a finding that the issuer is the legal beneficiary of customer trust assets. Trustee ownership and holder rights must be explicit; do not substitute operator capital for reviewed backing.
- program_pack: jurisdiction, country, owner, provider_ref, version, review_due, source_ids and review references: licensing_review_ref, access_review_ref, consumer_error_ref, aml_review_ref, support_ref, funding_ref, redemption_ref, reconciliation_ref. Mobile money adds agent_liquidity_ref, agent_due_diligence_ref, cash_reconciliation_ref. Kenya adds trust_arrangement_ref, cbk_authorization_ref, agent_supervision_ref, redemption_contract_ref, kenya_profile_review_ref; country KE and position currency KES only. EA requires a named euro-area member country and national review, not generic EU authorization.
- channels: explicit list of objects, type qr-merchant-presented|qr-consumer-presented|app|cash-agent, participant_access_ref, fraud_support_ref and source_ids. QR also requires qr_profile_ref, interoperability_test_ref, payee_binding_ref.

References are assertions from the supplied evidence, not independently authenticated confirmations. A pass-through model uses empty positions/events/backing; any stored value must be a separate request. Output liability_reconciliation separates derived liability, reported difference, backing surplus/shortfall and pending redemptions. No unlike-currency total, regulatory capital computation, live screening or cash-agent payout authorization is produced.
