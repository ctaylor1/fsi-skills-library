# Program controls

Map custody, legal claim and operations separately. Do not net liabilities across issuers or currencies, double-use backing accounts or funding receipts, reduce claims on a pending redemption, or count an internal purchase as extinguished program liability. Unknown events contaminate the group rather than becoming zero. Negative positions and shortfalls require owner review. Separate cash-agent cash from e-money float and trust backing; supplied agent-review references do not prove solvency or real-time liquidity.

Country/provider approval, data identity, AML/sanctions checks, safeguarding method, consumer servicing, QR interoperability and permissions require their accountable owners. Use approved retention and opaque references. No raw credentials, execution, secrets, live balances or signature validation are supported.
