# Commercial-card optional native schema

Use common schema_version 1.0 envelope with sources, as_of, jurisdiction US|EA|UK, participant and upstream_issues. No execution action. This is a separate mode from the invoice/bank-remittance core. Rows carry unique first IDs and registered source_ids. Amounts are nonnegative decimal strings or integers; null is unknown.

- program_type: virtual|purchasing|fleet|corporate; history_complete boolean; buyer_entity_id, issuer_ref, program_contract_ref, provider_control_review_ref.
- credentials[]: credential_id, owner_entity_id, supplier_ref, currency, valid_from, valid_until (timezone-aware ISO timestamps; start inclusive/end exclusive), usage_mode one-time|limited, max_uses positive integer (one-time=1), transaction_limit and total_limit (required nullable), allowed_mccs nonempty four-digit strings, provider_enforcement_ref, source_ids. No PAN or raw token. A supplier match is required independently of MCC match.
- invoices[]: invoice_id, invoice_identity_ref (unique economic document identity), buyer_entity_id, supplier_ref, currency, remittance_ref, open_amount, source_ids. open_amount is outstanding obligation immediately before the supplied canonical payment history, not an already reduced current ledger amount.
- attempts[]: payment_id, economic_id (unique across all attempts), credential_id, invoice_id, supplier_ref, currency, mcc, occurred_at (timezone-aware), state proposed|authorization-held|pending|settled|failed|unknown, remittance_ref, amount (required nullable), source_ids. Settled needs settlement_ref. Virtual/purchasing needs purchase_order_ref; fleet needs vehicle_ref and driver_or_fleet_policy_ref; corporate needs expense_policy_ref.

Represent one canonical current state per economic payment; duplicate auth/clearing records must be resolved before review. Failed entries consume neither uses nor budget; held and pending consume conservative commitments but never paid amounts. Unknown outcomes suppress credential and invoice availability. Historical outcomes after the UTC as_of day are rejected as evidence gaps; proposed future dates may be reviewed inside the supplied credential window. This does not implement provider clock precision or approval expiration.

Output attempt_reviews binds each row locally but never authorizes payment. credential_budgets include prior settled, held and pending exposure plus proposed use; invoice_positions count only evidenced settled payment as paid. Per-attempt counts_as_settled_payment never declares the whole invoice paid. Count/amount excesses are gaps. Ledger, issuer controls and remittance references remain unauthenticated; local matches do not prove network acceptance, settled bank cash or paid supplier acknowledgment.
