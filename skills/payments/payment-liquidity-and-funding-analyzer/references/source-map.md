# Source hierarchy and freshness

Primary orientation sources reviewed 2026-09-18:
- [Federal Reserve FedNow participant resources](https://www.frbservices.org/financial-services/fednow/organizations): settlement participants, settlement agents and liquidity providers.
- [Federal Reserve FedNow operating hours](https://www.frbservices.org/resources/financial-services/fednow/operating-hours): customer processing and liquidity-management windows are separate concepts; retrieve the current window for any operating plan.
- [Federal Reserve FedNow liquidity-transfer guide](https://explore.fednow.org/resources/fednow-liquidity-management-transfers-guide.pdf): master, correspondent and joint-account transfer context; orientation, not a grant of service access.
- [The Clearing House RTP document library](https://www.theclearinghouse.org/payment-systems/rtp/document-library): prefunded settlement models, funding-agent model and current/upcoming rules. On review, rules effective 2026-06-01 were current; upcoming 2026-09-30 and 2026-10-04 versions require renewed review.

Caller-owned account statements, funding agreements, source availability, institution calendars and actual access windows control the numerical scenario. The bundled rail profiles retain their individual source dates/review due dates. A failed lookup or expired profile does not imply current permission or 24-hour access. Recheck source applicability at the window date; the framework does not grant overdraft or funding-line rights.

Record effective/as-of dates, version, owner and review due dates. Current source identity is not authenticated by local scripts. Source dates after the request are rejected; overdue review dates produce gaps. Missing sources produce needs-evidence. Resolve conflicts with source owners instead of selecting the convenient value. Require specialist review before relying on an institution, national or scheme interpretation. Recertification is a package governance date, not a guarantee that mutable rules remain current until then.
