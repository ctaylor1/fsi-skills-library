# Adjacent work and handoff artifact

- Regulatory applicability: `payment-regulatory-applicability-reviewer`; send the
  proposition, legal entities, territories, customer segment, feature/funds model,
  source inventory and unresolved questions. This is not a legal determination.
- Credit-policy and underwriting evidence: `credit-application-packager`; retain
  issuer/program requirements while banking owns credit judgment.
- Provider selection: `payment-provider-evaluator`; compare roles and contract
  requirements without treating selection as program approval.
- Projected business economics: `payment-economics-modeler`; observed merchant cost
  belongs to `merchant-payment-cost-analyzer` and is a different input/output job.
- Existing card state or transaction lifecycle: `card-lifecycle-operations-assistant`.
- Provisioning, account updater and authentication evidence:
  `payment-credential-authentication-reviewer`; retain program roles, opaque token
  domains, consent and exact instruction bindings. Program design grants no authority.

- Supplier payment controls for virtual, purchasing, fleet and corporate cards:
  `commercial-payment-workflow-designer` commercial-card mode; send exact supplier,
  invoice/remittance, opaque credential limits, canonical held/pending/settled history
  and issuer enforcement evidence. Product credit and underwriting remain adjacent.

Handoffs carry target, reason, required native inputs and source IDs. They are work
proposals, not completed validation or an automatic pipeline. Verify the target is
installed and validate its own native schema. The shared payment contract governs
any later transport envelope and preserves unknowns and lack of authority.
