# Source map

Reviewed 2026-09-18. Sources below orient the design, not substitute for issuer,
network, sponsor or local legal agreements. Register the actual agreements and
approved policies used in each request, with review owner/effective dates.

- [OCC Credit Card Lending](https://www.occ.gov/topics/supervision-and-examination/credit/retail-credit/credit-card-lending.html): bank credit-card risk and program context; limited to its stated supervisory scope.
- [CFPB Regulation Z, section 1026.12](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/12/): card issuance and feature-specific provisions; commercial status must not be used as a universal exemption.
- [FCA safeguarding requirements](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/emi-payment-institutions-safeguarding-requirements): current payment/e-money customer-funds context; applicability depends on the actual institution and service.
- [FCA key publications](https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/emi-payment-institutions-key-publications): use the current final Approach Document and applicable rule dates, not older or draft versions.
- [EBA outsourcing guidelines resource](https://www.eba.europa.eu/activities/single-rulebook/regulatory-activities/internal-governance/guidelines-third-party-risk-management?version=2019): governance context; distinguish applicable, future and superseded documents and ICT/non-ICT scopes.

No network fee, consumer-error deadline, issuer permission or deposit-protection
claim is hard-coded. For those decisions obtain current applicable primary rules
and institution agreements through authorized distribution.
