# Source map

Verified orientation on 2026-09-17: [CFPB Regulation E, 12 CFR 1005.11](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/11/) for applicable consumer EFT errors; [CFPB Regulation Z, 12 CFR 1026.13](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/) for applicable credit billing errors. These regimes differ in notices, timing, triggers and remedies. They are not generic card-network response windows. Other products/jurisdictions require separate reviewed profiles. Case-system notices, statement/transaction records, customer allegations, credit and communication records provide case evidence. Synthetic fixture dates are supplied examples, not statutory calculations.

Source references are supplied pointers. The offline helper checks declared metadata and does not retrieve, authenticate or inspect the referenced content. Record limitations rather than upgrading the evidence status.
