# Domain rules

A statutory profile extends the shared contract with `regime`, `applicability_rationale`, `products`, `rails`, `required_clock_names`, `required_evidence_types`, and `required_action_reviews`. Each statutory clock supplies `name`, `source_ref`, `trigger_event`, `trigger_date`, `trigger_source_ref`, `due_date`, and `day_basis` (calendar-days/business-days/explicit-date); business-days also require `calendar_ref`. Due dates are reviewer-supplied; the script only compares dates and does not calculate statutory periods. Action reviews are objects keyed by the profile names, each with `review_ref`. Complete production profiles require qualified legal review; a fixture is not a complete legal profile. Scheme expiry cannot remove consumer rights or justify a customer denial. Firm policies cannot override law.

`processing_date`, cases with `case_id`, `role`, jurisdiction, rail, product, transaction identity, explicit amount/currency and source reference. Issuer cases supply a shared-contract `statutory_profile`, `statutory_clocks`, evidence and `statutory_action_reviews`. Optional scheme profile/clocks are separate. See [domain-rules.md](references/domain-rules.md) for the exact additional fields.
