# Incident consequence boundaries

The unique-payment inventory is the customer denominator; retry attempts are service load.
Amounts attach once to the payment's legal entity/currency. Successful API responses or
authorization alone cannot demonstrate settlement/funding. Failed and unknown amounts
are consequence/exposure categories, not realized financial losses. Conflicting payment
identity excludes the row and prevents a complete population claim.

A source-linked release/status timeline suggests hypotheses, not causality. Record
contradictory observations and the test that could distinguish competing explanations.
Technical recovery is separate from reconciliation of uncertain money. Recovery inventory
must partition every unique payment once and retain unknown outcomes as unresolved.
No blind resend/new key for timeouts. A no-effect record and same request identity are
necessary evidence for a separate authorized review, never permission to replay here.
