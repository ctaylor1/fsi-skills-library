# Sources

Use applicable enacted law/current consolidated rules first, regulator guidance
second, then reviewed institutional interpretation and actual entity agreements.
Public summaries do not establish a firm's permissions or exemptions. Record scope,
effective dates and review ownership; authenticated retrieval remains a deployment task.

Reviewed 2026-09-18:
- FinCEN MSB registration: https://www.fincen.gov/resources/money-services-business-msb-registration
- CFPB Reg E: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1005/11/
- CFPB Reg Z: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/13/
- PSD2: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2015/2366/oj/eng
- Instant Payments Regulation: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/886/oj/eng
- FCA payment/e-money scope: https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/payment-services-regulations-e-money-regulations
- FCA current safeguarding: https://www.fca.org.uk/firms/emi-payment-institutions-safeguarding-requirements
- FCA payment fraud: https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/fraudulent-payments

Only concise orientation is bundled. Obtain licensed scheme rules through authorized
institutional access; do not publish licensed manuals or treat proposals as active law.
