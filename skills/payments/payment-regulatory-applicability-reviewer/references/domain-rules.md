# Applicability distinctions

U.S. MSB/BSA review, state money-transmitter licensing and bank responsibilities are
separate. A FinCEN registration does not grant a national money-transmitter license.
Consumer EFT and consumer open-end-credit billing errors have different processes;
commercial payments, excluded wires, prepaid and remittances need precise product scope.

Euro-area review uses the PSD2 framework plus actual national transposition and
provider/customer/territorial scope. Instant euro-transfer requirements have phased
entity and location applicability. The package does not assume every PSP owes the
same requirement on the same date.

UK payment/e-money permissions and safeguarding must be reviewed against the
current FCA regime, including CASS/SUP where applicable. Safeguarding is different
from deposit insurance. Authorized APP scams differ from unauthorized payments.

The matrix is candidate obligations and evidence needs, not a definitive legal
opinion. Bundled orientation is bounded to the 2026-09-18 review and 2026-10-18
refresh date; older or later as-of requests return unresolved profile coverage.
