# Handoffs

- `payment-economics-modeler`: method/participant-specific economics, source costs, period/currency and all unresolved accounting issues.
- `merchant-payment-cost-analyzer`: incurred merchant processing costs with required source statements and classification evidence.
- `regulatory-change-impact-analyzer`: source rule changes affecting the actual company operating role and jurisdiction.

Company research collection and professional accounting judgment remain dependencies; this package does not scrape, authenticate filings, value securities or place trades. Retain evidence, case identity and unresolved issues; downstream native inputs must be constructed and validated separately.
