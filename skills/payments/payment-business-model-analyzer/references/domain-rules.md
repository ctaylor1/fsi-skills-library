# Strategy and accounting rules

Map roles, value delivered, paying customer, monetization and dependencies before interpreting margins. Payment volume is not revenue; gross merchant fees can include interchange/scheme/partner pass-through. Gross/net classification requires the company's actual accounting analysis and source policy. The local bridge is a management convention, not a new accounting standard.

Cost completeness is explicit. Unknown is not zero; refunds, rebates, pricing incentives, chargeback/fraud exposure, variable partner costs and allocated overhead require correct disjoint classification. Incentives cannot appear both as revenue reduction and variable cost. A supplied net revenue number is not reduced twice. Isolate period/currency/perimeter and retain negative contribution. Avoid total plus components; the helper checks declared overlap but a professional mapper must verify underlying partitions.

Competitor comparison requires aligned definitions and accounting review. Product mix, geographies, acquisitions and principal/agent choices can create different take rates without different underlying competitiveness. Arithmetic delta is not causal explanation. Form testable options and evidence needs; do not turn observed margins into unsupported market-share, regulatory moat, future earnings or securities claims.
