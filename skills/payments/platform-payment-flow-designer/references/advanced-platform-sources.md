[Stripe separate charges and transfers](https://docs.stripe.com/connect/separate-charges-and-transfers) is a concrete provider example showing that a platform charge and connected-account transfers can be separate objects; refunds do not automatically reverse related transfers. This is provider-specific evidence, not a universal PayFac, merchant-of-record or legal ownership rule. Provider scope, contracts, current API version and liability arrangements require a supplied pack.

Hierarchical conservation, distinct economic segment IDs, leaf-only consumption and exhaustive entity/currency batch partitioning are library design controls. They do not infer processor available balances, cross-border permissions, trust, ownership or recovery rights. Route licensing to payment-regulatory-applicability-reviewer and exact instructions to payment-initiation-control-reviewer. Public provider documentation does not establish those native inputs or confer authority.
