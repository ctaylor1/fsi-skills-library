# Source hierarchy and freshness

Actual signed processor/acquirer, seller and customer contracts govern roles, liabilities, fee splits and reserve treatment. Account evidence identifies legal account owner separately from economic beneficiary. Processor charge/payout ledgers and reconciliation evidence outrank presentation decks. A term such as marketplace, PayFac or merchant-of-record is a supplied arrangement requiring legal and sponsor review; this package makes no automatic legal classification.

All sample names and values are synthetic. No licensed scheme rule text is distributed. Use current institution-approved contract versions and national legal advice in regulatory handoffs.

Record effective/as-of dates, version, owner and review due dates. Current source identity is not authenticated by local scripts. Source dates after the request are rejected; overdue review dates produce gaps. Missing sources produce needs-evidence. Resolve conflicts with source owners instead of selecting the convenient value. Require specialist review before relying on an institution, national or scheme interpretation. Recertification is a package governance date, not a guarantee that mutable rules remain current until then.
