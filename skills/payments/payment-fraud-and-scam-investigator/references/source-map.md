# Source map

Case-management records anchor case state and supplied IDs. Fraud platforms supply signals, KYC/identity systems supply identity evidence, payment/beneficiary/ledger systems supply transaction facts, and customer/contact records supply deception allegations. The package reads JSON exports; connectors, entity resolution and retrieval are not implemented. Use current approved reimbursement/legal profiles in the issuer workflow; do not infer legal rights from the risk score.

Source references are supplied pointers. The offline helper checks declared metadata and does not retrieve, authenticate or inspect the referenced content. Record limitations rather than upgrading the evidence status.
