# Adjacent work and handoff artifact

- Integration evidence acceptance: `payment-integration-readiness-reviewer`; provide
  the selected obligations, inspected test evidence, reconciliation results and
  accountable reviewers. Architecture prose is not acceptance evidence.
- Bank operating flow: `bank-payment-workflow-designer`; resolve operational rails,
  participant responsibilities and settlement/finality requirements.
- Merchant acceptance design: `merchant-acceptance-strategy-designer`; resolve the
  acceptance proposition before choosing acquiring/routing components.
- Provider evaluation: `payment-provider-evaluator`; retain architecture constraints
  and supplied contract evidence, with no automatic procurement action.
- Card operations state: `card-lifecycle-operations-assistant`; send normalized
  issuer events and profiles, not broad architecture assumptions.

Use shared cyber, resilience and enterprise-architecture reviewers for specialist
questions through the deployment's installed inventory. Phase 3 adds scoped incident and active-active artifact review; it does not certify deployment. Handoffs are proposed
work items with target, requested evidence and source lineage; validate native inputs
and retain unresolved issues before any independently authorized action.

Use `payment-service-incident-investigator` for service-level consequences and
`payment-operations-improvement-analyzer` for workload and bounded automation business cases.
