# Initiation controls

Authentication answers an identity question; intent must bind the exact instruction. An account-holder login, API key, possession of credentials, provider access contract or request-for-payment receipt does not authorize a particular amount or beneficiary.

Consent, mandate and delegated-authority scope must cover accounts, payee, currency, amount, rail, permitted recurrence and execution date. Revocation remains a current evidence requirement. A debit mandate is not an unlimited payment authority. Policy limits and screening results must be applicable to the same proposed snapshot. No universal amount or fraud threshold is embedded.

Batch controls compare Decimal amounts by currency and source count. Duplicate IDs, payment-detail fingerprints or prior submissions hold the review, including a reused instruction under a new ID. Intentional repeated payments need a separately adjudicated new instruction; this helper cannot approve a duplicate override.

Human approval and operational enforcement remain distinct. A drafted finding or supplied `authorized: true` value is not an authenticated approval, and no output grants submission permission.
