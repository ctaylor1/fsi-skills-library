# Sources and refresh policy

Version 0.1.0; reference review date 2026-09-18. Primary evidence for any live use must come from the merchant/product owner, executed provider terms and current product scope, inspected test/operational evidence, loss/outcome data with maturity definitions, and documented assumptions. Distinguish an executed obligation, inspected result, current official product statement, proposal and assumption. The helper consumes supplied evidence; it has no source retrieval or provider database.

Public orientation (not pricing, reach, eligibility or legal-rule defaults):

- [Federal Reserve, Pay-by-Bank and the Merchant Payments Use Case, 7 July 2025](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/pay-by-bank-and-the-merchant-payments-use-case-benefits-20250707.html). Supports separating merchant, bank and customer perspectives, adoption/displacement and multiple fees; blanket savings estimates need care. No rates or savings figures are imported into the model.
- [BIS/CPMI, Improving access to payment systems for cross-border payments, 12 May 2022](https://www.bis.org/publications/improving-access-payment-systems-cross-border-payments-best-practices-self-assessments). Supports distinguishing direct access, intermediated access and participant constraints. Does not establish a named provider's eligibility or today's access terms.

Refresh official scope/terms for the actual jurisdiction, participant, channel, currency and as-of date before a live comparison. Review date does not imply legal applicability or current certification. Attach source excerpts and exact clause/test references to the institution's controlled evidence record; register only identifiers and permitted references here. Any unsupported legal/provider fact must remain a question rather than being filled from the synthetic example.
