# Observed cost rules

Sum supplied settled-sale volume and each fee category in one explicit currency. All-in total exists only with an explicit fixed-fee list. Effective rate is total fees / settled-sale volume × 10,000. Implied processor rate excludes fixed fees. Do not sum rates or currencies. No default markup, downgrade-recovery percentage, Level 2/3 savings, low-band factor or annualized savings is applied. Missing qualification inputs remain unknown.

New pricing scenarios require participant-specific contracts, effective dates, eligibility, mutually exclusive cohorts and explicit assumptions; that business-case capability is not implemented here.
