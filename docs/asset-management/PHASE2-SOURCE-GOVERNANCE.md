# Phase 2 source and method governance

Reviewed September 21, 2026. Package `references/source-map.md` files retain the relevant
primary URLs and explain their relationship to the local method. They are methodological
orientation, not authenticated feeds, adopted legal rules or permission grants.

- Institutional policy/allocation references: CFA Institute institutional IPS and asset-allocation material. The local implementation reviews supplied objectives, constraints, covariance and candidates; no recommended allocation is inferred.
- Performance references: GIPS calculation guidance and ILPA performance/reporting material. Bounded TWR, Modified Dietz, conventional dated XIRR and supplied private-fund multiples do not certify compliance or reporting acceptability.
- Private-capital references: ILPA model terms and capital-call/distribution guidance. Contract features and preferred entitlements must be supplied explicitly; unsupported catch-up, deal-by-deal and accrual methods are withheld for an accepted engine.
- Market/operating references: relevant SEC, DTCC and FCA primary materials. Supplied dealing cutoffs, corporate actions, AP terms, service contracts and reviewed rule packs remain deployment-specific facts.
- US tax and disclosure references: IRS Publication 550 and SEC investment-adviser marketing materials. Selected-lot/window and presentation checks remain bounded internal reviews; they do not decide deductions, legal applicability or approval to publish.
- Systematic research references: primary methodological sources and CFTC hypothetical-performance guidance. Supplied split, knowledge-time, reuse and cost controls do not validate an investment strategy or create a backtest engine.

Every native request declares object, currency, economic date, knowledge cutoff and
internal-review audience. Sources carry unique identifiers, versions, as-of and known-at
dates. Native schemas require relevant row references; a nonempty unrelated source
registry cannot substitute for provenance. Expired, contradictory or missing evidence
is rejected or produces explicit withheld results according to the package method.
Explicit audience restrictions cannot be bypassed by changing result labels. Actual
observations and completed events cannot occur after the review date, knowledge cutoff
or their linked source known-at date. Prospective deadlines, maturities and scenario
periods remain separately declared planning inputs; they are not completed observations.

Business narrative rows additionally match the exact approved source claim. Disclosure
sources declaring an object or currency cannot silently support another fund. Numeric
inputs are declared supplied observations or modeling assumptions, not independently
extracted facts; source references and reconstruction cannot authenticate those values.

Record independent benchmarks and institutional acceptance outside the native draft.
Change a canonical shared method deliberately, update its version/lock as appropriate,
regenerate affected bundles and replay relevant tests. The initial unreleased Phase 2
resource versions are 1.0; package implementations start at 0.1.0 and expanded packages
retain their own documented version history.
