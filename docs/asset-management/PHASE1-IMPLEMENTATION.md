# Asset Management Phase 1 — 2026-09-20

Phase 1 implements the approved structural and correctness foundation. It does not
implement the entire 64-capability target portfolio. The repository now contains
25 AM packages, 18 Investment Research packages and nine Investment Banking packages;
two shared investment data utilities sit in Data, AI & Model Governance. Across the
library, the two mergers and new AM router produce 302 packages in 15 categories.

## Delivered behavior

- A declared-intent router covers 24 representative request classes, distinguishes
  installed/unknown availability from supported/planned methods, reports partial
  institutional diligence and RFP scope, and never launches work or grants authority.
- All 30 original AM packages have one disposition and accountable owner. Neutral
  research has a dedicated category, transaction advisory remains in Investment
  Banking, and original names/paths resolve through an explicit migration registry.
- Quality checks and vendor comparisons share one entrypoint with separate modes;
  proxy evidence and policy checks share another. Native methods and schema boundaries
  are preserved. Aliases return the necessary mode; they do not convert inputs.
- Complete-input hashing and detached reconstruction reject altered native outputs,
  fabricated extra fields and mismatched original inputs. Common handoffs revalidate
  the original producer and the real receiver, retain uncertainty and require reviewed
  mappings for every receiver field and context change.
- Shared source is canonical and package resources are generated with pinned versions,
  hashes and license records. Affected packages can run their declared native checks
  in isolated temporary installations without sibling skill imports.
- The engine contract separates bounded local analysis from specialist engines and
  institutional records. An unavailable engine remains an explicit gap; no silent
  simplified fallback or external engine integration is added.

## Native repairs and their limits

| Area | Corrected behavior | Remaining boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Mandates | Applicable rules, absent minimum buckets, pre-trade limits, worsening breaches, dates and stable incidents | Supported supplied rules; no universal legal engine |
| Exposure and risk | Authoritative supplied NAV, complete dimensional tables, partial look-through, covariance checks and coverage gaps | Bounded holdings/sensitivity/volatility methods; no full repricing, VaR or ES claim |
| Liquidity and cash | Missing inputs remain unknown; daily deficits survive wider reporting buckets; date shifts and scenarios validated | ADV proxies and supplied cash assumptions; no execution or institutional liquidity certification |
| Reconciliation and NAV controls | Decimal strings, compatible bases, neutral correction alternatives, stale evidence, unknown FX and explicit residuals | Tie-out and challenge calculations; no book authority, posting or NAV strike |
| Research data | Declared source rights, ruleset binding, original-record provenance, missing prices, temporal observations and conflicting reference data | No authenticated connector, license grant or inferred source truth |
| Credit and investment review | Negated/draft/amended terms remain uncertain; exact maturity windows; scenario and hard-limit gaps retained | Bounded document normalization, issuer snapshots and private-deal IC schema |
| Service and stewardship | Explicit DDQ approval, fact identity/share-class checks, restricted-data exclusion, evidence-bound commentary, entitlement and applicable policy | Separate professional review and publication/vote approval; no outward action |

The independent forward review found additional precision and envelope-validation
defects after initial regressions passed. Original failures, exact evidence and repair
replays are retained in the [validation record](phase1-validation/README.md). Passing
reconstruction alone is not an independent numerical or source-fidelity benchmark.

## Acceptance and remaining work

See the [validation record](phase1-validation/README.md) for exact commands, results,
independent cases and limitations. The [support matrix](SUPPORT-MATRIX.md) states each
implemented method's scope and every planned capability. The common contract supports
22 native package adapters / 24 explicit schemas or modes; six packages remain
native-only until their identity/time schemas have reviewed adapters.

Institutional manager diligence, full performance measurement and attribution,
production fund accounting, optimization, advanced risk/pricing, point-in-time data
services, authenticated entitlements, additional jurisdictions and the broader business
workflows remain future work. Phases 2–4 are recorded in the [plan](IMPLEMENTATION-PLAN.md).
Practitioner acceptance, independent institutional benchmarks and deployment controls
are separate gates. Neither technical checks nor this implementation authorize trading,
posting, publication, a release, a merge to main or a portfolio version bump.

The pre-existing Insurance Phase 4 work is preserved. Its protected tracked files and
prior catalog additions/overrides are checked independently of this AM change.
