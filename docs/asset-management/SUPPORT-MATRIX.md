Phase4 implements all64 AM packages with the bounded scopes below; package presence does not establish complete professional coverage.

`bounded-native` means documented offline methods are implemented; `implemented-router` means declared-intent route planning; `planned` means unavailable. All entries remain draft-only, without authenticated external access or professional acceptance. A renamed package does not gain the broader methods implied by its future role.
