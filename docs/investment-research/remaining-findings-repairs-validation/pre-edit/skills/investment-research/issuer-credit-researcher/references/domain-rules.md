# Method and limitations

Reported EBITDA remains distinct from adjusted EBITDA. Adjusted EBITDA equals
reported EBITDA plus each reconciled signed economic event once. A source-bound
event ledger establishes the supplied canonical identity, financial context,
version and additive treatment. One event total or its complete nonoverlapping
components may contribute, with matching selected evidence. Renaming row IDs,
changing corroborating document/locator, repeating totals, overlapping components,
or leaving identities unresolved cannot increase adjusted EBITDA. Equal amounts
for distinct explicitly evidenced events remain additive.

Contractual principal is not accounting gross debt. Reconcile the explicitly
included, comparable supplied principal inventory through an evidenced signed
principal-to-carrying-value bridge to reported gross debt. A cited zero bridge
states equality when applicable. An excluded legal instrument remains available
for structural risk review without being automatically consolidated. All target
entity, scope, currency, unit, basis and period assertions remain source-bound
supplied evidence. Foreign-currency translation and inferred consolidations are
unsupported. Coverage declarations are not independent completeness assurance.

Event-component and debt tieouts use exact rational arithmetic on supplied finite
JSON decimal values; no caller tolerance or floating-point epsilon can authorize
a mismatch. No external source authentication, real-world event-key verification,
legal or institutional accounting acceptance is claimed.

Gross leverage = reconciled reported gross debt / positive EBITDA. Net leverage =
(reconciled gross debt - unrestricted cash) / positive EBITDA. Interest coverage =
EBITDA / positive interest expense. FCF = operating cash flow - capex. FFO/debt
requires positive reconciled reported debt. Event reconciliation gaps withhold
adjusted EBITDA and all adjusted ratios, retaining reported EBITDA. Debt
reconciliation gaps withhold gross/net leverage and FFO/debt while retaining
independently supported EBITDA, interest coverage and FCF. Reasons and original
evidence remain inspectable. Ineligible common sources withhold the full result
projection instead.

Only a reported financial period ending no later than economic as-of and the
knowledge-cutoff date can support reported ratios. A future financial period
rejects even when its source is labeled available. Separate issuer obligations
without an explicit subject guarantee are structural review flags, not legal
subordination determinations. Unknown seniority is unresolved. Financial
institutions and agency rating methodologies are unsupported; no credit decision,
rating, publication, trade or system-of-record change is made.
