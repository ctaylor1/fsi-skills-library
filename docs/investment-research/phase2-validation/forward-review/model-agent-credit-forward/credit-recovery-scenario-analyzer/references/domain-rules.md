# Method and limitations

Deduct costs from unencumbered assets only. Within each collateral pool allocate by collateral_priority; ties share pro rata to collateral_cap. Release pool surplus only when explicitly supplied. Deficiency claims enter the same-estate general pool only when eligibility is explicitly true; unknown eligibility withholds that estate general waterfall. General claims allocate by supplied priority, ties pro rata to remaining claims. Never transfer cash, collateral or guarantees across estates. A cost shortfall does not invade collateral without a supplied reviewed legal allocation. Values are hypothetical recoveries, not legal forecasts or investment recommendations.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
