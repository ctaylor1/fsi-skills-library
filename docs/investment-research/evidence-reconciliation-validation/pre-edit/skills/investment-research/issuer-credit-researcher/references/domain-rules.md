# Method and limitations

Reported EBITDA and analyst-adjusted EBITDA are separate: adjusted = reported + signed supplied adjustments. Gross leverage = debt / positive EBITDA; net leverage = (debt - unrestricted cash) / positive EBITDA; interest coverage = EBITDA / positive interest. FCF = operating cash flow - capex; FFO/debt requires positive debt. Separate issuer obligations without an explicit subject guarantee are structural review flags, not a determination of subordination. Financial institutions and agency methodologies are unsupported.

Only a reported financial period ending no later than the economic as-of date
and the knowledge-cutoff date can support these ratios. A future period is an
invalid intake, even if a source is labeled available; source eligibility is a
separate control. EBITDA adjustment IDs must be unique before summation. Each
distinct supplied adjustment is applied once with its signed amount; reported
EBITDA remains unchanged.

Software checks establish finite arithmetic and exact reconstruction only. Domain, legal and institutional acceptance remain not established.
