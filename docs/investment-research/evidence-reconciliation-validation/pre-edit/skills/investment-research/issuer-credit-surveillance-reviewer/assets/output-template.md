# Corporate issuer surveillance — draft rendering

Render from the validated immutable native packet; keep the original input and input_hash separately. This template does not extend method coverage or grant delivery authority. Never invent source facts or replace null values with zero.

Header: legal issuer_id/name, economic as_of/prior_as_of, knowledge_cutoff, financial currency/units/period/accounting basis, packet/config versions, support_status and unresolved gates.

## Issuer summary <!-- issuer_summary -->
Report the supplied corporate research scope and headline observations. No complete issuer credit, rating, thesis or portfolio conclusion is formed.

## Leverage <!-- leverage -->
Gross/net debt-to-EBITDA; withhold for nonpositive EBITDA. Preserve source and comparable basis.

## Coverage <!-- coverage -->
Interest coverage and the (EBITDA − capex) / interest expense proxy. The latter is not contractual FCCR or DSCR. Zero denominators are unknown, not infinite or zero coverage.

## Liquidity <!-- liquidity -->
Separate nominal cash/revolver from available amounts based on supplied accessibility/availability assertions. Restricted/conditional funding stays unavailable for the comparison. Compare only eligible funding with complete dated scheduled maturities, explicitly excluding operating uses and refinancing feasibility. Full liquidity sufficiency remains unassessed.

## Maturity wall <!-- maturity_wall -->
Preserve instrument_id, legal issuer_id, currency, amount, exact date and source. Distinguish complete totals, known subtotals, missing dates and past-due amounts.

## Spreads <!-- spreads -->
Report the identified instrument, spread_type, currency, current/prior supplied observation and delta. This is not an issuer rating, pricing model or relative-value conclusion.

## Ratings <!-- ratings -->
Report the named agency and issuer/instrument rated object. No rating is assigned. Source eligibility is a caller assertion, not source authentication.

## Covenant changes <!-- covenant_changes -->
Preserve instrument/document/version and source. Flag supplied changes for terms review; no consolidation, headroom, compliance, waiver or default opinion.

## Citations and evidence <!-- citations_and_evidence -->
List source references, types, economic dates and knowledge vintages; preserve missing coverage and review gates. Source_truth remains unverified. A faithful PARTIAL packet can pass reconstruction while its data/review gates stay held.

Review appendix: supplied preparer/reviewer records and differences from prior evidence. Distinct role/person labels do not authenticate people or authorize external delivery. Actual review, entitlements, MNPI controls and any delivery remain institution-owned.
