# Sources and method provenance

The example is synthetic. Actual event terms, status, balance dates, financing,
security identity and source rights must be supplied with versioned precise
citations. No data subscription or source-authentication service is bundled.

The financial bridges are explicit cash/debt/share identities documented in
`domain-rules.md`; they are a bounded research method, not a complete accounting
standard or legal determination. Primary reference material checked 2026-10-20:

- [SEC Form 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/about/forms/form8-k.pdf), Items 1.01 and
  2.01 distinguish entering an agreement from completion of an acquisition or
  disposition. The method preserves that status distinction without adopting a
  jurisdiction-wide legal conclusion or filing deadline rule.
- [IFRS IAS 7 standard overview](https://www.ifrs.org/issued-standards/list-of-standards/ias-7-statement-of-cash-flows/)
  and [IAS 7 supporting material](https://www.ifrs.org/supporting-implementation/supporting-materials-by-ifrs-standards/ias-7/)
  provide the financing/cash-flow context. The package does not decide formal
  statement classification, accounting recognition or disclosure sufficiency.

No third-party text or external filing is included as test evidence. Formula
verification uses independently specified synthetic numerical cases.
