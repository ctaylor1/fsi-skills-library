All 36 target workflow rows have bounded native methods. X1 is the retained
transitional scenario evaluator, X2 the adjacent privacy job, and Z1 the router.
CFC contains 38 packages including its router; privacy is separately owned.
Phase4 overlays and institutional/professional acceptance remain pending.

Common adapters cover 11 preserved original schemas, 23 new domain objects and two
explicit customer modes. Four original schemas remain native-only: AML alert batch,
mixed sanctions case batch, policy framework comparison and exam request/response.
All common handoffs require actual native producer/receiver validation. Foundation
handoffs preserve exact source facts and cutoff, context, access and open issues;
cross-context/audience conversions and foundation-to-legacy projections need
separately reviewed adapters. Native case-to-SAR conversion is not implemented. Two [review projections](../../skills/compliance-financial-crime/compliance-financial-crime-router/references/review-projection.md) separately support same-subject scenario design to effectiveness and single-case investigation to QA, retaining underlying IDs/versions and exact source/access scope.

External financial promotions route to financial-promotion-reviewer. Capital
Markets retains broker-dealer supervision, records/retention, internal communications
and conditional US specialist support. Supplied criteria profiles are bounded
methods, not approved comprehensive current-law packs. Sector/jurisdiction/activity
applicability and practitioner/institutional acceptance remain separate work.
