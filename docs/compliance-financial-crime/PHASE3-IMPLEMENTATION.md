# Phase3 implementation — 2026-10-04

The user authorized “implement the next phase now” after Phase2. Phase3 adds four
bounded native jobs: sanctions-screening-control-evaluator (S3),
transaction-monitoring-scenario-designer (M1), transaction-monitoring-effectiveness-reviewer
(M2), and financial-crime-case-quality-reviewer (T2). CFC now contains 38 packages,
including its router; all 36 target workflow rows have bounded native methods. This
count excludes the separately owned privacy job and includes the retained transitional
scenario evaluator. Advanced Phase4 work and professional/institutional acceptance
remain open. The external source workbook has not been reconciled.

Screening review reconciles population, feed, list/version and field coverage and
reviews supplied scoped matching tests, benchmarks, resilience and timing evidence.
Engine outcomes and independently supplied reference labels remain distinct. A list
load/update lag failure is a control finding; whether eligible detection observations
are measurable depends separately on the evaluated window and available evidence.
Nothing runs a screening engine, certifies coverage or clears a transaction.

Scenario design binds the risk hypothesis to population grain, data lineage,
identifier namespaces, graph/mapping references, ambiguity/conflict evidence,
parameters and aggregation windows. It requires scoped engine evidence and controlled
change/evaluation prerequisites before emitting a configuration proposal. Proposed
configuration remains a draft; a benchmark or vendor statement cannot replace engine
observations. Missing or unusable prerequisite evidence withholds the proposal.

Effectiveness review separates engine observations from labels, reconciles the
observed/sample/population units, and retains explicit limits on prevalence,
unlabeled cases, selection bias, incomparability, identity mapping, latency and
benchmarks. Exact integer counts and source-bound decimal criteria support bounded
sample comparisons; they do not prove population performance. Operational time and
cost have separate dependencies and scoped denominators. Fewer alerts, faster closure
and lower cost alone never establish effectiveness.

Case QA links selected/population case IDs, evidence exhibits, chronology, hypotheses,
contradictions/counterevidence, disposition reasoning and tool claims. Five mechanical
criterion methods supplement supplied human judgments. Known failed completeness
checks remain failed; absent evidence cannot support dependent reasoning checks.
Operational QA and second-line mandates remain distinct from independent testing,
audit and model validation. No case closure, reporting determination or statutory
assurance is produced.

All four packages use the unchanged compliance-foundation/2.0 source/access/date
contract, complete/partial/malformed and forged detached fixtures, native input/output
validators, executable evaluations and portable pinned resources. Source facts are
supplied and unverified; hashes and exact fact matches do not authenticate them.

The router adds four intents, bringing the declared total to 41. Existing generic
architecture, data, AI/model, vendor, privacy, economics and automation owners are
composed through [modernization boundaries](MODERNIZATION-COMPOSITION.md), preserving
their actual native contracts. Four new foundation object adapters and two explicit
same-subject review projections use actual installed native validators. Monitoring
projection retains underlying scenario/version/engine/population/identity links;
investigation-to-QA supports one case linked to the original input hash. Other
cross-object, cross-audience, temporal and adjacent-schema conversions are unsupported.
Original common handoffs retain their strict context and source rules.

Independent fresh evidence review is recorded in [validation](phase3-validation/README.md),
including initial failed cases, separately amended adapters and repaired replay. It
identified embedded-subject projection failures, list timing over-withholding,
missing engine prerequisite gates, coupled time/cost dependencies and known QA
completeness failure classification. These are bounded supplied-evidence checks;
practitioner/model narrative evaluation and institutional acceptance remain separate.
No commit, push, merge, release or live operational action is part of this phase.
