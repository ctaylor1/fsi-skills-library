# Monitoring evidence and action controls

Validate scope and structure before computation. Require a durable request/native object ID, institution/legal entity/capacity/represented party, activity, jurisdiction, purpose, role, audience/classification, review date and knowledge cutoff. The Foundation 2.0 validator rejects duplicate source/fact IDs, non-finite JSON, duplicate JSON keys, mismatched native context and unauthorized declared source audiences. These labels do not authenticate access or legal applicability.

Every consequential field is an exact `value/source_refs/fact_ids` claim. Source registry enrollment alone is not evidence. Missing, stale/expired, future or conflicting support retains unknowns and withholds dependent outputs. Material blockers and unknown metrics are separate lists. Optional unmeasured costs do not invalidate independent supported observation metrics. Do not fill absent labels, thresholds, vendor properties, feeds, identities, approvals or capacity measures.

Separate engine observations from benchmark/audit/reference labels and professional interpretations. Preserve population unit, currency/accounting, event identity, dataset/version/identifier namespace, graph/reference scope, real-time/latency and version boundaries. Repeated source records are not additive economic events. An extract completeness assertion, zero conflicts or a successful reconciliation remains supplied evidence needing actual owner review.

Retain real-time event/ingestion timestamp, watermark, late-arrival, replay/deduplication/idempotency, SLA/outage and recovery controls when applicable. Retain vendor version/change notice, documentation/export rights, opaque-logic limits, access/audit constraints and exit/rollback ownership. Statistical model applicability/validation and drift are model-owner concerns; copilot citation/retrieval, input isolation, prompt-injection handling, human review and confidentiality have explicit owners. This method records supplied requirements rather than performing those adjacent assessments.

Store reviewed narrative separately from native JSON. Require detached original and full recomputation. Hashes prove consistency, not truth, permission or professional acceptance. No source authentication, legal default, connector, data matching, production configuration, alert closure, customer communication, case/SAR conversion, filing, notification or live action is implemented. No approval token or supplied boolean changes that boundary.
