# Compliance and Financial Crime architecture

Flat deployable packages expose practitioner jobs. The domain index/router owns routing;
capability families are metadata rather than duplicated sector/jurisdiction folders.
Combine workflow × sector/product × jurisdiction × institutional responsibility.

The capability catalog retains the approved 36-workflow future state. Phase1 delivers
bounded corrections to the original 15 packages, a router, shared contracts and explicit
migrations. The monitoring scenario evaluator is transitional, not the future full
financial-crime-case-investigator. The privacy job moves to data/AI governance without
transferring controller/DPO accountability to a technical model owner.

## Ownership and composition

Compliance owns applicability implementation, obligation/control evidence, financial-crime
case support and oversight. Legal owns contested interpretation; authorized professionals
own consequential decisions. Operational QA, second-line monitoring/testing, statutory
independent testing, model validation and third-line audit remain separate mandates.

Payments retains product fraud, disputes and recovery/reimbursement; Insurance retains
claims/SIU and its market-conduct methods; Capital Markets retains trade/order/ecomms
surveillance; Wealth/Asset Management retain advice, investment and fund methods.
Customer Service retains individual complaints/interactions. CFC supplies bounded thematic customer-conduct evidence review in Phase2. Existing architecture, workflow automation, TPRM, data/AI, privacy, cyber, HR,
procurement, accounting, tax and prudential reporting owners retain their methods.

One primary job owns each result. Consultations return evidence and open issues.
Native schemas are not implicitly compatible because a handoff name resolves. The
common wrapper extends workflow-evidence/1.0, requires detached producer validation and
reviewed exact field mappings, and never grants professional acceptance or action rights.
Phase1 adapters deliberately reject cross-context/time/audience transfers.

## Shared resources

Reuse canonical native_integrity.py from investment-data and workflow_contract.py from
workflow-review. CFC owns its domain adapter, evidence/source standards and initial
primary source catalog. Screening/replay methods remain bounded analytical components.
Generated copies have version, SHA256, license and file-manifest records, enabling
isolated packages without runtime sibling imports. Edit canonical resources and use
tools/sync_cfc_resources.py after review; --check detects drift without writing.

Source/legal status, confidentiality and institutional approval are independent facts.
Never turn a citation or hash into source authentication, a policy into universal law,
an anomaly into guilt, a draft into a filing or a tester role string into independence.
