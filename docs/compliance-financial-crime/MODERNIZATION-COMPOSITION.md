# Modernization composition and deployment boundaries

Phase3 implements offline screening/monitoring design and evaluation and case QA.
It uses existing technology and governance owners rather than duplicating their jobs.
A named route or supporting package is a consultation boundary, not a data adapter or
completed assessment. Review the actual receiver schema, supplied sources, mandate
and confidentiality before composing any workflow.

| Need | Native evidence / owner | Bounded result and remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Detection-data reconciliation | S3/M1/M2 population, count, window, source/feed and lineage evidence; data-quality-issue-investigator and data-lineage-documenter | Scoped defects and detection limits; no source-to-engine connector or general reconciliation run |
| Graph/entity resolution | M1/M2 identifier namespaces, dataset IDs, mapping/graph refs/versions, ambiguity/conflict evidence; solution-architecture-planner | Review supplied identity mappings and design requirements; no identity matching algorithm, graph extraction or identity authentication |
| Identity adapters | identity-data-adapter route to solution-architecture-planner with data-lineage and privacy owners | Mapping design, lawful access, provenance and review gates; adjacent native schemas need separately reviewed conversion. Identity/access entitlement review is a different job |
| Real-time detection | Native processing/timing/resilience requirements and observations; solution-architecture-planner | Supplied latency/control evidence, explicit unknowns, rollback and fallback requirements; no live streaming or failover exercise |
| Vendor evidence | Scoped native engine observations and independent benchmark references; third-party-risk-assessor | Separate vendor claims, configuration/version and independently observed evidence; no vendor certification or provider approval |
| Program economics | Scoped M2 operational measures and Phase2 effectiveness synthesis; data-product-value-evaluator | Time and monetary observations retain their own denominators, currency/basis and comparability; no causal ROI from alert or closure reductions |
| Controlled investigator copilots | T2 tool-output factual links; solution-architecture-planner, workflow-automation-assessor, ai-risk-assessment-builder and agent-audit-trail-reviewer | Review supplied tool claims, traceability, review/authority gates and architecture suitability; no agent execution, engine changes or case disposition |
| Model/change governance | model-validation-assistant, model-change-impact-analyzer and AI risk owner | Mandate and applicable method classification first; CFC package checks do not establish independent validation |
| Privacy/disclosure | privacy-impact-assessment-assistant with privacy/controller/DPO and FIU owners | Evidence minimization, lawful access, audiences and SAR restrictions remain owner-reviewed; no automatic disclosure permission |

Adjacent workflow-review schemas differ from compliance-foundation/2.0. Foundation
source registries, cutoff/access and unresolved issues cannot silently disappear into
an adjacent request. Retain detached originals and conduct a separately scoped field,
source/date, identity, mandate and audience review. The current router has no automatic
adjacent-schema converter; aliases do not convert data.

The two executable review projections are limited to design-to-effectiveness and
single-case investigation-to-QA. They preserve subject, institution, source registry,
assessment/cutoff and audience. A sourced assertion records the changed review
capacity/purpose/role/object type; it does not authenticate appointment, independence,
source truth or semantic sufficiency. Unsupported pairs and disclosures stop.

The primary-source review on October4 verified SR26-2 and its revised model definition,
including scope exclusions for simple deterministic calculations, generative AI and
agentic AI. These scope distinctions do not remove separate AI, compliance, data,
third-party or operational governance responsibilities. See the
[source review](phase3-validation/SOURCE-REVIEW.md). No comprehensive current-law pack,
enterprise access, scheduler, live connector, approved configuration, rollback service
or production acceptance is bundled. Those require institutional evidence and owners.
