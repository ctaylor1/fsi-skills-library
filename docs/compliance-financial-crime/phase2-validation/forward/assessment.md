**Multi-account suspected mule case.** Currency totals were correct and separate: USD external inflow 6,000.30, outflow 3,700.05, internal movement 3,500.00 and unique turnover 13,200.35; EUR figures were 2,100.15, 1,900.05, 1,000.10 and 5,000.30. **Promotion exact-content review.** The unsupported guarantee differed from the supplied research statement, negative risk balance was exposed, and a disclosure asserted present conflicted with the actual content. The native result provided edit requests and owner questions without granting approval or disseminating content. **Control operation and independence.** The incomplete independent-test case reported one supported pass, one supported fail and one unknown workpaper, a 50% observed exception rate over the two completed supported observations, and I3/I4 as lacking completed evidence. **Remediation stages and closure.** Design records were separate from operating evidence. ## Coverage and acceptance limits

This forward exercise interprets realistic professional requests through the installed instructions and schemas and executes actual CLIs. It is not an independent source-authentication exercise or a repeated stochastic agent evaluation. Every record is explicitly synthetic and supplied-unverified. Source fidelity to real bank records, institution policy acceptance, Legal approval, independent-testing acceptance, privacy/retention governance, professional usefulness in production and deployment readiness remain unestablished.

The native results preserved claim locators and detached input identity, required human review, asserted no action, and avoided filing, contact, approval, closure, customer restriction or system changes. No package/shared/catalog files were edited, no methods were repaired, and no live operational or external communications actions occurred during this assessment.
