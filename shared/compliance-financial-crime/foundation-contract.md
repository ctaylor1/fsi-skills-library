# Compliance foundation contract — Phase 2

The native contract compliance-foundation/2.0 extends the Phase 1 evidence controls.
Institution, legal entity, activity, jurisdiction, responsibility, purpose, native subject,
assessment/knowledge dates and audience are explicit. Context labels are supplied facts;
they do not authenticate appointments, permissions, legal nexus or independence.

A detached source registry binds unique source refs and versions, publication/receipt
dates, expiry if supplied, confidentiality, recipient access, exact fact values and
supplied excerpts. Consequential assertions reference both source and fact IDs. Every
cited fact must match the asserted value. Conflicting, missing, future or expired support
remains an issue. A matching excerpt/value proves consistency of supplied assertions,
not their truth, independent corroboration or substantive legal support.

No universal stale-age rule, legal materiality, risk appetite, rating weights or residual
risk formula is provided. Each domain declares its supported method and accepts explicit
institutional rules and qualified interpretations; unsupported profiles remain visible.
Binding obligations, guidance, standards, institution policy and illustrative methods
have separate authority. Actual occurrence, source receipt, publication, effective,
compliance, transition and planned dates must not be interchanged.

Native output retains context, classification, role, audience and inherited issues. Draft
completeness does not establish professional acceptance. No source authenticity or action
authority follows from a hash, exact mapping or supplied approval. Operational proposals,
professional decisions and executions remain separate. Common handoffs run installed
producer/receiver validators and retain the detached originals; schema or audience
conversions require a reviewed adapter.

Calculators validate intake themselves. Native output validation requires the detached
original and recomputes the entire result; added narrative, fabricated sources, altered
values and self-rehashed envelopes are rejected. CLI intake also rejects non-finite JSON
and duplicate keys. Generated helper copies are version/SHA256/license pinned and have
no runtime imports from sibling packages.

Native results retain `claim_bindings`: input JSON pointers, source and fact IDs,
exact supplied-value consistency or unresolved state, and binding issues. This
locator map omits raw claim values. It does not assert that every input claim was
used by the domain method or establishes a professional conclusion. The native
method and row IDs explain dependent results; detached original evidence remains
necessary for output reconstruction.
